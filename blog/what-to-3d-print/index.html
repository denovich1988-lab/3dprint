3D print ideas – useful, fun and cool things to 3D print

Updated November 22, 2026

Key points
  • The best 3D print ideas solve a small problem you already have, such as a missing clip, a cable mess or a broken knob.
  • PLA suits most decorative and indoor items; PETG suits outdoor and heat-exposed parts; TPU suits anything that must flex.
  • Free model libraries such as Printables, MakerWorld and Thingiverse host millions of ready-to-print designs.

The best 3D print ideas are small, practical objects that solve a problem you already have, plus a few fun prints to show what your printer can do. This list groups things to 3D print by purpose, with the filament that suits each and a note on difficulty, so you can pick a project that matches your printer and your skill.

Every idea here is a common category on free model libraries. Search the name on Printables, MakerWorld or Thingiverse to find dozens of versions.

Useful things to 3D print at home

These prints pay for your filament quickly:

  • Replacement knobs and handles for appliances and furniture. Measure the shaft with calipers first.
  • Wall hooks and key holders that fit exactly where you need them.
  • Furniture feet and levellers for wobbly tables.
  • Shelf brackets and clips for glass or wooden shelves.
  • Door stops and wedges.

Tip: For anything near a window, in a car or outdoors, print in PETG or ASA. PLA softens in strong sun and heat. Our PETG vs PLA guide explains the difference.

Organisers and storage

  • Drawer dividers sized to your drawers.
  • Modular storage bins (the gridfinity system is a popular standard with thousands of compatible bins).
  • Tool holders for pegboards and walls.
  • Battery and SD-card organisers.
  • Filament spool holders and swatches for your own printer.

Desk and tech accessories

  • Phone and tablet stands
  • Cable clips and under-desk cable trays
  • Headphone hooks
  • Laptop risers
  • Controller and remote holders
  • Monitor-mounted shelves

Fun things to 3D print

  • Articulated animals – dragons, snakes and fish that print in one piece with moving joints.
  • Fidget toys – spinners, clickers and infinity cubes; read 3D printed fidget toys.
  • Puzzles – interlocking cubes, mazes and puzzle boxes.
  • Print-in-place mechanisms – gears, hinges and chains that come off the bed already working.
  • Miniatures and terrain for tabletop games (resin gives the most detail; FDM works for terrain).

3D printed gifts

  • Lithophanes – a photo that appears when lit from behind. Make one from any picture with our free lithophane maker.
  • Name signs and logos – turn an SVG into a 3D model with SVG to STL.
  • Vases in spiral (vase) mode – one wall, fast to print, watertight with the right settings.
  • Personalised keychains and bookmarks.
  • Planters with drainage trays.

Kitchen ideas (and a warning)

Bag clips, spice racks, utensil holders and cookie cutters are popular. Items that touch food need care: layer lines trap bacteria. Read is PLA food safe? before printing anything for food contact.

Things to 3D print for your printer

  • Spool holders and filament guides
  • Tool trays that clip to the frame
  • Nozzle and scraper holders
  • Enclosure parts and cable chains
  • Calibration models (temperature towers, retraction tests)

Turn your own ideas into prints

You do not need CAD skills for simple custom prints:

  1. From a picture: convert a logo or drawing with image to STL.
  2. From an AI 3D model: read how to 3D print AI-generated models.
  3. Check before slicing: open any file in the free 3D viewer to see its size and shape.

What to 3D print first

  1. Print the test model that came with your printer to confirm it works.
  2. Print one useful thing for your home – a hook or a clip.
  3. Print one fun thing – an articulated animal or a fidget toy.
  4. Try something custom from your own image or SVG.

Verdict

Start with small, useful prints that fix everyday annoyances, then mix in fun and gift projects. PLA covers most of them; switch to PETG for outdoor or warm places and TPU for anything flexible.

Questions and answers

What are good 3D print ideas for beginners?

Start with small, flat or single-part models that print without supports, such as cable clips, bag clips, keychains, plant labels, phone stands and the calibration cube your printer came with.

What are the most useful things to 3D print?

Replacement parts (knobs, clips, feet), organisers (drawer dividers, tool holders), cable management, wall hooks and custom adapters are the most useful because they fix real problems for pennies of filament.

Where can I find things to 3D print for free?

Printables, MakerWorld and Thingiverse are the largest free libraries. Check each model's licence before selling prints.

What can you make with a 3D printer?

Almost any small plastic object that fits the build volume, including tools, toys, gifts, cosplay props, miniatures, planters, lamps, replacement parts and prototypes.
