<h2>How much bigger is the A1?</h2>
<p>A 256 mm cube holds about 2.9 times the volume of a 180 mm cube. In practice, the A1 prints helmets, large planters and long brackets in one piece, while the A1 mini is best for parts up to about the size of a coffee mug.</p>
