FBX viewer

View FBX files online without installing anything. Rotate and zoom, see dimensions, triangle count and volume, and convert to another format if you need to.

Files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Drop your FBX file on the viewer or click “Upload your model”.
  2. Rotate with the mouse or one finger, zoom with the wheel or a pinch, and switch to wireframe to inspect the mesh.
  3. Read the size, triangle count and volume, save a PNG, or convert the model to another format.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no file limit set by us. Very large files are limited only by your device’s memory.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your web browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your file is read from your device and the result is created on your device, so nothing is sent to our server.

Which formats can the viewer open?

STL, 3MF, OBJ, GLB, glTF, FBX, PLY, DAE, 3DS, USDZ, OFF, AMF, VRML (WRL), STEP, STP, IGES, IGS and G-code, plus ZIP archives that contain a model.

How accurate are the measurements?

Dimensions come straight from the file’s coordinates and are shown in the file’s own units (millimetres for most 3D-printing files). Volume is exact for closed (watertight) meshes; for open meshes it is only an estimate.

Can I open huge files?

Files with a few million triangles open on most computers. Very large scans may be slow on phones because everything runs on your device.
