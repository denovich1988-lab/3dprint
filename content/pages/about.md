my3dprinter.io is a set of free tools and guides for people who make things with 3D printers.

**Tools.** The viewers and converters open STL, 3MF, STEP, OBJ, GLB and many other formats. They run entirely in your web browser, so your files are never uploaded to us. That keeps your designs private and makes large files quick to open.

**Guides.** Our printer and how-to guides explain setup, slicer settings, materials and common print problems. We base facts on manufacturers’ documentation and other reliable sources and list them at the end of each guide. If something is unclear or not confirmed, we say so.

**Independence.** my3dprinter.io is not affiliated with Bambu Lab, Prusa Research, Creality, Elegoo, Anycubic or any other brand we write about. Product names are trademarks of their owners.
