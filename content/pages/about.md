**my3dprinter.io is a free toolbox and guide site for people who make things with 3D printers.** You can open, measure and convert 3D files in your browser, turn images and SVGs into printable models, and read plain-English guides to printers, slicers and filament.

## What you can do here

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| [3D viewer](/3d-viewer/) | Open STL, 3MF, STEP, OBJ, GLB, FBX, PLY, USDZ and G-code files, see their size and volume |
| [Converters](/converters/) | Convert between mesh and CAD formats for slicers, Blender, game engines and AR |
| [Image to STL](/image-to-stl/) and [lithophane maker](/lithophane-maker/) | Turn photos and logos into reliefs, stamps and lithophanes |
| [3D printers](/printers/) | Model guides: who a printer suits, setup, connection and software |
| [Blog](/blog/) | Comparisons, filament explainers, ideas and fixes for common print problems |
| [File formats](/formats/) | What each 3D file format stores and which programs open it |

## How the tools work

Every viewer and converter runs **entirely in your web browser**. Your files are read and converted on your own device and are never uploaded to our server. That keeps designs private, works with large files and needs no account. The tools are free and have no watermark or file limit beyond what your device can handle.

## How we write guides

- Specifications, dates and feature claims come from the manufacturer's own pages, wiki or manual, or from two reputable sources that agree.
- If something is not confirmed, we say so or leave it out.
- We do not publish invented test results, reviews or user numbers, and we do not claim to have tested printers we have not used.
- Launch prices, when mentioned, are labelled as launch prices with their region.
- We update guides when firmware, software or product lines change and show the date of the last update.
