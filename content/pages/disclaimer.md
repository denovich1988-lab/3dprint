## Accuracy of guides

We check specifications and feature claims against manufacturers' published information or two reputable sources that agree, and we note the date of each update. Products, firmware and software change, so information can go out of date. Confirm anything important, such as compatibility, safety or warranty terms, with the manufacturer.

## No testing claims

Unless a page says otherwise, we have not tested the printers or materials we describe. Our guides summarise official documentation and reliable public information.

## Prices

We do not show current prices. Where a launch price is mentioned, it is labelled as a launch price with its region and may no longer apply.

## Converted files

File conversion can change or drop data that the target format cannot store, such as colours, units, textures or CAD history. Check every converted model before printing or manufacturing.

## Safety

3D printers have hot parts, moving mechanics and, with some materials, fumes. Always follow the manufacturer's safety instructions. Information on this site does not replace them.
