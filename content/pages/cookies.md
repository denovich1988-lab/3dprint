**my3dprinter.io uses analytics cookies set by Google tags, advertising cookies set by Google AdSense, and may use strictly necessary security cookies from our network provider.**

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

## Cookies on this site

| Type | Used? | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Strictly necessary (security) | Possibly | Set by our network and security provider, Cloudflare, to detect bots and protect the site from abuse. They do not identify you personally |
| Preferences | No | — |
| Analytics | Yes | Set by Google tags loaded through Google Tag Manager to count visits and see how pages are used. Typical names start with `_ga` |
| Advertising | Yes | Set by Google AdSense and its partners to show ads, limit repeats and measure performance. Personalised ads in the EEA, UK and Switzerland are used only with consent |

Strictly necessary cookies are needed for the site to work securely. Analytics and advertising cookies are optional: you can block or delete them in your browser at any time without losing access to any tool.

## Local processing, not storage

Our 3D tools keep the files you open in your browser's memory while the page is open. They are not saved to cookies or sent to us, and they disappear when you close the tab.

## How to control cookies

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Blocking security cookies may cause some visits to be checked with an extra verification step.

## Changes

If we add or change cookies, we will update this page and the date above.
