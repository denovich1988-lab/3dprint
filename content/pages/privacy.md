**Files you open with my3dprinter.io stay on your device: our viewers and converters run in your browser and never upload your files.** This policy explains what limited data is processed when you visit the site.

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

## Summary

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Are my 3D files or images uploaded? | No. They are read and converted in your browser only |
| Do I need an account? | No. The site has no accounts or sign-in |
| Do you use analytics? | Yes. We use Google Tag Manager to load Google's measurement tags, which count visits and how pages are used |
| Do you use advertising cookies? | Yes. Ads are served by Google AdSense, which may use cookies to show and measure ads |
| Who else processes data? | Google (Tag Manager, analytics and AdSense advertising), our hosting server, and our network and security provider, Cloudflare |

## Files and content you open in the tools

When you drop a file into a viewer or converter, your browser reads it into memory, displays it and creates any converted file locally. The converted file is downloaded from your browser's memory, not from our server. We never receive, see or store the files, images or SVGs you use. Closing the tab clears them.

## Data processed when you visit

Like every website, our server and network provider receive technical data needed to send you pages and keep the site secure:

- IP address
- browser and operating system type
- the page requested, time of request and referring page
- basic security signals used to block abusive traffic

This data is used only to deliver the site, prevent abuse and fix errors. Server logs are kept for a limited period and then deleted. We do not sell this data or use it to build profiles.

## Analytics (Google Tag Manager)

We use Google Tag Manager to load Google's analytics tags. They help us understand which tools and guides are used, which pages load slowly and where visitors come from, so we can improve the site. These tags may set cookies or similar identifiers in your browser and send Google information such as the pages you view, your approximate location derived from your IP address, your device and browser type, and referring pages. Google processes this data under its own privacy policy. The files you open in our tools are never part of this data: they stay in your browser.

You can block analytics with your browser's privacy settings or a content blocker, or opt out of Google Analytics with Google's browser add-on.

## Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads served by Google AdSense to keep the tools free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar identifiers to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, to limit how often you see an ad and to measure ad performance. Visitors in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalised ads are used. You can turn off personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

## Cookies and local storage

my3dprinter.io uses analytics cookies set through Google Tag Manager, advertising cookies set by Google AdSense, and our network provider may set strictly necessary security cookies to tell people from automated attacks. See the [cookie policy](/cookies/) for details.

## Emails you send us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply and to fix the issue you raise. We keep correspondence only as long as needed for that purpose.

## Children

The site is a general-audience tool site. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and to object to processing. Because we do not keep accounts or files, the personal data involved is limited to short-lived server logs, analytics data processed by Google and any emails you send. To make a request, write to hello@my3dprinter.io.

## Changes

If we change how data is handled, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect, and ask for consent where the law requires it.
