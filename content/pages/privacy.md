title: Privacy policy
description: How my3dprinter.io handles your data. Files you open in our tools are processed in your browser and never uploaded.
date: 2026-11-22
---
**Your files stay on your device.** The viewers and converters on my3dprinter.io read your files with your browser and create the results in your browser. We do not receive, store or see the files you open.

**What our server receives.** Like any website, our web server receives standard request data (IP address, browser type, the page requested) to deliver pages and protect the site. Server logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting.

**Analytics and cookies.** If we add privacy-friendly analytics or advertising in the future, this page will be updated first and any consent you need to give will be requested on the site.

**Contact.** For privacy questions write to hello@my3dprinter.io. The site is operated by OUTRANKING LTD.
