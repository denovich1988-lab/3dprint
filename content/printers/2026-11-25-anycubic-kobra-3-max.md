**The Anycubic Kobra 3 Max is a large open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a 420 × 420 × 500 mm build volume, 600 mm/s top speed and LeviQ 3.0 automatic levelling, sold mainly as the Kobra 3 Max Combo with Anycubic's ACE Pro multi-colour unit.** Anycubic showed it at Formnext in November 2024, opened pre-orders in December 2024 and launched it on 21 February 2025 at US$999 / £999 for the Combo (launch price, US and UK). This page covers specs, setup, connections and the newer V2.

## Anycubic Kobra 3 Max at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 420 × 420 × 500 mm |
| Speed / acceleration | 600 mm/s (300 recommended), 10,000 mm/s² |
| Hotend / bed | 300 °C / 90 °C |
| Bed | Heated PEI spring-steel plate |
| Levelling | LeviQ 3.0 automatic |
| Multi-colour | ACE Pro: 4 colours, 8 with two units |
| Firmware | Kobra OS |
| Launch price | US$999 / £999 for the Combo (Feb 2025) |

## Who the Kobra 3 Max suits

The Kobra 3 Max suits makers who print large single parts – helmets, costume armour, furniture parts, big planters – or several smaller parts in one go, and want multi-colour without a second printer. As an open bed-slinger with a 90 °C bed, it is best with PLA, PETG and TPU. Anycubic lists ABS and ASA as workable but PA and PC as not recommended.

## The ACE Pro on the Kobra 3 Max Combo

The ACE Pro holds four spools, feeds them to the print head and dries filament – also while printing. Two ACE Pro units with an eight-colour hub give eight colours. Expect purge waste on colour changes; Anycubic's slicer lets you adjust purge volumes.

**Tip:** dry PETG and TPU before long prints – see our [filament dryer guide](/blog/filament-dryer/).

## Setting up the Kobra 3 Max

Based on Anycubic's quick start guide, at a high level:

1. Unbox and remove the transport screws from the gantry.
2. Set the gantry on the base, fix it with three screws per side and add the left and right support rods.
3. Plug in the labelled cables, mount the print head and fit the touchscreen.
4. For the Combo: fit the filament cutter and purge wiper, then connect the ACE Pro and its four PTFE tubes.
5. Power on, run the initial calibration with auto levelling and update the firmware.
6. Load filament into the ACE Pro, set type and colour on the screen, then slice and print.

## Connecting the Kobra 3 Max

The printer joins **Wi-Fi** and works with the **Anycubic app** and Anycubic Cloud, with a **LAN mode** for local printing. You can also print from a **USB drive**. A camera for AI detection and time-lapse is optional.

## Manual, firmware and slicer

Anycubic publishes the Kobra 3 Max quick start guide and the multi-language Kobra 3 Max Combo user manual on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D printers. A paper quick start guide and a USB stick with the manual come in the box. The printer runs Kobra OS; check the wiki for the current firmware.

Anycubic recommends **Anycubic Slicer Next**, based on OrcaSlicer; other slicers also work. Open projects from other slicers with our [3MF to STL converter](/3mf-to-stl/), or preview a sliced job with the [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## Kobra 3 Max V2: what changed

The Kobra 3 Max V2 keeps the 420 × 420 × 500 mm volume, 600 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s², and adds an all-metal hotend, a dual-gear extruder, a hardened steel nozzle and the ACE 2 Pro. Anycubic notes that the V2 hotend and print head do not fit the original Kobra 3 Max.
