The **Bambu Lab A1** is Bambu Lab’s full-size open printer. It moves the bed back and forth (a “bed-slinger”) rather than using a CoreXY frame, but adds the automation Bambu printers are known for: automatic bed levelling and Z-offset, vibration compensation, flow-rate compensation and motor-noise cancelling. Bambu Lab launched it in December 2023 at US$399, or US$559 with the AMS lite.

## Who the A1 suits

It suits beginners and hobbyists who print mostly PLA and PETG and want a large build area without an enclosure. With the AMS lite it prints up to four colours. It is quieter than many printers thanks to active motor-noise cancellation.

## Setup

The A1 ships partly assembled. Follow the quick-start guide in the box and on the Bambu Lab Wiki, run the on-screen calibration, then bind the printer in the **Bambu Handy** app. Send prints from **Bambu Studio**; Bambu Studio saves projects as 3MF.

## Manual

Bambu Lab publishes the A1 user manual, maintenance guides and troubleshooting on its official wiki. We do not copy the manual here; use the official version so instructions match your firmware.

## A1 or A1 mini?

Read our [A1 vs A1 mini comparison](/blog/bambu-lab-a1-vs-a1-mini/). In short, choose the A1 if any part you plan to print is larger than 180 mm.
