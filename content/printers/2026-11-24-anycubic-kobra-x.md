**The Anycubic Kobra X is an open-frame multi-colour 3D printer with a 260 × 260 × 260 mm build volume and a four-colour filament changer built into the print head.** Anycubic first showed it at Formnext 2025 and opened deposits on 26 December 2025; the US early-bird launch price was US$279 against a US$399 list price. It is aimed at beginners who want colour printing without a separate feeder box.

## Anycubic Kobra X at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 260 × 260 × 260 mm |
| Max speed / acceleration | 600 mm/s / 20,000 mm/s² |
| Nozzle / bed | 300 °C / 100 °C |
| Colours | 4 built in, up to 19 with ACE 2 Pro |
| Frame | Open, moving bed |
| Camera | 720p with AI detection |
| Connectivity | Dual-band Wi-Fi, USB |
| Weight | About 9.5 kg |

## How the Kobra X colour system works

Most multi-colour printers park a feeder box beside or on top of the printer and push long lengths of filament through a tube at every change. On the Kobra X, Anycubic puts the changer – which it calls ACE Gen 2 – in the print head itself, so four filaments sit right above the nozzle. Anycubic presents this as a way to reduce waste at each colour change.

**Expanding colours:** Anycubic sells the Kobra X alone (4 colours), as a Combo (7 colours), and with one or three extra ACE 2 Pro units (11 or 19 colours). The ACE 2 Pro used with the Kobra X has no active drying of its own.

## Who the Kobra X suits

The Kobra X suits first-time buyers and families who want multi-colour toys, signs and gifts in PLA and PETG. It also lists TPU, PVA support material, carbon-fibre PLA and PETG, and ASA. Because it is open-frame, ABS is not listed; for that, the enclosed [Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo](/printers/anycubic-kobra-s1-combo/) is the better choice.

## Setting up the Anycubic Kobra X

At a high level, setup follows these steps:

1. Remove the shipping ties and fixtures.
2. Mount the gantry on the base and snap on the covers.
3. Plug in the labelled connectors.
4. Fit the four-spool holder on top of the frame.
5. Power on, join Wi-Fi and let LeviQ 3.0 level the bed and set the Z offset.

Anycubic publishes the full quick start guide and the user manual on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D printer → Anycubic Kobra X.

## Connecting the Kobra X

Send files over **Wi-Fi** from Anycubic Slicer Next or the **Anycubic app**, or print from a **USB drive**. The 720p camera supports remote monitoring and AI spaghetti detection.

## Firmware and software

The Kobra X runs Anycubic's Kobra OS. Anycubic's firmware update guide for the printer is on the Anycubic Wiki in the Kobra X section. Slice in **Anycubic Slicer Next**, which is based on OrcaSlicer. Check colour projects in our [3MF viewer](/3mf-viewer/) before slicing.

## Kobra X in the Anycubic range

The Kobra X shares its 260 mm build volume with the Kobra 4, which uses an external ACE 2 Pro feeder instead of a changer in the head.
