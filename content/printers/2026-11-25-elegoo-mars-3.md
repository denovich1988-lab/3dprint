**The Elegoo Mars 3 is an MSLA resin 3D printer with a 6.6-inch 4K mono LCD (4098 × 2560), 35 µm XY resolution and a 143 × 89.6 × 175 mm build volume.** Elegoo released it in 2021 as the first 4K model in the Mars line, aimed at miniatures, jewellery models and small detailed parts. This page covers the specs, setup and levelling, slicing, where the official manual and firmware are, and how the Mars 3 Pro differs.

## Elegoo Mars 3 at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Technology | MSLA resin, 4K mono LCD |
| Screen size and resolution | 6.6 inch, 4098 × 2560 |
| XY resolution | 35 µm |
| Build volume | 143 × 89.6 × 175 mm |
| Light | COB UV LED, 405 nm |
| Control | 3.5-inch touchscreen |
| Files | USB drive, sliced in CHITUBOX |
| Size and weight | 227 × 227 × 438.5 mm, 5.2 kg |

## Who the Elegoo Mars 3 suits

The Mars 3 suits hobbyists who print tabletop miniatures, figurines and small detailed parts and have a ventilated space for resin. The mono screen cures layers much faster than the 2K colour screen of the [original Elegoo Mars](/printers/elegoo-mars/). For larger models, look at the Saturn range instead.

## Setting up the Elegoo Mars 3

1. Unpack the printer, fit the build plate and connect the power adapter.
2. Insert the USB drive that comes with it; it holds the test file and software.
3. Level the build plate (see below).
4. Wearing gloves and a mask, fill the resin vat to around a third.
5. Print the test model, then wash and UV-cure the part.

**Tip:** resin prints best in a room that is not cold. Keep the UV cover on whenever the vat holds resin.

## Levelling the Elegoo Mars 3

Levelling is manual. Loosen the bolts on the build plate, lay a sheet of paper on the LCD and lower the plate onto it from the touchscreen. Press the plate flat, tighten the bolts and set the position as Z = 0. Repeat only if prints stop sticking or the plate has been removed and refitted loosely.

## Slicing for the Elegoo Mars 3

Elegoo pairs the Mars 3 with **CHITUBOX**; at launch the printer came with a CHITUBOX Pro licence. Hollow and support your model, slice and save to the USB drive, then start the print on the touchscreen. Before slicing, check the mesh in our [STL viewer](/stl-viewer/), or convert other formats with the [OBJ to STL converter](/obj-to-stl/).

## Elegoo Mars 3 manual and firmware

Elegoo publishes the Mars 3 user manual on its "Mars 3 3D printer support files" page, with one manual covering both the Mars 3 and Mars 3 Pro. The same page has the firmware versions, the USB drive files, resin settings and open-source files. Elegoo warns that flashing firmware meant for another printer can make the printer unusable, so check the model name before you update.

## Elegoo Mars 3 vs Mars 3 Pro

| Feature | Mars 3 | Mars 3 Pro |
|---|---|---|
| LCD | 6.6-inch 4K mono | Same |
| Build volume | 143 × 89.6 × 175 mm | Same |
| Light source | COB UV LED | COB with Fresnel lens, 36 LEDs |
| Screen protection | – | Replaceable 9H tempered glass |
| Odour | – | Plug-in mini air purifier |
| Build plate | Standard | Sandblasted |

The Pro's air purifier plugs in under the cover and reduces resin odour; it does not replace good ventilation.
