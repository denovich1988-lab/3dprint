**The Prusa CORE One is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer from Prusa Research with a 250 × 220 × 270 mm build volume, a 290 °C Nextruder and an actively controlled chamber.** Josef Prusa announced it on 19 November 2024 at Formnext. It is open source and repairable: the steel frame comes apart with a screwdriver and nothing is glued. This page covers the official specs, how the CORE One, CORE One+ and Gen 2 differ, setup and connection.

## Who the Prusa CORE One suits

The CORE One suits buyers who want an enclosed CoreXY printer they can repair and upgrade themselves. It handles PLA and PETG with the door closed, and Prusa lists ABS, ASA, HIPS and PA as advanced materials that need the optional Advanced Filtration System. MK4S owners can convert their printer with an official conversion kit.

## Prusa CORE One vs CORE One+ vs Gen 2

| Version | When | What changed |
|---|---|---|
| CORE One | Announced 19 Nov 2024 | Original model |
| CORE One+ | Shipping from 27 Nov 2025 | Automatic top vent, easier flexible-filament loading, bayonet spool holder that takes a drybox |
| CORE One+ (Gen 2) | Announced 13 Aug 2026 | GT1.5 belts, silicone nozzle wiper, new heatbed mounting, tool-free top panel |

The build volume and core mechanism did not change. Original owners can upgrade to the CORE One+ with free printed parts or Prusa's low-cost kit and firmware 6.4.0. Prusa also announced an upgrade path to Gen 2. The larger sibling is the CORE One L with 300 × 300 × 330 mm.

## Prusa CORE One launch prices

| Version | Launch price |
|---|---|

## First setup

Prusa's "Unpacking and first run" guide for the current version goes like this:

1. Lift the printer out, stick on the anti-vibration feet and remove the cardboard fixtures on the X axis.
2. Mount the screen and peel the protective films off the panels.
3. Clean the heatbed, lay the print sheet on it and fit the spool holder.
4. Power on, choose your language and confirm the printer model.
5. Set up Wi-Fi through the Prusa app, or skip it.
6. Run the self-test wizard with the door closed – it checks the load cell, the gearbox and the filament sensors.

**Tip:** the load cell measures the first layer automatically and levels only the area you print on, so there is no manual Z offset to set.

## Connecting the CORE One

The printer has Ethernet and Wi-Fi; Wi-Fi is set up by holding a phone to the NFC receiver. Remote control runs through Prusa Connect and the Prusa app, and you can also print from a USB drive or run the printer fully offline.
