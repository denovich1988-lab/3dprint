**The Elegoo 3D printer lineup covers both filament (FDM) printers – the Centauri, Neptune and OrangeStorm Giga families – and MSLA resin printers in the Mars, Saturn and Jupiter series.** Elegoo was founded in 2015 and is based in Shenzhen, China. This page sorts the current models by type and size so you can see which one fits what you print.

## Centauri series: enclosed CoreXY printers

The [Elegoo Centauri Carbon](/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/) is a fully enclosed CoreXY printer with a die-cast aluminium frame, 500 mm/s top speed, 20,000 mm/s² acceleration and a 320 °C hardened steel nozzle for carbon-fibre filaments. The [Centauri Carbon 2](/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon-2/) keeps the 256 mm cube volume, raises the nozzle to 350 °C and is sold as a combo with **CANVAS**, Elegoo's four-colour filament system. The **Centauri 2** is an open-frame version without the enclosure, camera or filter.

**Tip:** choose an enclosed Centauri if you plan to print ABS, ASA or nylon blends; open-frame printers are best kept to PLA, PETG and TPU.

## Neptune 4 series: Klipper bed-slingers

The Neptune 4 printers are open-frame bed-slingers with Klipper firmware pre-installed, dual-gear direct extruders and 121-point automatic levelling. The **Neptune 4 Pro** is the compact model, the **Neptune 4 Plus** steps up to 320 × 320 × 385 mm, and the [Elegoo Neptune 4 Max](/printers/elegoo-neptune-4-max/) is the largest at 420 × 420 × 480 mm. The older [Neptune 3 Pro](/printers/elegoo-neptune-3-pro/) is a simpler microSD-card printer without Klipper.

## OrangeStorm Giga: large-format FDM

The OrangeStorm Giga has an 800 × 800 × 1000 mm build volume, four separately heated 410 × 410 mm PEI bed sections and room for up to four printheads on the X axis. Elegoo rates it at 300 mm/s maximum and recommends 150 mm/s. It suits props, furniture parts and cosplay pieces that would otherwise be printed in sections.

## Mars and Saturn: resin printers

- The [Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra](/printers/elegoo-mars-5-ultra/) is a small 7-inch 9K resin printer with tilt release, an AI camera and Wi-Fi.
- The [Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra](/printers/elegoo-saturn-4-ultra/) uses a 10-inch 12K LCD with a larger 218.88 × 122.88 × 220 mm volume.
- The **Saturn 4 Ultra 16K** keeps a 10-inch screen at 16K resolution (14 × 19 µm pixels) and heats the resin tank to 30 °C.

## Jupiter 2: large resin printer

The Jupiter 2, launched at RAPID + TCT 2026, is Elegoo's large-format resin printer with a 302 × 162 × 300 mm build volume, a 16K LCD, force-sensor auto levelling, a heated vat and a two-way resin pump fed from a 2 kg bottle.

## Elegoo software

- **ELEGOO Slicer (ElegooSlicer)** – the FDM slicer for current printers, based on OrcaSlicer.
- **ELEGOO Cura** – supplied with older Neptune printers.
- **SatelLite** – Elegoo's free resin slicer for Windows and macOS; resin printers also work with CHITUBOX.
- **Elegoo Matrix app** – remote control of supported printers.

Firmware, user manuals and slicers are in the **ELEGOO Download Center**, and how-to guides are on the **ELEGOO Wiki**. Before slicing, you can check a model in our [STL viewer](/stl-viewer/) or a multi-colour project in the [3MF viewer](/3mf-viewer/).

## How to choose an Elegoo printer

1. **Filament or resin?** Filament for functional parts and large objects; resin for miniatures and fine detail.
2. **Materials:** for ABS, ASA or carbon-fibre filaments, choose an enclosed Centauri Carbon model.
3. **Size:** Neptune 4 Plus or Max for big prints on a budget; OrangeStorm Giga for very large parts.
4. **Colours:** Centauri Carbon 2 with CANVAS for four-colour printing.
