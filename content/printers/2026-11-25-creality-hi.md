**The Creality Hi is an open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a die-cast aluminium frame, a 260 × 260 × 300 mm build volume and support for up to 16 colours through Creality's CFS filament system.** Creality announced it at CES on 8 January 2025 at US$299 for the Hi and US$469 for the Hi Combo (US), and started worldwide sales on 11 March 2025. Creality pitches it at families and beginners who want multi-colour printing.

## Creality Hi at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 260 × 260 × 300 mm |
| Speed / acceleration | 500 mm/s (300 typical), 12,000 mm/s² |
| Hotend / bed | 300 °C / 100 °C |
| Frame | Open, die-cast aluminium |
| Multi-colour | 1 CFS in the Combo, up to 4 CFS for 16 colours |
| Camera | Built in, privacy cover, time-lapse |
| Noise | Silent mode at 48 dB or less |
| Announced price | US$299 (Hi), US$469 (Hi Combo) at CES 2025 |

## Creality Hi vs Hi Combo

The **Hi** is the printer on its own and prints one filament at a time. The **Hi Combo** bundles one four-spool [Creality CFS](/printers/creality-cfs/). The CFS holds 1 kg spools, keeps them dry with desiccant and reads RFID tags on Creality filament, so colours are mapped automatically. When a spool runs out, the printer can switch to a matching spool and keep going.

**Tip:** if you start with the plain Hi, you can add a CFS later – the multi-colour upgrade is the same unit.

## Who the Creality Hi suits

The Hi suits beginners and families who want an easy first printer with a path to multi-colour prints. It is open, so it is best for PLA, PETG and PLA-CF; for regular ABS or ASA an enclosed printer such as the [Creality K2 Plus](/printers/creality-k2-plus/) is a better fit.

## Setting up the Creality Hi

Creality says the Hi arrives about 95% pre-assembled and takes around 8 minutes to build:

1. Fit the gantry to the base with the supplied screws.
2. Mount the CFS hub on the gantry leg and connect the cables (Combo only); Creality's unboxing video shows this step.
3. Power on; automatic levelling and calibration run on first start.
4. Load filament into the printer or the CFS and print a test file.

## Connecting the Creality Hi

The Hi joins **Wi-Fi** and works with **Creality Cloud** for remote monitoring with the built-in camera. Files can also go on a **USB drive**, and the printer has 8 GB of internal storage. Check sliced files with our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).
