**The Elegoo Mars is a compact MSLA resin 3D printer with a 2K (2560 × 1440) LCD and a 120 × 68 × 155 mm build volume – the model that started Elegoo's Mars line of budget resin printers.** It was on sale by 2019 and became a popular first resin printer for miniatures and small detailed parts. This page covers the original Mars, how to set it up and level it, and how the later Mars models differ.

## Elegoo Mars at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Technology | MSLA resin, 2K RGB LCD |
| Build volume | 120 × 68 × 155 mm |
| XY resolution | 0.047 mm |
| Layer thickness | 0.01–0.2 mm |
| Print speed | 22.5 mm/h |
| Light | Integrated 405 nm UV LED |
| Control | 3.5-inch touchscreen |
| Files | USB drive, sliced in CHITUBOX |

## Who the Elegoo Mars suits

The Mars suits hobbyists who print miniatures, jewellery models or small detailed parts and have a ventilated space for resin. Its 2K colour LCD exposes layers more slowly than modern mono screens, so a newer Mars model is a better buy if you are starting today.

## Setting up the Elegoo Mars

Based on Elegoo's product information and published reviews, setup is short:

1. Unpack, fit the build plate and connect the power adapter.
2. Insert the USB drive that ships with the printer.
3. Level the platform with a sheet of paper (see below).
4. Wearing gloves and a mask, pour resin into the vat – about a third full is enough for a first print.
5. Print the test file from the USB drive.

**Tip:** resin is sensitive to temperature. Print in a room that is not cold, and wash and cure parts after printing.

## Levelling the build plate

Loosen the screw on the build plate, lay a sheet of paper on the LCD, then home the plate from the touchscreen. Press the plate flat onto the paper, tighten the screw and save the zero position. Elegoo describes a steel-ball balancing joint in the plate that helps it settle flat.

## Slicing for the Elegoo Mars

Elegoo pairs the Mars with **CHITUBOX**. Hollow and support your model, slice, save the file to the USB drive and start the print on the touchscreen. Check that your model is a clean mesh before slicing – our [STL viewer](/stl-viewer/) shows it in the browser, and the [OBJ to STL converter](/obj-to-stl/) handles other formats.
