**The Creality Ender 5 Plus is a large open cube-frame 3D printer with a 350 × 350 × 400 mm build volume, BL Touch automatic levelling and a 4.3-inch touchscreen.** In the cube design the print head moves in X and Y at the top and the bed moves only down, so tall parts are not shaken back and forth. It is an older Creality model that is still popular for big prints.

## Creality Ender 5 Plus at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 350 × 350 × 400 mm |
| Nozzle / bed | 260 °C / 110 °C |
| Frame | Open cube, dual Y, dual Z screws |
| Bed surface | Tempered glass |
| Levelling | BL Touch (16 points) |
| Screen | 4.3-inch touchscreen |
| Connectivity | SD card, USB |
| Power | Max 550 W |

## Who the Ender 5 Plus suits

It suits makers who need a big build volume for cosplay helmets, props or long parts and are happy to tune an open printer. It has no Wi-Fi, camera or enclosure. Reviewers noted the BL Touch sits about 7 cm left of the nozzle, so it cannot probe the full bed.

## Assembling the Ender 5 Plus

The user manual describes a semi-assembled kit:

1. Fix three aluminium profiles to the base with M5×25 screws.
2. Mount the top frame; tighten the top screws before the side screws.
3. Fit the dual Z-axis frame and check that the Y axis moves smoothly.
4. Fix the hot bed to the Z support plate with eight M4×10 screws.
5. Fit the filament holder, connect all cables and set the voltage switch for your mains supply.

**Tip:** check every connector against the wiring diagram in the manual before the first power-on.

## Levelling the Ender 5 Plus

Level the four corners by hand first, using the knobs and a sheet of A4 paper, with a nozzle gap of about 0.2 mm. Then home the printer and run the auto level, which probes 16 points with the BL Touch.

## Loading filament and printing

Preheat with the PLA or ABS preset, hold the extruder lever and push 1.75 mm filament through the run-out sensor into the extruder until it flows from the nozzle. Copy sliced G-code to the SD card and print from the touchscreen. To check a sliced file first, use our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## Ender 5 Plus manual

Creality lists the Ender-5 Plus user manual in the Download Center on its website under "Ender-5 Plus 3D Printer". It covers component identification, frame assembly, cable connection, screen menus, loading filament, levelling, software installation and circuit wiring.

## Firmware and software

The download page offers **Marlin 2.0** firmware for the **V4.2.2** mainboard; early printers shipped with an older board, so match the file to your board. The current slicer is **Creality Print**; older units came with Cura or Creality Slicer.
