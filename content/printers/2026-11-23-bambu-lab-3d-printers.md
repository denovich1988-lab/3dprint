**Bambu Lab 3D printers range from the compact, open A1 mini to the enclosed H2 series with heated chambers, dual nozzles and laser modules.** All of them use the same software – Bambu Studio for slicing and the Bambu Handy app for monitoring – and all current models can print in several colours with an AMS unit. This overview helps you pick a Bambu printer; each model links to its own page with full specs.

## A1 series: open printers for beginners

The [Bambu Lab A1 mini](/printers/bambu-lab-a1-mini/) and [A1](/printers/bambu-lab-a1/) are open-frame bed-slingers. They suit PLA, PETG and TPU, and pair with the four-spool AMS lite. Bambu Lab does not recommend them for ABS, ASA, PC or nylon, which need an enclosure.

**Tip:** choose the A1 over the A1 mini if you print helmets, storage boxes or other parts wider than 180 mm.

## P series: enclosed all-rounders

The [P1S](/printers/bambu-lab-p1s/) and its successor the [P2S](/printers/bambu-lab-p2s/) are enclosed CoreXY printers with a 256 mm cube build volume. The enclosure lets them print ABS and ASA. The P2S, announced in October 2025, adds a 5-inch touchscreen, a 1080p camera and a servo extruder.

## X series: from X1 Carbon to X2D

The [X1 Carbon](/printers/bambu-lab-x1-carbon/) was Bambu Lab's first printer (2022), with lidar and a hardened steel nozzle. It went out of production on 31 March 2026. The [X2D](/printers/bambu-lab-x2d/), announced in April 2026, takes its place in the range with two switchable nozzles, a 65 °C heated chamber and HEPA plus carbon filtration.

## H2 series: large format, lasers and many materials

The H2 printers share one large frame, a 350 °C hot end and a 65 °C heated chamber:

- [H2S](/printers/bambu-lab-h2s/) – one nozzle and the tallest build volume (340 mm).
- [H2D](/printers/bambu-lab-h2d/) – two nozzles; optional 10 W or 40 W laser.
- [H2C](/printers/bambu-lab-h2c/) – the Vortek system swaps between six hot ends for multi-material prints.

## Multi-colour printing with AMS

Bambu Lab's automatic material systems feed up to four spools each. The AMS lite fits the A1 series; the AMS and AMS 2 Pro fit the enclosed printers, and the AMS HT holds one spool for drying and high-temperature filaments. See our [Bambu Lab AMS](/printers/bambu-lab-ams/) page for compatibility.

## Software for every Bambu printer

- **Bambu Studio** – Bambu Lab's free slicer for Windows, macOS and Linux. It saves projects as 3MF files; our [3MF viewer](/3mf-viewer/) and [3MF to STL converter](/3mf-to-stl/) open them in a browser.
- **Bambu Handy** – the phone app for binding, starting and watching prints.
- **MakerWorld** – Bambu Lab's model library, linked from Bambu Studio and Bambu Handy.
- **LAN mode** – lets every model print without the cloud.

## How to choose a Bambu Lab printer

1. **Materials:** only PLA, PETG and TPU → A1 or A1 mini. ABS, ASA → P1S or P2S. Nylon, PC and fibre-filled at scale → X2D or the H2 series.
2. **Size:** 180 mm (A1 mini), 256 mm (A1, P series, X2D) or about 330–350 mm (H2 series).
3. **Supports and colours:** two nozzles (X2D, H2D) for soluble or break-away supports; the H2C for many materials with little waste.
4. **Extra tools:** a laser module is only available on the H2 series.
