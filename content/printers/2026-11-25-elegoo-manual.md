**Elegoo publishes the official Elegoo manual for each of its 3D printers in the ELEGOO Download Center on its website, where you pick the product series and model to download the user manual, firmware and test models.** The ELEGOO Wiki and Elegoo's user guide blog add how-to pages for updates, repairs and print problems. This page explains where each kind of document lives and what a typical Elegoo manual covers, in our own words.

## Where Elegoo publishes its manuals

| What you need | Where Elegoo puts it |
|---|---|
| User manual (PDF) | ELEGOO Download Center → category → series → model |
| Firmware and "how to update" notes | Same Download Center entry |
| Test models and compliance files | Same Download Center entry |
| How-to and repair guides | ELEGOO Wiki, organised by product |
| Troubleshooting articles | Elegoo's 3D printer user guide blog, tagged by model |
| Older model support files | Model support pages, for example the Mars 3 support files page |

## Elegoo printer series in the Download Center

- **Mars** (resin): from the original Mars to the Mars 5 Ultra. See our [Elegoo Mars](/printers/elegoo-mars/) and [Elegoo Mars 3](/printers/elegoo-mars-3/) pages.
- **Saturn** (larger resin): Saturn to Saturn 4 Ultra 16K. See the [Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra](/printers/elegoo-saturn-4-ultra/).
- **Neptune** (open FDM): Neptune 2 to Neptune 4 Max. See the [Elegoo Neptune 4](/printers/elegoo-neptune-4/).
- **Centauri** (enclosed CoreXY FDM): Centauri Carbon and Centauri Carbon 2 Combo. See the [Elegoo Centauri Carbon](/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/).

**Tip:** manuals change between hardware revisions. Some Neptune models have separate firmware for ribbon-cable and Type-C mainboards, so check the label on your printer before you download.

## What a typical Elegoo FDM manual covers

Taking the Neptune 4 Pro manual as an example, the sections are:

1. Machine parameters and components.
2. Packing list.
3. Setup and installation.
4. Screen operation.
5. Levelling (manual corners, then automatic).
6. Model test.
7. Resume printing after an interruption.
8. Software installation and LAN printing.
9. Mainboard wiring diagram and a troubleshooting guide.

The Centauri Carbon 2 Combo manual follows a similar order for an enclosed printer: specs, components, unboxing, installing the printer and its multi-colour unit, loading filament, the first print, ElegooSlicer and LAN printing.

## Quick start on an Elegoo FDM printer

- **Unbox:** remove the transport screws or locks the manual marks. On the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo, three screws lock the bed.
- **Assemble:** Neptune printers need the gantry fitted to the base; Centauri printers ship assembled.
- **Level:** on the Neptune 4 Pro, set the centre height with paper, adjust the corners, run the automatic mesh and set the Z offset.
- **Load filament** from the screen menu and print the test model from the USB drive.

## What a typical Elegoo resin manual covers

Resin manuals such as the Mars 3 manual cover safety with resin, levelling the build plate with a sheet of paper, filling the vat, the first print from the USB drive, slicing in CHITUBOX and washing and curing the parts.

## Maintenance Elegoo recommends

- Wipe the printer with a dry cloth while it is switched off.
- Lubricate the X/Y rods and the Z lead screws as the manual says.
- Clean the nozzle while it is warm.
- On resin printers, keep sharp tools away from the release film and clean the vat when you change resin.

## Common problems and fixes

| Symptom | Fix from Elegoo's guide |
|---|---|
| Nozzle clog | Heat to about 230 °C and clear the nozzle |
| First layer does not stick | Re-set the nozzle distance and add a brim |
| Layer shift | Lower the speed and check belt tension |
| Stringing | Increase retraction and use a small Z hop |
| Axis does not move | Check cables, limit switches and belts |

## Firmware and software

Firmware for each model is in its Download Center entry with update notes. For the Saturn, Elegoo's guide says to copy the files to the root of a USB drive and install the .LCD file before the .CBD file. Elegoo lists ElegooSlicer, OrcaSlicer, ELEGOO Cura, CHITUBOX and ELEGOO SatelLite as slicers.
