**The Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (X1C) is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, a hardened steel nozzle, a lidar sensor and optional four-spool AMS multi-colour printing.** It was Bambu Lab's first printer, launched on Kickstarter in May 2022. Bambu Lab ended X1-series production on 31 March 2026, so this page also covers how long the X1C stays supported.

## Who the Bambu X1 Carbon suits

The X1C was built for engineering materials. The enclosure, the 300 °C all-metal hot end, the hardened steel nozzle and the activated carbon filter let it print PA (nylon), PC and fibre-reinforced filaments, as well as everyday PLA and PETG. The lidar scans the first layer and helps calibrate flow.

It was sold in two versions: the **X1-Carbon** on its own and the **X1-Carbon Combo** with an AMS for four-colour printing. The plain X1 (without the "Carbon" parts) had the chamber camera only as an option.

If you are buying new today, look at its successors in our [Bambu Lab 3D printers overview](/printers/bambu-lab-3d-printers/) – the [Bambu Lab P2S](/printers/bambu-lab-p2s/) has the same 256 mm build volume.

## X1 Carbon end of life: what it means for owners

Bambu Lab's announcement of 31 March 2026 sets these dates:

| Support | Ends |
|---|---|
| Production | 31 March 2026 |
| Bug-fix and feature firmware | 31 May 2027 |
| Security patches | 31 May 2029 |
| Spare parts and repair service | 31 March 2031 |

**Tip:** if you own an X1C, plan for wear parts (nozzles, hot ends, belts) while official spares are sold.

## First setup

Bambu Lab's Quick Start Guide for the X1-Carbon Combo describes this order:

1. Do not connect power until assembly is finished.
2. Remove the transport screws and slide the AMS out of the top of the box.
3. Remove the AMS packing, the toolhead cardboard and the foam pieces.
4. Mount the AMS on top and connect its PTFE tubes and the Bambu Bus cables.
5. Fit the screen and lock it in place.
6. Remove the three hot-bed screws.
7. Power on and follow the on-screen calibration.
8. Load filament into the AMS, apply glue to the plate and print a sample file from the printer.

## Connecting the X1C

The X1 Carbon joins 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi. You slice and send prints in **Bambu Studio** and monitor them in the **Bambu Handy** app, with the chamber camera for live view. Without the cloud, you can use LAN mode or print from a microSD card.

## Bambu Lab X1 Carbon manual and firmware

Bambu Lab publishes the X1 manual on the Bambu Lab Wiki in the X1 series section, which has the quick start, printer features, maintenance, troubleshooting and part-replacement guides. Firmware installs from the touchscreen, Bambu Handy or Bambu Studio until the update dates above.
