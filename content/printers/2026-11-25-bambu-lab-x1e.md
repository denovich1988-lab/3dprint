**The Bambu Lab X1E is an enterprise version of the X1 Carbon with a heated chamber up to 60 °C, a 320 °C hotend, wired Ethernet and network security features for offices and engineering teams.** It keeps the X1 Carbon's 256 × 256 × 256 mm CoreXY platform and AMS support. Bambu Lab announced it in October 2023 at a launch price of US$2,499, sold through resellers.

## Bambu Lab X1E at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 256 × 256 × 256 mm |
| Speed / acceleration | 500 mm/s, 20,000 mm/s² |
| Hotend | Up to 320 °C, hardened steel |
| Chamber | Active heating to 60 °C (380 W heater) |
| Network | Wi-Fi 2.4 GHz, Ethernet 100 Mbps, 802.1X, WPA2-Enterprise |
| Filtration | G3 pre-filter, H12 HEPA, activated carbon |
| Multi-colour | Up to 16 colours with 4 AMS |
| Launch price | US$2,499 (October 2023, via resellers) |

## Bambu Lab X1E vs X1 Carbon

| Feature | X1E | X1 Carbon |
|---|---|---|
| Chamber heating | Active, up to 60 °C | Passive |
| Max nozzle temperature | 320 °C | 300 °C |
| Ethernet | Yes | No |
| Enterprise Wi-Fi (802.1X, WPA2-Enterprise) | Yes | No |
| Network kill switches | Physical, separate for Wi-Fi and Ethernet | No |
| Filtration | HEPA + activated carbon | Activated carbon |

**Why it matters:** a heated chamber reduces warping and layer splitting on ABS, ASA, PA and PPS parts, and the network features let IT teams put the printer on a managed corporate network. Bambu Lab's wiki says an [X1 Carbon](/printers/bambu-lab-x1-carbon/) cannot be converted into an X1E.

## Who the X1E suits

The X1E is aimed at professional and engineering users who print functional parts in high-temperature materials and must meet company network rules. For home use, the [X1 Carbon](/printers/bambu-lab-x1-carbon/) or the newer [H2D](/printers/bambu-lab-h2d/) usually make more sense.

## Setting up the X1E

The X1E follows the X1 unboxing: remove the packaging and transport fixings, fit the AMS if you have one, power on and follow the on-screen calibration. During this first setup you connect either by **Ethernet** or by **direct Wi-Fi** with your Bambu account. Enterprise Wi-Fi is added afterwards by loading certificate files from the setup guide.

## Connecting and network security

- **Ethernet** takes priority when both wired and wireless are connected; custom IP and DNS are supported.
- **WPA2-Enterprise** supports EAP-PEAP, EAP-TLS and EAP-TTLS, with CA and client certificates.
- **Kill switches** physically turn off Wi-Fi and Ethernet, and the network module can be removed.
- **LAN-only mode** keeps print jobs off Bambu Cloud; firmware updates still need internet access.

## Bambu Lab X1E manual and firmware

Bambu Lab publishes the X1E documentation on the Bambu Lab Wiki under the X1 series: an X1E FAQ, the X1E network connection guide, unboxing steps and the firmware release history. Firmware updates are installed from the touchscreen, Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy when the printer is online.

## Software

Slice in **Bambu Studio** on Windows or macOS; chamber temperature can be set from the touchscreen, Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy. Other G-code slicers work, but some features may not. Check exported files with our [3MF to STL converter](/3mf-to-stl/) or [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).
