**The Snapmaker Artisan is a 3-in-1 machine that 3D prints, laser engraves and cuts, and CNC carves, with a 400 × 400 × 400 mm dual-extrusion print volume and toolheads you swap in about a minute.** It is the successor to the Snapmaker 2.0, has an all-metal frame and comes with an enclosure. Snapmaker opened pre-orders on 9 August 2022 at US$2,799 including the enclosure.

## Snapmaker Artisan at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Functions | 3D printing, laser engraving and cutting, CNC carving |
| Print volume | 400 × 400 × 400 mm (350 × 400 × 400 mm dual) |
| Laser | 10 W or 40 W, 450–460 nm, 400 × 400 mm |
| CNC | 200 W spindle, up to 18,000 RPM, 400 × 400 mm |
| Enclosure | Included, with emergency stop |
| Screen | 7-inch Android touchscreen |
| Accuracy | ±0.1 mm |
| Pre-order price | US$2,799 with enclosure (August 2022) |

## 3D printing on the Artisan

The printing module has two quick-swap hot ends for two colours or a support material such as PVA or breakaway PLA. The bed is zone-heated – up to 110 °C in the 260 × 260 mm centre and 80 °C at the edges – with a double-sided textured PEI sheet. Snapmaker recommends up to 180 mm/s, so the Artisan is slower than modern CoreXY printers.

**Tip:** nylon and fibre-filled nylon need the optional hardened steel nozzle.

## Laser module

The 10 W and 40 W laser modules engrave at up to 200 mm/s over a 400 × 400 mm area. Snapmaker says they cut basswood plywood up to 5 mm (10 W) or 15 mm (40 W) thick. The enclosure reduces the laser hazard from Class 4 to Class 1. You can turn SVG artwork into 3D models for printing with our [SVG to STL tool](/svg-to-stl/).

## CNC module

The 200 W CNC spindle reaches 18,000 RPM and takes bits with 0.5–6.35 mm shanks. It carves wood, MDF, acrylic and PCB, and Snapmaker says it can also cut aluminium, brass and copper. Work area is 400 × 400 mm.

## Who the Snapmaker Artisan suits

The Artisan suits makers, schools and small workshops that want one enclosed machine for printing, engraving and light carving. If you only need 3D printing, a dedicated printer is faster and costs less; see the [Snapmaker U1](/printers/snapmaker-u1/) or the wider [Snapmaker 3D printers guide](/printers/snapmaker-3d-printers/).

## Connecting the Artisan

The Artisan connects over **Wi-Fi**, a **USB cable** or a **USB drive**. Jobs are prepared in Snapmaker Luban and sent to the machine or loaded from the drive. The 10 W laser module includes a camera for positioning the material.

## Snapmaker Artisan manual and software

Snapmaker documents the Artisan on the Snapmaker Wiki, including setup guides and FAQs for each module. Software is **Snapmaker Luban**, a combined slicer and CAM program for Windows, macOS and Linux (tested on Ubuntu); third-party software also works.
