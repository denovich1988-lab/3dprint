**A Snapmaker 3D printer is one of three designs: the U1 four-toolhead toolchanger, the J1 or J1s IDEX dual-extruder printer, or the Artisan and Snapmaker 2.0 3-in-1 machines that also laser-engrave and CNC-carve.** This page lists them with Snapmaker's own figures as of November 2026. Snapmaker showed no new printer at Formnext 2026 and focused on accessories for the U1.

## Snapmaker 3D printers at a glance

| Model | Type | 3D print volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| U1 | 4-toolhead toolchanger, CoreXY | 270 × 270 × 270 mm | 500 mm/s, 20,000 mm/s² |
| J1 / J1s | IDEX (independent dual extruder) | 300 × 200 × 200 mm | Copy and mirror modes |
| Artisan | 3-in-1 (print, laser, CNC) | 400 × 400 × 400 mm | Enclosure included |
| 2.0 A350T / F350 | 3-in-1 modular | 320 × 335 × 310 mm | Smaller A150 and A250 versions too |

## Snapmaker U1: four-colour toolchanger

The **U1** swaps between four toolheads with Snapmaker's SnapSwap system, so each colour has its own nozzle and only a small prime tower is needed. Snapmaker lists a 270 mm cube build volume, 500 mm/s toolhead speed, 20,000 mm/s² acceleration, 32 mm³/s flow, a 300 °C nozzle, a 100 °C PEI bed and a 2 MP chamber camera. Base filaments are PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA and PCTG; the optional top cover adds ABS, ASA, PA and PC. The Kickstarter campaign started on 19 August 2025 with an early-bird price of US$749.

**Why it matters:** a toolchanger wastes far less filament than single-nozzle colour changers. Full specs are on our [Snapmaker U1](/printers/snapmaker-u1/) page.

## Snapmaker J1 and J1s: IDEX printing

The **J1** (announced October 2022) and **J1s** (June 2023) have two independent print heads. In default and backup modes the build volume is 300 × 200 × 200 mm; copy mode prints two parts at once at 160 mm wide, and mirror mode prints mirrored pairs at 150 mm wide. Snapmaker lists 350 mm/s, 10,000 mm/s², a 300 °C nozzle, a 100 °C glass bed and a 5-inch touchscreen. The J1s added a rear cooling fan and pre-installed side walls.

**Tip:** IDEX is ideal for soluble PVA supports or two materials in one part without purge waste.

## Snapmaker Artisan: large 3-in-1

The **Artisan** prints up to 400 × 400 × 400 mm with a dual-extrusion module and swaps in about a minute to a 10 W or 40 W laser module or a 200 W CNC module, each with a 400 × 400 mm work area. It has a 7-inch touchscreen and comes with an enclosure. Pre-orders opened in August 2022.

## Snapmaker 2.0: the original modular machine

The **Snapmaker 2.0** launched on Kickstarter in May 2019 and comes in A150, A250 and A350 sizes, plus the A250T and A350T Quick Swap and the F250 and F350 versions. The A350T and F350 print up to 320 × 335 × 310 mm with a single extruder and include a 1.6 W laser and a 50 W CNC module.

## Which Snapmaker to choose

1. **Multicolour prints with little waste:** U1.
2. **Soluble supports or duplicate parts:** J1s.
3. **Printing plus serious laser and CNC work:** Artisan.
4. **A smaller 3-in-1 for a desk:** Snapmaker 2.0.

## Snapmaker software

**Snapmaker Orca** is an open-source slicer built on OrcaSlicer for Windows and Mac. Snapmaker says it is optimised for the U1, with the Artisan, 2.0, J1 and J1s not yet fully supported. **Snapmaker Luban** remains the software for 3D printing, laser and CNC on the J1s, Artisan and 2.0. The U1 also works with standard OrcaSlicer and the Snapmaker app. To turn a logo into a printable part, try our [SVG to STL converter](/svg-to-stl/).
