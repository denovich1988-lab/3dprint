**The Flashforge Adventurer 3 is a compact, fully enclosed FDM 3D printer with a 150 × 150 × 150 mm build volume, a quick-swap nozzle and a built-in camera.** Flashforge unveiled it at CES 2018 as a plug-and-play printer for homes, offices and classrooms. It prints PLA and ABS and connects over Wi-Fi, Ethernet or a USB stick.

## Flashforge Adventurer 3 at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 150 × 150 × 150 mm |
| Nozzle / bed | 240 °C / 100 °C |
| Nozzle | 0.4 mm, quick-swap |
| Build plate | Heated, flexible, removable |
| Enclosure | Fully enclosed |
| Camera | Built-in 2 MP |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi, Ethernet, USB stick, cloud |
| Screen | 2.8-inch touchscreen |
| Weight | 9 kg |

## Who the Adventurer 3 suits

It suits beginners, families and classrooms that want a quiet, closed printer for small PLA parts with little setup. The enclosure keeps fingers away from hot parts and helps with ABS. The build volume is small and the spool holder only fits smaller reels, so it is less suited to large prints. Flashforge's newer [Adventurer 5M](/printers/flashforge-adventurer-5m/) is faster and larger.

## Adventurer 3 features

- **Quick-swap nozzle:** a buckle release lets you change the 0.4 mm nozzle without tools.
- **Flexible heated plate:** removable and bendable, so prints pop off.
- **No levelling out of the box,** according to Flashforge.
- **Filament run-out detection** and automatic filament loading.
- **Built-in camera** for remote monitoring.
- Flashforge quotes a noise level of about 45 dB.

## Setting up the Adventurer 3

1. Unpack the printer and remove the packing pieces inside the chamber.
2. Power on and follow the touchscreen setup.
3. Fit a small 1.75 mm PLA spool on the internal holder and use the screen's load function to feed it.
4. Connect to Wi-Fi or Ethernet, or use a USB stick.
5. Print the sample file to check the first layer.

**Tip:** if prints do not stick, clean the flexible plate and make sure it is seated flat on the bed before starting.

## Connecting the Adventurer 3

Send files from **FlashPrint** over **Wi-Fi** or **Ethernet**, print from a **USB stick**, or use cloud printing through FlashCloud or Polar Cloud. The camera feed lets you watch prints from your computer. To check a sliced file, open it in our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## Adventurer 3 manual

The quick start guide that ships with the printer points users to Flashforge's official website for the latest user guide and quick start guide. Downloads for the Adventurer family are in the Download Center on Flashforge's website.

## Firmware and software

Slice with **FlashPrint**, Flashforge's free slicer with standard and expert modes. Get firmware only from Flashforge's website and follow the instructions supplied with it.

## Adventurer 3 vs Adventurer 3 Lite

| Model | Difference |
|---|---|
| Adventurer 3 | Camera, filament run-out sensor, flexible heated bed |
| Adventurer 3 Lite | Same volume and connections, no camera or run-out sensor |
