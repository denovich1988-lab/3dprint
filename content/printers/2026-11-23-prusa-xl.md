**The Prusa XL is a large CoreXY 3D printer with a 360 × 360 × 360 mm build volume and an automatic toolchanger that holds 1, 2 or 5 toolheads.** Prusa Research announced it in November 2021, started shipping in March 2023, and in August 2026 replaced it with the updated XL+.

## Who the Prusa XL suits

The XL suits engineers, schools and small production shops that print big parts or several materials at once. Because each material has its own toolhead, a multi-material print wastes very little filament compared with single-nozzle systems that purge between colours. The segmented heat bed only heats the area under the part, which saves time and energy on small prints.

## Prusa XL toolheads

- **Single-tool** – one Nextruder; you can add more heads later.
- **Dual-head** – two materials, such as a model and soluble PVA support.
- **Five-head** – up to five colours or materials in one print.

Each toolhead can carry a different nozzle size between 0.25 and 0.8 mm. Prusa notes that with three or more toolheads the printer needs an extra power supply, which ships with those versions.

## Prusa XL vs XL+

Prusa announced the XL+ on 13 August 2026. Unshipped XL orders were upgraded automatically, and existing owners keep firmware, spare parts and support. The XL+ keeps the 360 mm build volume, 290 °C nozzle and 1–5 toolheads, and adds:

- an eddy-current tool-offset sensor that Prusa says is about 20× faster than the old pin;
- 360° part cooling, GT1.5 belts and high-flow nozzles as standard;
- a nozzle wiper;
- a new enclosure option.

Prusa lists the XL+ bed at up to 115 °C. Upgrade kits from XL to XL+ are sold for each toolhead version.

## First setup

The XL ships assembled or semi-assembled; the XL+ ships assembled, and you fit the toolheads. On first start a wizard runs. For the XL+, Prusa lists this order:

1. Firmware check and nozzle seal height.
2. Prepare the printer.
3. Network and Prusa Connect setup.
4. Calibration tests and dock position calibration for each tool.
5. Loadcell test and filament sensor calibration.
6. Phase stepping calibration, then the first prints.

## Connecting the XL

The XL has Ethernet built in and optional Wi-Fi. **PrusaLink** gives you a local web interface at the printer's IP address, and **Prusa Connect** lets you send and watch prints from anywhere. You can also print from a USB drive. Slice in **PrusaSlicer**, which includes profiles for each toolhead setup. ## Manual and firmware

Prusa publishes the XL and XL+ assembly manuals in the Prusa Knowledge Base under Original Prusa XL, with separate guides for single-, dual- and five-head versions. The XL Handbook and XL+ Handbook PDFs are in the Firmware & Downloads section, alongside the latest firmware.
