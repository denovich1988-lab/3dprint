**An Anycubic 3D printer is either a Kobra filament (FDM) printer or a Photon resin printer, and the current range runs from the open-frame Kobra 4 and Kobra X to the enclosed Kobra S1 Max and the large-format Photon P1 Max.** This page lists the models on Anycubic's global store as of November 2026, with Anycubic's own figures.

## Anycubic FDM printers at a glance

| Model | Build volume | Frame | Multi-colour |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kobra 4 / Kobra 4 Combo | 260 × 260 × 260 mm | Open | ACE 2 Pro: 4 colours, up to 8 with two units |
| Kobra X | 260 × 260 × 260 mm | Open | 4 colours built into the head, up to 19 with ACE 2 Pro |
| Kobra S1 ACE 2 Pro Combo | 250 × 250 × 250 mm | Enclosed CoreXY | 4 colours, up to 16 with four units |
| Kobra S1 Max Combo | 350 × 350 × 350 mm | Enclosed, heated chamber (65 °C) | Up to 16 with four units |
| Kobra 3 Max V2 Combo | 420 × 420 × 500 mm | — | 4 colours, up to 8 |

## Kobra 4 and Kobra X: entry multi-colour

The **Kobra 4** has a 260 mm cube build volume, a 300 °C nozzle and up to 600 mm/s, with an ACE 2 Pro feeder for four colours. The **Kobra X**, first shown at Formnext 2025, keeps the same build volume but moves the filament changer into the print head (Anycubic calls it ACE Gen 2), which Anycubic designed to cut waste. See our [Anycubic Kobra X](/printers/anycubic-kobra-x/) page for full specs.

## Kobra S1 and S1 Max: enclosed CoreXY

The **Kobra S1 ACE 2 Pro Combo** is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 250 mm cube build volume, a 320 °C nozzle, a 120 °C bed, 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s². Its ACE 2 Pro dries filament at up to 65 °C. The **Kobra S1 Max** scales up to 350 × 350 × 350 mm, with a 350 °C hot end and active chamber heating to 65 °C for engineering filaments. Our [Kobra S1 Combo](/printers/anycubic-kobra-s1-combo/) page covers the original S1.

## Kobra 3 Max V2: large format

The **Kobra 3 Max V2** has the biggest FDM build volume in the range at 420 × 420 × 500 mm, with up to 600 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² and an ACE 2 Pro for four to eight colours. It suits cosplay helmets, large props and long functional parts.

## Anycubic resin printers

| Model | Build volume | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Photon Mono 4 Ultra | 153.4 × 87 × 165 mm | Compact, up to 80 mm/h |
| Photon Mono M7 | 223 × 126 × 230 mm | Up to 150 mm/h |
| Photon Mono M7 Pro | 223 × 126 × 230 mm | Up to 170 mm/h |
| Photon Mono M7 Max | 298 × 164 × 300 mm | Up to 83 mm/h |
| Photon P1 | 223 × 126 × 230 mm | Optional dual-vat platform for two resins |
| Photon P1 Max | 285.5 × 214 × 300 mm | 14-inch 12K screen, temperature-controlled vat |

The **Photon P1** launched on Kickstarter on 17 December 2025 at a US$799 retail price. Resin printers give finer detail for miniatures and figures but need gloves, ventilation, washing and curing.

## Anycubic software and apps

- **Anycubic Slicer Next** – FDM slicer based on the open-source OrcaSlicer.
- **Anycubic app** – remote monitoring and cloud printing on iOS and Android.
- **Photon Workshop** – slicer for Photon resin printers.

Before slicing a downloaded multi-colour project, check it in our [3MF viewer](/3mf-viewer/); to use a 3MF part in another program, convert it with the [3MF to STL converter](/3mf-to-stl/).

## Where to find Anycubic manuals

Anycubic publishes user manuals and quick start guides on the Anycubic Wiki, filed by model, together with firmware update guides and software downloads.

## Which Anycubic printer to choose

Pick the **Kobra 4** or **Kobra X** for an affordable first multi-colour printer, the **Kobra S1** for ABS and ASA in an enclosure, the **S1 Max** for large engineering parts, and a **Photon** resin printer for miniatures.
