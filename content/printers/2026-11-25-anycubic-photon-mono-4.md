**The Anycubic Photon Mono 4 3D printer is an entry-level resin printer with a 7-inch 10K mono LCD, 17 × 17 µm XY resolution and a 153.4 × 87 × 165 mm build volume.** Anycubic announced it in September 2024 alongside the Mono 4 Ultra. It levels by hand, prints from a USB drive and suits miniatures, jewellery models and other small detailed parts.

## Anycubic Photon Mono 4 at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| LCD | 7-inch, 10K mono |
| XY resolution | 17 × 17 µm |
| Build volume | 153.4 × 87 × 165 mm (2.2 L) |
| Speed | Up to 50 mm/h (standard resin), 70 mm/h (high-speed resin) |
| Z axis | Single linear rail, 30 µm accuracy |
| Levelling | Manual, four screws |
| Vat | Up to 0.39 L, not heated |
| Connectivity | USB drive only |
| Size and weight | 230 × 235 × 391 mm, 4 kg |

## Who the Photon Mono 4 suits

The Mono 4 suits first-time resin users and tabletop painters who want fine detail on small parts and do not need Wi-Fi or an app. Its vat is not heated, so in a cold room you may need longer exposures or a warmer workspace. For larger parts, look at the bigger models in the [Anycubic Photon range](/printers/anycubic-photon/).

## Photon Mono 4 vs Mono 4 Ultra

| Feature | Mono 4 | Mono 4 Ultra |
|---|---|---|
| Light source | Parallel matrix, ~3000 µW/cm² | COB with Fresnel lens, ≥3500 µW/cm² |
| Max speed (standard / high-speed resin) | 50 / 70 mm/h | 80 / 120 mm/h |
| Z accuracy | 30 µm | 20 µm |
| Touchscreen | 2.8-inch | 4.3-inch |
| Connectivity | USB | USB, Wi-Fi, OTA firmware |
| Size and weight | 230 × 235 × 391 mm, 4 kg | 231 × 236 × 430 mm, 5 kg |
| Power | 54 W | 66 W |

**Why it matters:** both print the same size and the same pixel detail. The Ultra is mainly faster and more convenient; the Mono 4 is the simpler, lighter choice.

## Setting up the Photon Mono 4

Following Anycubic's quick start, at a high level:

1. Unpack, check the parts and fit the LCD screen protector.
2. Power on and attach the build platform.
3. Level: lay the levelling paper on the screen, press Home, press the platform down gently, tighten the four screws and set Z zero.
4. Run the exposure test screen, then fit the vat and fill it with resin below the max mark.
5. Print the test model from the USB drive, then run Anycubic's exposure test file to dial in your resin.

**Tip:** the USB drive must be FAT or FAT32, 32 GB or smaller, with the print file in the root folder.

## Maintenance

Anycubic's guide covers the vat cleaning function, checking the release film's use count, lubricating the Z screw and cleaning with alcohol. Wear gloves and work in a ventilated room when handling resin.

## Photon Mono 4 manual and firmware

Anycubic publishes the Photon Mono 4 quick start guide, FAQ and firmware files on the Anycubic Wiki under resin 3D printers. With no Wi-Fi, firmware is installed from a USB drive through the system menu.
