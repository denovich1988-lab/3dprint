**Creality lists the official K1 Max manual in the Download Center on its website under the K1 Max page, and on the Creality Wiki under K1 Flagship Series → K1 Max → Quick Start Guide.** The wiki also has a firmware upgrade guide for the printer. This page summarises the Creality K1 Max manual in our own words; it does not replace it.

## What the official K1 Max manual covers

The user manual is organised around these topics:

1. **Installing the product** – unboxing, screen cable and spool holder.
2. **Power-on guide** – language, transport screws, LiDAR check, network, time zone, cloud binding and self-test.
3. **Screen menus** – Tune and Prepare, Files, extrude and retract, calibration and self-inspection.
4. **Printing** – local printing, LAN printing from Creality Print and cloud printing.
5. **AI functions** – camera and LiDAR features.
6. **Network** – wired and Wi-Fi setup.
7. **Precautions, lubrication and maintenance.**

## Quick-start summary

**Unboxing.** The K1 Max arrives almost fully assembled. Remove the foam, connect the touchscreen cable and fit the spool holder at the back.

**Power-on guide.**

1. Switch on and choose a language.
2. Remove the three screws that lock the bed for shipping; the screen shows where they are.
3. Make sure nothing blocks the LiDAR's view.
4. Connect to Ethernet or 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, set the time zone and bind the printer to Creality Cloud if you want remote access.
5. Let the self-test run automatic levelling and input shaping until it reports completion.

**First print.** Load filament with the Extrude function, then print a file from the screen, send one over the LAN from Creality Print, or start one from Creality Cloud.

**Tip:** reviewers found that the printer needed one Creality Cloud binding to unlock LAN and Wi-Fi printing, so do this during the power-on guide.

## Maintenance Creality recommends

- Grease the parts marked in the manual every **300 hours** of printing.
- Keep the area around the LiDAR clear, as the power-on guide asks.

## Common problems and fixes

| Problem | What to try |
|---|---|
| Wi-Fi network not found | The K1 Max supports 2.4 GHz only; use a 2.4 GHz band or Ethernet |
| AI alerts missing | Bind the printer to Creality Cloud |

## Firmware and software

Creality's K1-series firmware updates **over the air** when the printer is online, from a **USB drive** with the file from the download page, or through the **Creality Cloud** app. The download page also offers **Creality Print**, the recommended slicer.
