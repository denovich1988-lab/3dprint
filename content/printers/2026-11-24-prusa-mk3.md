**The Prusa MK3 is Prusa Research's open-frame i3 3D printer, sold in three versions – the MK3, MK3S and MK3S+ – with a 250 × 210 × 210 mm build volume and a direct-drive Bondtech extruder.** Prusa introduced the MK3 in September 2017 and the final MK3S+ in November 2020. Kit sales ended on 29 February 2024, and the MK4S is now Prusa's i3 model, but the MK3S+ is still supported and can be upgraded.

## Prusa MK3S+ at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 250 × 210 × 210 mm |
| Bed temperature | Up to 120 °C |
| Extruder | Direct drive, Bondtech gears, E3D V6 hotend |
| Levelling | SuperPINDA probe, mesh bed levelling |
| Print surface | Magnetic heatbed, removable PEI spring steel sheets |
| Multi-material | Up to 5 with the MMU2S |
| Weight | 7 kg |
| Status | Discontinued (kits from 29 February 2024) |

## Prusa MK3, MK3S and MK3S+ versions

| Version | Released | Main changes |
|---|---|---|
| MK3 | September 2017 | Bondtech extruder, magnetic heatbed with PEI sheets, power panic |
| MK3S | July 2019 | Lever-triggered optical filament sensor that works with any filament, redesigned extruder |
| MK3S+ | November 2020 | SuperPINDA probe, metal Y bearing clips, reinforced X-ends with new belt tensioning, revised filament path |

**Tip:** the MK3S and MK3S+ share the same firmware, so make sure you pick the MK3S/MK3S+ build in Prusa's download section, not the original MK3 one.

## Who the Prusa MK3 suits today

A used MK3S+ suits people who want a reliable, well-documented i3 printer with a huge base of guides, spare parts and community mods. It is slower than current CoreXY printers and has no network connection without an add-on. If you buy new, look at the [Prusa MK4S](/printers/prusa-mk4/) or the [Prusa Core One](/printers/prusa-core-one/) instead.

## Setting up a Prusa MK3S+

Kits are built with Prusa's illustrated assembly manual; the assembled version needs only unpacking. After that, the printer's built-in wizard runs a self-test, XYZ calibration with the SuperPINDA probe, a first-layer calibration to set the Z height, and filament loading. Then print one of the test files from the SD card.

## Connecting the MK3S+

The MK3S+ prints from the **SD card** (a 16 GB card comes in the box) and uses **USB** for firmware updates. Wi-Fi or Ethernet printing needs an add-on such as a Raspberry Pi running Prusa's network software. Check sliced files with our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/) before you copy them to the card.

## Prusa MK3 manual

Prusa publishes the MK3S+ assembly manual, user handbook and troubleshooting articles in the Prusa Knowledge Base. For an overview of what it covers, see our [Prusa MK3S manual guide](/printers/prusa-mk3s-manual/).

## Firmware and software

Firmware is flashed over **USB** with PrusaSlicer, using the file from Prusa's downloads. Slicing is done in **PrusaSlicer**, which still includes MK3S+ profiles.

## Upgrade paths

Prusa offers MK3.5, MK3.9 and MK4 upgrade kits, plus an MK3/S to MK4S kit, which reuse the frame and many parts. The successors added a lot: the MK4 (March 2023) brought the Nextruder with load-cell levelling, a 32-bit board and Ethernet, and the MK4S (August 2024) added 360° cooling and a high-flow nozzle.
