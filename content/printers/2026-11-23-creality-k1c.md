**The Creality K1C 3D printer is an enclosed CoreXY machine with a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume, a quick-swap hardened-steel-tip nozzle and a built-in AI camera.** Creality opened pre-orders in January 2024 at US$559. It builds on the K1 with changes aimed at carbon-fibre-filled filaments.

## Creality K1C at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 220 × 220 × 250 mm |
| Max speed / acceleration | 600 mm/s / 20,000 mm/s² |
| Nozzle / bed | 300 °C / 100 °C |
| Enclosure | Enclosed, carbon air filter |
| Camera | AI camera included |
| Connectivity | Wi-Fi, USB drive |
| Weight | 12.4 kg |

## Who the Creality K1C suits

The K1C suits makers who want an enclosed, fast printer for PLA and PETG that can also handle ABS, ASA and carbon-fibre-filled PLA, PA and PET. Creality says the typical print speed is 300 mm/s and that silent mode runs at 45 dB or less at one metre. Buyers who need a larger bed or multi-colour printing should look at the CFS-equipped [Creality K2 Combo](/printers/creality-k2-combo/).

## Creality K1C vs K1

| | K1 | K1C |
|---|---|---|
| Carbon-fibre filaments | Not listed at launch | PLA-CF, PA-CF, PET-CF |
| Nozzle | 0.4 mm | Quick-swap tri-metal, hardened steel tip |
| Extruder | Dual-gear direct drive | All-metal direct drive |
| AI camera | Optional | Included |
| Air filter | No | Yes |
| Build plate | Flexible plate | PEI plate with silicone wiping strip |

Both share the 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume, 600 mm/s top speed and 350 W rating.

## Setting up the Creality K1C

Creality's user manual describes these steps:

1. Remove the packaging, the grey foam and the foam holding the extruder.
2. Fit the door handle and the air filter cartridge.
3. Connect the touchscreen's flat cable and snap the screen into place.
4. Fit the material rack, load filament into the PTFE tube and install the top cover.
5. Set the voltage switch (115 V or 230 V) to match your mains before plugging in.
6. Switch on, accept the privacy policy, join your network, choose a time zone and bind the printer in the Creality Cloud app.
7. Let the self-check run for about 11 minutes without touching the printer.

**Tip:** for low-temperature or flexible filaments, Creality advises removing the top cover if the chamber goes above 35 °C.

## Connecting the Creality K1C

Print from a **USB drive**, over **LAN** from Creality Print (add the printer by scanning or by IP address), or remotely through **Creality Cloud**. The AI camera sends failure alerts and records time-lapses; active alerts need the Creality Print or Creality Cloud app.

## Creality K1C manual

Creality publishes the K1C user manual and maintenance tips on the Creality Wiki under K1 Flagship Series → K1C. The manual recommends recalibrating the platform after 300 hours of printing or after replacing the platform or nozzle, and lubricating the X, Y and Z axes as routine maintenance.

## Firmware and software

The K1C runs Creality OS, based on Klipper. For the K1 series, Creality offers firmware updates over the air, through the Creality Cloud app, or from a USB drive; the printer reboots several times and you should run the self-tests before printing again. Slice in **Creality Print**, which accepts STL, OBJ and 3MF files. Check a 3MF project first in our [3MF viewer](/3mf-viewer/).
