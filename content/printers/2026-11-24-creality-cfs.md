**The Creality CFS (Creality Filament System) is a sealed four-spool filament box that lets Creality K2-series printers and the Creality Hi print in up to 16 colours when four units are chained.** Creality announced it alongside the K2 Plus in April 2024, and the K2 Plus + CFS combo went on pre-sale in June 2024. Each unit stores, feeds and switches filament automatically, including refilling from a matching spool when one runs out.

## Creality CFS at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Slots | 4 × 1 kg spools |
| Max units per printer | 4 (16 colours) |
| Compatible printers | K2, K2 Pro, K2 Plus, Creality Hi |
| RFID | Yes, reads Creality RFID spools |
| Storage | Sealed, desiccant, temperature and humidity display |
| Active drying | No (CFS 2 has it) |
| Filament diameter | 1.75 ± 0.05 mm |
| Spool size | 197–202 mm diameter, 42–68 mm wide |
| Size and weight | 379 × 314 × 276 mm, 4.56 kg |
| Power | DC 24 V, 20 W rated |

## Which printers work with the Creality CFS

Creality lists the **K2 series** – [K2 Plus](/printers/creality-k2-plus/), [K2 Pro](/printers/creality-k2-pro/) and K2 – and the **Creality Hi** as compatible. The K2 combos and the Hi Combo ship with one CFS in the box. See our [Creality K2 combo guide](/printers/creality-k2-combo/) for how the combos differ.

K1-series printers, including the [Creality K1 Max](/printers/creality-k1-max/) and [K1C](/printers/creality-k1c/), do not use the CFS. Creality sells the **CFS-C** for them instead (see below).

## Supported filaments and spools

Creality lists PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, PET and PLA-CF for the CFS. It does **not** support flexible filament such as TPU 95A, damp BVOH or PVA, or warped cardboard spools. Spools must be 1 kg size, 197–202 mm across and 42–68 mm wide.

**Tip:** cardboard spools can deform and jam the feeder. Respool the filament onto a plastic spool or print an adapter ring before loading it.

## RFID and non-Creality filament

When you load a Creality spool with an RFID tag, the CFS reads its material and colour and sends them to the printer, so the slicer shows the right filament. RFID is optional: for other brands you choose the brand, type and colour on the printer screen yourself.

## Humidity and drying

The CFS is a sealed box with desiccant and a small display that shows the temperature and humidity inside. It keeps dry filament dry but does **not** heat or actively dry spools. If you print moisture-sensitive materials, dry them first, or look at the CFS 2, which dries at up to 85 °C.

## How to load filament into the CFS

1. Open the lid and place a spool in a free slot.
2. Push the filament end into the slot's PTFE tube until you feel resistance, then let go – the slot light turns on and the CFS pulls it in.
3. On the printer, select the slot and set the material if it has no RFID tag.
4. Press **Extrude** to feed it to the nozzle; **Retract** cuts the filament and pulls it back.

For connecting a second or third unit, follow the installation steps on the Creality Wiki for your printer.

## CFS vs CFS-C vs CFS Lite vs CFS 2

| Unit | For | Key points |
|---|---|---|
| CFS | K2 series, Creality Hi | 4 slots, up to 4 units, RFID, no drying |
| CFS-C | K1-series printers | 4 slots, one unit per printer, external cutter and waste collection, CAN connection |
| CFS Lite | SPARKX i7 only | Enclosed storage, auto load and resupply, one unit per printer |
| CFS 2 | K2 series (firmware dependent) | 4 slots in two chambers, drying up to 85 °C, RFID, up to 4 units |

Creality says the CFS and CFS 2 can be used together on the same printer, subject to firmware support.

## CFS compared with other multi-colour systems

The CFS works much like the [Bambu Lab AMS](/printers/bambu-lab-ams/): four spools per unit, chained units and one shared nozzle, so multi-colour prints still create purge waste.
