## Ender 3 Max Neo quick start

A summary of the manual's setup steps:

1. **Assemble the frame.** Set the gantry into the base and fix it with the four long screws.
2. **Fit the screen and spool holder**, connect the screen cable, then wire the Z motor and the other labelled connectors.
3. **Set the voltage switch** to 115 V or 230 V to match your mains before powering on.
4. **Initialise.** Go to Control → Reset configuration, then Prepare → Auto Home, and clear the Z value.
5. **Level.** Choose Leveling; the CR Touch probes 25 points. Use the knobs under the bed and a sheet of A4 paper to set a light drag at each corner, then adjust Prepare → Z-offset.
6. **Load filament.** Preheat the nozzle, press the extruder lever and push the filament in until it flows from the nozzle.
7. **First print.** Slice the model, save the G-code to the card, insert it and choose the file on the screen.

**Tip:** the manual asks for file names with Latin letters and numbers only, and warns not to remove the card while printing. To check a sliced file on your computer, use our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## Maintenance Creality recommends

- Wipe the printer with a dry cloth, with the power off.
- Clear dust and stuck filament from the guide rails.
- Clean the nozzle while it is hot, using tools only.
- Use the recommended filament, and keep the printer away from heat, vibration and flammable materials.

## Common problems and fixes

- **First layer does not stick:** the nozzle is too far from the bed. Re-level and lower the Z offset.
- **Under-extrusion or a scratched bed:** the nozzle is too close. Raise the Z offset slightly.
- **Changing filament mid-print:** the manual suggests dropping the print speed to 10% first. To pull filament out after cooling, heat the nozzle to at least 185 °C.
