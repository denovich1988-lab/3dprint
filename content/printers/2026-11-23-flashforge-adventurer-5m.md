**The Flashforge Adventurer 5M 3D printer is an open-frame CoreXY machine with a 220 × 220 × 220 mm build volume, one-click auto levelling and a quick-release nozzle.** Flashforge introduced it in September 2023 at US$399 as the open "sister version" of the enclosed Adventurer 5M Pro (US$599). It is aimed at beginners who want a fast printer for PLA and PETG.

## Who the Flashforge Adventurer 5M suits

The 5M suits first-time buyers and hobbyists who print PLA and PETG and want CoreXY speed without tuning. Flashforge says it arrives 95% pre-assembled. With a 0.6 or 0.8 mm nozzle it also prints TPU and carbon-fibre-filled PLA and PETG. It is not meant for ABS – that needs the enclosed 5M Pro.

The nozzle unlocks with a lever, so changing nozzle size or clearing a clog takes seconds rather than a heated disassembly.

## Adventurer 5M vs Adventurer 5M Pro

| | Adventurer 5M | Adventurer 5M Pro |
|---|---|---|
| Enclosure | Open frame | Fully enclosed |
| Filtration | None | HEPA + activated carbon |
| Camera | Not built in | Built-in camera with time-lapse |
| Extra materials | – | ABS, ASA |
| Launch price (US) | US$399 | US$599 |

Build volume (220 mm), the 280 °C nozzle and Wi-Fi/Ethernet are the same on both.

## First setup

Flashforge's Quick Start Guide lists these steps:

1. Unbox and remove the foam, bags and tape.
2. Remove the transport screws and sheet-metal brackets, then flip up and lock the screen.
3. Fit the spool holder and the guide tube between the run-out sensor and the extruder.
4. Remove the four build-plate lock screws.
5. Switch on, choose a language and join Wi-Fi.
6. Optionally bind the printer in the Flash Maker app by scanning the QR code on the screen.
7. Run the first auto-calibration – noise during it is normal.
8. Load PLA through the sensor, clean the plate, apply glue and print the built-in test file.

## Connecting the Adventurer 5M

The 5M connects over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Send prints from **Orca-Flashforge** (from the Flashforge Download Center), OrcaSlicer 1.8.0 or later, or FlashPrint 5. The **Flash Maker** app handles binding and remote control, and a USB drive works for offline printing.

## Flashforge Adventurer 5M manual

Flashforge publishes the quick-start guide on the Flashforge Wiki under Adventurer Series → Manual → "Quick-start Guide for Your Adventurer 5M", with a separate guide for the 5M Pro in the same place. Firmware updates install from the touchscreen.
