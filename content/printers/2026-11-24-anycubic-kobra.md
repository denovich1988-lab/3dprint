**The Anycubic Kobra is an open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume, a direct-drive extruder and Anycubic's LeviQ 25-point automatic bed levelling.** Anycubic launched it at an online event on 27 March 2022, at a US launch price of $299. It started the Kobra family, which now includes the Kobra 2, Kobra 3, Kobra S1 and Kobra X.

## Anycubic Kobra at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 220 × 220 × 250 mm |
| Speed | Up to 180 mm/s (80–100 mm/s normal) |
| Nozzle / bed | 260 °C / 110 °C |
| Extruder | Integrated direct drive |
| Levelling | LeviQ, 25 points, inductive sensor |
| Build plate | Magnetic PEI spring steel |
| Connectivity | microSD card, USB |
| Weight | 7 kg |

## Who the Kobra suits

The original Kobra suits beginners who find a used one or want a simple, offline printer for PLA, PETG and TPU. The direct-drive extruder handles flexible filament better than a Bowden setup. It has a single Z lead screw and no network connection, and newer Kobra models are much faster.

## Setting up the Anycubic Kobra

1. Join the two main sections with the supplied bolts and screws (about 20 minutes).
2. Connect the cables between the base and the gantry and power on.
3. Run **Prepare → Leveling → Auto-Leveling**; the sensor probes 25 points.
4. Set the **Z-offset** with a sheet of paper under the nozzle.
5. Load filament with **Prepare → Filament → Filament In** and print a test file from the microSD card.

**Tip:** LeviQ maps the bed, but the Z-offset is still yours to set. If the first layer is too thin or too squashed, adjust the Z-offset in small steps rather than re-running levelling.

## Connecting the Kobra

The original Kobra has no Wi-Fi. Copy sliced files to the **microSD card**, or print over **USB** from a computer. Open G-code in our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/) to check it before printing.

## Firmware and software

The Kobra runs Marlin-based firmware. It shipped with **Cura** and Kobra profiles on the microSD card. Before flashing any firmware, check the Kobra section of Anycubic's support pages and note your Z-offset in case a firmware update resets it.
