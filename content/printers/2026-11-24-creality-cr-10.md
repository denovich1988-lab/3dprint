**The Creality CR-10 is a large open-frame 3D printer with a 300 × 300 × 400 mm build volume, a Bowden extruder and a separate control box, sold as a semi-assembled kit from around 2016–2017.** Creality has since used the CR-10 name for a whole family – from the 500 mm cube CR-10 S5 to the compact CR-10 SE.

## Creality CR-10 at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 300 × 300 × 400 mm |
| Extruder | Bowden, 0.4 mm nozzle |
| Filament | 1.75 mm |
| Bed | Removable glass, manual four-corner levelling |
| Z axis | Single lead screw |
| Controls | LCD and knob in a separate control box |
| Connectivity | SD card, USB |
| Power | 110 V / 220 V switch |

## Who the CR-10 suits

The original CR-10 suits tinkerers who want a big build area for props, helmets and large parts and do not mind manual levelling and upgrades. It has no filament run-out sensor and no power-loss resume, so long prints are best watched. For a modern printer with a similar size, look at the [Creality Ender 5 Plus](/printers/creality-ender-5-plus/) or the [Creality K2 Plus](/printers/creality-k2-plus/).

## Setting up the original CR-10

1. Bolt the vertical gantry to the base.
2. Connect the motor, heater and sensor cables to the control box.
3. Power on and **auto-home** the axes.
4. Level the bed with a sheet of paper at the four corners, using the wheels under the bed. Repeat once.
5. Load filament and print the sample file from the SD card.

**Tip:** with a 300 × 300 mm glass bed, re-check the centre after levelling the corners. If the glass is not flat, the middle of the first layer will show it first.

## Connecting the CR-10

Print from the **SD card**, which originally held a Cura installer, a sample G-code file and the assembly instructions, or connect a computer over **USB**. Common slicers for it include Cura, Repetier-Host and Simplify3D. To check a file before a long print, open it in our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## CR-10 manual and firmware

For the original CR-10, the assembly instructions came on the included SD card. For the current CR-10 SE, Creality publishes the user manual and firmware guide on the Creality Wiki under CR Series → CR-10 SE.
