**The Creality K2 Combo is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 260 × 260 × 260 mm build volume, bundled with one CFS (Creality Filament System) for four-colour printing.** Creality launched the K2 and K2 Combo on 25 August 2025, next to the K2 Pro. The Combo cost US$699 at launch and the printer alone US$549.

## Who the Creality K2 Combo suits

The K2 Combo suits makers who want an enclosed, fast multi-colour printer with a bed a little larger than the common 256 mm. It handles PLA, PETG, PET, ABS and PLA-CF. Buyers who need a heated chamber or nylon-based engineering filaments should look at the K2 Pro or K2 Plus.

## Creality K2 vs K2 Pro vs K2 Plus

| | K2 | K2 Pro | K2 Plus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Build volume | 260 × 260 × 260 mm | 300 × 300 × 300 mm | 350 × 350 × 350 mm |
| Max bed temperature | 100 °C | 110 °C | 120 °C |
| Chamber heating | No | Up to 60 °C | Up to 60 °C |
| Nozzle camera and auto flow | No | Yes | Yes |
| Ethernet | No | Yes | Yes |

## The CFS multi-colour system

- **Four spools** per CFS in a sealed box, read by RFID.
- **Auto refill** and tangle detection.
- **Up to four units** for 16 colours.
- **Not a dryer:** the original CFS stores filament; the CFS 2 adds drying. A review by Notebookcheck noted that the CFS does not feed TPU.

## First setup

From Creality's K2 user manual:

1. Remove the four bed locking screws with yellow labels.
2. Route the screen's ribbon cable through the top slot, connect it and slide the screen into place.
3. Connect the CFS.
4. Switch on and follow the start-up guide: language, network and time zone.
5. Wait for the automatic self-check, about 13 minutes, without touching the printer.
6. Load filament, set its details and start a print.

**Tip:** after any firmware update, run input shaping and auto levelling again, as Creality advises.

## Connecting the K2

The K2 joins 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and links to the Creality Cloud app for remote printing and monitoring. Creality Print can send jobs over the local network; the printer's IP address is under Settings → Network. You can also print from a USB drive. The base K2 has no Ethernet port.

## Manual, firmware and slicer

The user manual is on the Creality Wiki under K2 Flagship Series → K2, and firmware is also in Creality's Download Center. Update from the screen under Settings → Version Checking, or from a USB drive. The CFS updates through the printer; Creality advises connecting only one CFS during an online update.
