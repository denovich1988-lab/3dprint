**The Snapmaker U1 is a CoreXY 3D printer with four independent toolheads that swap automatically, giving four-colour or four-material prints with almost no purged filament.** Its build volume is 270 × 270 × 270 mm. Snapmaker launched it on Kickstarter in August 2025, where it raised over US$20 million from more than 20,000 backers, according to TechRadar.

## Who the Snapmaker U1 suits

The U1 suits makers who print in several colours or materials and dislike the waste of single-nozzle filament changers. Because each colour has its own toolhead, the printer only needs a small prime tower instead of purging at every change. A toolchanger also makes it practical to combine a model material with PVA soluble supports, or rigid and flexible filament in one part.

Snapmaker's "SnapSwap" system parks the four toolheads at the back of the frame. Reviewers measured a swap at roughly 10–12 seconds including priming.

Prices exclude shipping and taxes. The box includes the printer with four toolheads and four 500 g spools of PLA. A top cover, hardened steel hot ends, a PEI steel sheet and a filament dryer set were sold as add-ons.

## Filaments and the top cover

Out of the box Snapmaker lists PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA and PCTG. Fitting the optional top cover adds PET, ABS, ASA, nylon (PA) and PC. Carbon- and glass-fibre-filled filaments need both the top cover and hardened steel nozzles.

**Tip:** if you plan to print ABS or ASA, budget for the top cover from the start – the open top lets heat escape.

## Snapmaker U1 setup

Snapmaker's Support Center has a U1 "Getting Started" section with unboxing, assembly and initial setup guides. Setup involves clicking in the four toolheads and spool holders, fitting the feeding tubes and cables, removing the packing and bed screws, and running the automatic calibration, which measures the bed, input shaping, flow and the offset between toolheads. The slicer is a download; no USB drive with software is included.

## Connecting the U1 and software

- **Firmware:** based on Klipper.
- **Slicer:** Snapmaker Orca or OrcaSlicer for Windows, macOS and Linux.
- **App:** the Snapmaker App for iOS and Android, with the 2 MP chamber camera.
- **Offline:** print from a USB flash drive; Tom's Hardware also describes a LAN mode without the cloud.

## Snapmaker U1 manual

Snapmaker publishes the U1 guides in its Support Center under the U1 "Getting Started" section, and further articles in the U1 Knowledge Base on the Snapmaker Wiki, including a multi-colour printing guide.
