**The Creality Ender 3 Max is a large open-frame 3D printer with a 300 × 300 × 340 mm build volume – essentially a scaled-up Ender-3 with a 32-bit board, a Bowden extruder and manual four-knob bed levelling.** Creality released it in 2020 as the big member of the Ender-3 family, and the later Ender-3 Max Neo replaced it. This page covers specs, setup, levelling, connections and firmware.

## Ender 3 Max at a glance

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Build volume | 300 × 300 × 340 mm |
| Speed | Up to 180 mm/s, 30–60 mm/s typical |
| Hotend / bed | 250 °C / 100 °C |
| Bed surface | Textured carborundum glass |
| Levelling | Manual, four knobs (BL Touch optional) |
| Board | 32-bit 4.2.2 mainboard |
| Printing from | SD card or USB |
| Released | 2020 |

## Who the Ender 3 Max suits

It suits hobbyists who need a big bed for helmets, cosplay parts or long brackets on a budget and are happy to tune a classic Cartesian printer. It is less suited to beginners who want automatic levelling or fast printing – it has neither, and a large bed-slinger moves a heavy bed back and forth.

## Setting up the Ender 3 Max

Creality's manual describes a semi-assembled kit. At a high level:

1. Place the gantry frame on the base and fix it with the four long screws.
2. Mount the power supply and the screen, then fit the spool holder.
3. Connect the X, E and Z motors and the limit switches using the labelled cables.
4. Set the power supply switch to your mains voltage (115 or 230 V), then power on.
5. Level the bed with paper at the four corners and the centre.
6. Heat the nozzle and load 1.75 mm filament through the extruder lever.
7. Slice a model and print from the SD card.

## Bed levelling on the Ender 3 Max

Home the axes, disable the steppers and move the nozzle over each corner. Turn the knob under that corner until a sheet of A4 paper drags slightly between nozzle and glass. Go round twice, then check the centre. The large 300 mm bed needs this more often than a small one.

**Tip:** if you add a BL Touch probe, you also need firmware built for it.

## Ender 3 Max vs Ender 3 Max Neo

| | Ender 3 Max | Ender 3 Max Neo |
|---|---|---|
| Build volume | 300 × 300 × 340 mm | 300 × 300 × 320 mm |
| Z axis | Single lead screw | Dual lead screws |
| Levelling | Manual knobs | CR Touch automatic |
| Screen | LCD with knob | 4.3-inch colour screen |

See the [Ender 3 Max Neo manual](/printers/ender-3-max-neo-manual/) for the newer model.

## Connecting the Ender 3 Max

There is no Wi-Fi. You print from an **SD card**, or over a **USB cable** from a computer running a host program. Check a sliced file before a long print with our [G-code to STL tool](/gcode-to-stl/).

## Manual, firmware and slicer

Creality publishes the Ender-3 Max user manual and firmware in the Creality download centre on its website. The printer uses Creality firmware for the 4.2.2 board; there are separate files for the STM and GD chip versions, so check which chip your board has before flashing.

The manual lists Creality Slicer, Cura, Repetier-Host and Simplify3D as slicers. It reads STL, OBJ and AMF models – convert other files with our [OBJ to STL converter](/obj-to-stl/).
