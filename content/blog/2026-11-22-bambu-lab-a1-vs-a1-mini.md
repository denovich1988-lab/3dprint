**The Bambu Lab A1 and A1 mini are the same kind of printer in two sizes.** Both are open bed-slingers with automatic calibration, a 300 °C quick-swap hot end and AMS lite support. The real decision is how big your prints are.

Specifications below are from Bambu Lab’s published spec sheets.

## Side-by-side

| | A1 | A1 mini |
|---|---|---|
| Build volume | 256 × 256 × 256 mm | 180 × 180 × 180 mm |
| Max bed temperature | 100 °C | 80 °C |
| Max hot end temperature | 300 °C | 300 °C |
| Max speed / acceleration | 500 mm/s / 10,000 mm/s² | 500 mm/s / 10,000 mm/s² |
| Max hot end flow | 28 mm³/s | 28 mm³/s |
| Ideal materials | PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA | PLA, PETG, TPU, PVA |
| Launch price (US) | $399 ($559 with AMS lite) | $299 |

## How much bigger is the A1?

A 256 mm cube holds about 2.9 times the volume of a 180 mm cube. In practice, the A1 prints helmets, large planters and long brackets in one piece, while the A1 mini is best for parts up to about the size of a coffee mug.

## Check your models before you buy

Open a few models you want to print in our [STL viewer](/stl-viewer/) or [3D viewer](/3d-viewer/). The size readout shows the X, Y and Z dimensions in millimetres. If anything is over 180 mm, choose the A1.
