JPG to DDS converter

Turn JPG images into DDS textures with DXT1 or DXT5 compression and mipmaps.

Converted on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

How to use it

  1. Upload a JPG image.
  2. Choose DXT5, DXT1 or uncompressed, and whether to build mipmaps.
  3. Click “Convert to DDS” and download the texture.

About JPG to DDS conversion

What is a JPG file?

JPG is the common photo format. Photos work well for lithophanes; logos are cleaner as PNG or SVG. More about JPG.

What is a DDS file?

DDS is a texture format used by games. It stores GPU-compressed images (DXT/BC) and mipmaps so textures load fast.

What each format keeps

JPG

Pixels (lossy compression).

Used by: Cameras, phones, the web.

DDS

Compressed pixels, mipmaps, sometimes cube maps.

Used by: Game engines, modding tools for Skyrim, Fallout, Minecraft Bedrock, flight simulators.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no file limit set by us. Very large files are limited only by your device’s memory.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your web browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your file is read from your device and the result is created on your device, so nothing is sent to our server.

Which DDS compression should I choose?

DXT5 (BC3) keeps smooth transparency and suits most game textures. DXT1 (BC1) gives the smallest files but only on/off transparency. Uncompressed RGBA is exact but four to eight times larger.

Do I need mipmaps?

Most games expect mipmaps for textures shown at different distances; they reduce shimmering. UI images and icons usually do not need them.

Which DDS files can be opened?

DXT1, DXT3, DXT5 and uncompressed DDS files. BC7 files that use the newer DX10 header are not supported yet.
