JPG to STL converter

Turn a JPG photo into an STL. Lithophane mode makes a thin panel that shows the photo when lit from behind; relief mode makes a raised picture.

Processed on your device. Nothing is uploaded.

Style

How to use it

  1. Upload a JPG or PNG image.
  2. Choose relief, lithophane or logo/stamp and set width, base and height in millimetres. The 3D preview updates as you change settings.
  3. Click “Create STL” and download the file for your slicer.

About JPG to STL conversion

What is a JPG file?

JPG is the common photo format. Photos work well for lithophanes; logos are cleaner as PNG or SVG. More about JPG.

What is a STL file?

STL is the most common file for 3D printing. It stores a surface as a list of triangles and nothing else: no colours, no materials, no units. Every slicer can open it.

What each format keeps

JPG

Pixels (lossy compression).

Used by: Cameras, phones, the web.

STL

Triangle geometry only. Binary STL is about five times smaller than ASCII STL.

Used by: Every slicer (Bambu Studio, PrusaSlicer, OrcaSlicer, Cura), Thingiverse and Printables downloads, most CAD tools as an export.

Questions and answers

Is it free?

Yes. There is no sign-up, no watermark and no file limit set by us. Very large files are limited only by your device’s memory.

Are my files uploaded?

No. The conversion runs in your web browser with JavaScript and WebAssembly. Your file is read from your device and the result is created on your device, so nothing is sent to our server.

Which image works best?

Use a well-lit photo with clear contrast. Crop it to the shape you want before uploading. Faces and landscapes work well; very dark photos come out thick and dim.

What settings should I print a lithophane with?

Lithophanes are usually printed upright in white PLA with 100% infill and fine layers (0.12–0.16 mm) so the thickness differences show clearly. Check your slicer’s recommendations for your printer.

Can I change the size later?

Yes. Set the width here, or scale the STL in your slicer. Scaling in the slicer also scales the thickness, so set the thickness here for the best control.
