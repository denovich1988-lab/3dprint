Questions and answers

Is my3dprinter.io free?

Yes. Every viewer and converter is free to use, with no account and no watermark.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Models are opened and converted inside your browser using JavaScript and WebAssembly. The file never leaves your computer or phone, which also makes large files fast.

Which 3D formats can I open?

STL, 3MF, OBJ, GLB, glTF, FBX, PLY, DAE, 3DS, USDZ, OFF, AMF, VRML, STEP, STP, IGES, IGS and G-code, plus ZIP files that contain a model.

Which formats can I save?

STL (binary or ASCII), 3MF, OBJ, GLB, glTF, PLY, USDZ, DXF and OFF. Images can become STL, 3MF, OBJ or GLB, and textures can be converted between DDS, PNG and JPG.

Can I convert models from AI 3D generators?

Yes. Tools such as Meshy, Tripo and Rodin usually export GLB. Open the GLB here, check the size, and convert it to STL or 3MF for your slicer.
