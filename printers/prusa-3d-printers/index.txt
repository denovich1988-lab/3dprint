<p>On 13 August 2026 Prusa updated three models – the XL became the XL+, the CORE One L the CORE One L+ and the CORE One+ moved to Gen 2 – adding GT1.5 belts and a nozzle wiper.</p>
<h2>Prusa CORE One+</h2>
<p>The CORE One+ is Prusa&#39;s enclosed CoreXY all-rounder with an actively heated chamber. It comes as a kit or assembled and suits everyone from beginners to professionals who print ABS, ASA or other warp-prone materials.</p>
<h2>Prusa CORE One L+</h2>
<p>The larger CORE One L+ has a 300 × 300 × 330 mm build volume and a chamber heated up to 60 °C. It ships assembled and is aimed at workshops, small businesses and production work.</p>
<h2>Prusa XL+</h2>
<p>The XL+ is the biggest Prusa FDM printer, with a 360 mm cube build volume and an automatic toolchanger for up to five toolheads, so each material gets its own nozzle. Read our <a href="/printers/prusa-xl/">Prusa XL page</a> for specs and setup.</p>
<h2>Original Prusa MK4S</h2>
<p>The MK4S is the latest version of Prusa&#39;s classic open-frame i3 design, with a loadcell for automatic first-layer calibration. It is sold as a kit or assembled and replaced the MK4 in 2024 – see our <a href="/printers/prusa-mk4/">Prusa MK4 page</a>.</p>
<h2>Original Prusa MINI+</h2>
<p>The MINI+ is the compact, entry-level model with a 180 mm cube build volume. It suits beginners, small parts and print farms.</p>
<h2>Other Prusa printers</h2>
<p>Prusa also lists the Pro HT90 for industrial use, the SL1S SPEED resin printer and the Prusa Signature Oak in its printer range.</p>
<h2>Prusa software and connectivity</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>PrusaSlicer</strong> – the free slicer with profiles for every Prusa printer.</li>
<li><strong>PrusaLink</strong> – a local web interface built into newer printers, opened in a browser at the printer&#39;s IP address.</li>
<li><strong>Prusa Connect</strong> – Prusa&#39;s free cloud service for sending and monitoring prints remotely.</li>
<li><strong>Printables</strong> – Prusa&#39;s free model library.</li>
</ul>
<p>Prusa printers read STL, 3MF and STEP models in PrusaSlicer. Our <a href="/step-to-stl/">STEP to STL converter</a> helps if you need a mesh from a CAD file.</p>
<h2>How to choose a Prusa printer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Want an enclosed printer for ABS or ASA?</strong> CORE One+, or CORE One L+ for bigger parts.</li>
<li><strong>Want the classic open printer, possibly as a kit?</strong> MK4S.</li>
<li><strong>Need several materials in one print with little waste?</strong> XL+ with multiple toolheads.</li>
<li><strong>Short on space or budget?</strong> MINI+.</li>
</ol>
