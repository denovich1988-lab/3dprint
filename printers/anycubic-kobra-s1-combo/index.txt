27:Td25,<p><strong>The Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo 3D printer pairs the enclosed Kobra S1 CoreXY printer, with a 250 × 250 × 250 mm build volume, and the ACE Pro unit for four-colour printing.</strong> Anycubic showed it in December 2024 and opened orders in January 2025. Below are the official specs, the launch price, setup and how it connects.</p>
<h2>Who the Anycubic Kobra S1 suits</h2>
<p>The Kobra S1 suits buyers who want an affordable enclosed printer with multi-colour printing and filament drying in one box. It is a good fit for PLA and PETG multi-colour prints and for people who like to start and watch prints from an app. It ships assembled only, and its firmware is closed, so it is less suited to tinkerers.</p>
<h2>The ACE Pro unit</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Four spools</strong> per unit, with automatic refill when one runs out and anti-tangle detection.</li>
<li><strong>Drying while printing</strong>, so spools stay dry during long jobs.</li>
<li><strong>Two units</strong> give eight colours.</li>
<li><strong>ACE 2 Pro:</strong> Anycubic now sells the Kobra S1 with the newer ACE 2 Pro, which dries at up to 65 °C and links up to four units; it cannot be mixed with the original ACE Pro.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Kobra S1 speed and filaments</h2>
<p>Anycubic lists 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² as maximums, with 300 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² as recommended values. The 320 °C hotend and 120 °C bed cover PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA and TPU 95A. The enclosure is passive – it has no chamber heater – but a carbon filter is built in.</p>
<h2>First setup</h2>
<ol>
<li>Remove the packing and transport screws.</li>
<li>Connect the ACE Pro&#39;s PTFE tubes to the printer and plug in both power cords – the printer and the ACE Pro each need a socket.</li>
<li>Switch on and follow the touchscreen wizard; it runs LeviQ 3.0 levelling and flow calibration.</li>
<li>Bind the printer to the Anycubic app.</li>
<li>Load filament into the ACE Pro and match the slots in Anycubic Slicer Next.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Kobra S1</h2>
<p>The printer joins Wi-Fi and links to the Anycubic app and Anycubic Cloud for remote control and camera view. Anycubic Slicer Next can also send jobs in LAN mode, and a USB drive works for printing at the machine.</p>
<h2>Manual, firmware and slicer</h2>
<p>The quick start guide and firmware guide are on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D Printer → Kobra S1 Combo. Firmware updates install online from the touchscreen under Settings → Device → Printer Information; the ACE Pro has its own entry there. The slicer is Anycubic Slicer Next. Open a multi-colour model in our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a> before slicing.</p>
<h2>Compare the Kobra S1 Combo</h2>
<p>Its closest rivals are four-colour enclosed printers such as the <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-p2s/">Bambu Lab P2S</a> with <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-ams/">AMS</a> and the <a href="/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/">Elegoo Centauri Carbon</a>. Compare them in our <a href="/printers/compare/">printer comparison tool</a>.</p>
