Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo 3D printer – specs and setup

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Anycubic Kobra S1 is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 250 × 250 × 250 mm build volume; the Combo adds the ACE Pro four-spool unit.
  • Anycubic opened a founder's pre-sale in January 2025 at a launch price of US$549 against a regular US$749.
  • Anycubic lists 600 mm/s, 20,000 mm/s², a 320 °C nozzle and a 120 °C bed.

The Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo 3D printer pairs the enclosed Kobra S1 CoreXY printer, with a 250 × 250 × 250 mm build volume, and the ACE Pro unit for four-colour printing. Anycubic showed it in December 2024 and opened orders in January 2025. Below are the official specs, the launch price, setup and how it connects.

Who the Anycubic Kobra S1 suits

The Kobra S1 suits buyers who want an affordable enclosed printer with multi-colour printing and filament drying in one box. It is a good fit for PLA and PETG multi-colour prints and for people who like to start and watch prints from an app. It ships assembled only, and its firmware is closed, so it is less suited to tinkerers.

The ACE Pro unit

  • Four spools per unit, with automatic refill when one runs out and anti-tangle detection.
  • Drying while printing, so spools stay dry during long jobs.
  • Two units give eight colours.
  • ACE 2 Pro: Anycubic now sells the Kobra S1 with the newer ACE 2 Pro, which dries at up to 65 °C and links up to four units; it cannot be mixed with the original ACE Pro.

Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo launch price

Offer Price
Founder's pre-sale (2–5 Jan 2025, US) US$549
Regular price at launch (US) US$749

First US shipments were planned for February 2025.

Kobra S1 speed and filaments

Anycubic lists 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² as maximums, with 300 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² as recommended values. The 320 °C hotend and 120 °C bed cover PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA and TPU 95A. The enclosure is passive – it has no chamber heater – but a carbon filter is built in.

First setup

  1. Remove the packing and transport screws.
  2. Connect the ACE Pro's PTFE tubes to the printer and plug in both power cords – the printer and the ACE Pro each need a socket.
  3. Switch on and follow the touchscreen wizard; it runs LeviQ 3.0 levelling and flow calibration.
  4. Bind the printer to the Anycubic app.
  5. Load filament into the ACE Pro and match the slots in Anycubic Slicer Next.

Connecting the Kobra S1

The printer joins Wi-Fi and links to the Anycubic app and Anycubic Cloud for remote control and camera view. Anycubic Slicer Next can also send jobs in LAN mode, and a USB drive works for printing at the machine.

Manual, firmware and slicer

The quick start guide and firmware guide are on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D Printer → Kobra S1 Combo. Firmware updates install online from the touchscreen under Settings → Device → Printer Information; the ACE Pro has its own entry there. The slicer is Anycubic Slicer Next. Open a multi-colour model in our 3MF viewer before slicing.

Questions and answers

What is the Anycubic Kobra S1 Combo 3D printer?

It is the enclosed Kobra S1 CoreXY printer bundled with the ACE Pro, a four-spool unit that switches colours, refills automatically and dries filament while printing.

How many colours can the Kobra S1 print?

Four with one ACE Pro and eight with two. Anycubic now also sells a Kobra S1 bundle with the newer ACE 2 Pro, which links up to four units; ACE Pro and ACE 2 Pro units cannot be mixed.

Can the Anycubic Kobra S1 print TPU?

Yes, TPU 95A, but only in single-colour mode – not through the ACE multi-colour system.

How much did the Kobra S1 Combo cost at launch?

The founder's pre-sale from 2 to 5 January 2025 priced it at US$549, against a regular US$749.
