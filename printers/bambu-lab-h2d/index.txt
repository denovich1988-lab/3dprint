2c:Td7d,<p><strong>The Bambu H2D (Bambu Lab H2D) is an enclosed, dual-nozzle 3D printer with a 350 × 320 × 325 mm total build volume, a 350 °C hot end and an actively heated chamber up to 65 °C.</strong> Bambu Lab announced it on 25 March 2025, together with the AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT, as a &quot;personal manufacturing&quot; machine that can also laser-engrave, cut and draw.</p>
<h2>Who the Bambu Lab H2D suits</h2>
<p>The H2D suits people who need two materials in one print: a model with dissolvable or break-away support, a rigid part with a flexible section, or two engineering filaments. Because each material has its own nozzle, there is far less purging than with a single-nozzle AMS setup.</p>
<p>It is also for workshops that want one machine for printing and laser work. US launch prices from Bambu Lab&#39;s announcement:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>H2D</strong> – US$1,899</li>
<li><strong>H2D AMS Combo</strong> – US$2,199</li>
<li><strong>H2D Laser Full Combo 10 W</strong> – US$2,799</li>
<li><strong>H2D Laser Full Combo 40 W</strong> – US$3,499</li>
</ul>
<h2>Build volume with one or two nozzles</h2>
<p>The 350 mm width is the total travel. Printing with one nozzle gives 325 × 320 × 325 mm, and using both nozzles in the same print gives 300 × 320 × 325 mm. Bambu Lab&#39;s FAQ says both hot ends must use the same nozzle size, and an eddy-current sensor calibrates the offset between them.</p>

<ol>

<li>Mount the AMS 2 Pro on top, connect its PTFE tube (at least 600 mm) and the 6-pin cable.</li>
<li>Fit the spool holder and its PTFE tube.</li>
<li>Insert the safety key, connect power and switch on.</li>

<h2>Connecting the H2D</h2>
<p>The H2D connects over Wi-Fi to the Bambu Handy app and to <strong>Bambu Studio</strong>. It has a USB port instead of an SD card slot: use one USB 2.0 or newer drive, formatted FAT32 or exFAT. Bambu Lab notes that without a USB drive there is no LAN printing, no sending files from Bambu Studio and no time-lapse.</p>
<h2>Manual and firmware</h2>
<p>The official H2D manual pages, FAQ and maintenance guides are on the Bambu Lab Wiki under the H2 series manual pages. Update firmware from the touchscreen, the app or Bambu Studio.</p>
