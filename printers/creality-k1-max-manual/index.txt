2a:Td5c,<p><strong>Creality lists the official K1 Max manual in the Download Center on its website under the K1 Max page, and on the Creality Wiki under K1 Flagship Series → K1 Max → Quick Start Guide.</strong> The wiki also has a firmware upgrade guide for the printer. This page summarises the Creality K1 Max manual in our own words; it does not replace it.</p>
<h2>What the official K1 Max manual covers</h2>
<p>The user manual is organised around these topics:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Installing the product</strong> – unboxing, screen cable and spool holder.</li>
<li><strong>Power-on guide</strong> – language, transport screws, LiDAR check, network, time zone, cloud binding and self-test.</li>
<li><strong>Screen menus</strong> – Tune and Prepare, Files, extrude and retract, calibration and self-inspection.</li>
<li><strong>Printing</strong> – local printing, LAN printing from Creality Print and cloud printing.</li>
<li><strong>AI functions</strong> – camera and LiDAR features.</li>
<li><strong>Network</strong> – wired and Wi-Fi setup.</li>
<li><strong>Precautions, lubrication and maintenance.</strong></li>
</ol>
<h2>Quick-start summary</h2>
<p><strong>Unboxing.</strong> The K1 Max arrives almost fully assembled. Remove the foam, connect the touchscreen cable and fit the spool holder at the back.</p>
<p><strong>Power-on guide.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Switch on and choose a language.</li>
<li>Remove the three screws that lock the bed for shipping; the screen shows where they are.</li>
<li>Make sure nothing blocks the LiDAR&#39;s view.</li>
<li>Connect to Ethernet or 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi, set the time zone and bind the printer to Creality Cloud if you want remote access.</li>
<li>Let the self-test run automatic levelling and input shaping until it reports completion.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>First print.</strong> Load filament with the Extrude function, then print a file from the screen, send one over the LAN from Creality Print, or start one from Creality Cloud.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> reviewers found that the printer needed one Creality Cloud binding to unlock LAN and Wi-Fi printing, so do this during the power-on guide.</p>
<h2>Maintenance Creality recommends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Grease the parts marked in the manual every <strong>300 hours</strong> of printing.</li>
<li>Keep the area around the LiDAR clear, as the power-on guide asks.</li>
</ul>
