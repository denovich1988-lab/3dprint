Bambu Lab A1 – specs, setup, manual and A1 vs A1 mini

Updated November 22, 2026

Key points
  • The A1 is an open bed-slinger with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, launched in December 2023.
  • Bambu Lab lists a 300 °C hot end, 100 °C bed, 500 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² acceleration.
  • It works with the four-spool AMS lite for multi-colour printing.
Specifications (from the maker’s published spec sheet)

Multi-colourUp to 4 colours with the optional AMS lite

The Bambu Lab A1 is Bambu Lab’s full-size open printer. It moves the bed back and forth (a “bed-slinger”) rather than using a CoreXY frame, but adds the automation Bambu printers are known for: automatic bed levelling and Z-offset, vibration compensation, flow-rate compensation and motor-noise cancelling. Bambu Lab launched it in December 2023 at US$399, or US$559 with the AMS lite.

Who the A1 suits

It suits beginners and hobbyists who print mostly PLA and PETG and want a large build area without an enclosure. With the AMS lite it prints up to four colours. It is quieter than many printers thanks to active motor-noise cancellation.

Setup

The A1 ships partly assembled. Follow the quick-start guide in the box and on the Bambu Lab Wiki, run the on-screen calibration, then bind the printer in the Bambu Handy app. Send prints from Bambu Studio; Bambu Studio saves projects as 3MF.

Manual

Bambu Lab publishes the A1 user manual, maintenance guides and troubleshooting on its official wiki. We do not copy the manual here; use the official version so instructions match your firmware.

A1 or A1 mini?

Read our A1 vs A1 mini comparison. In short, choose the A1 if any part you plan to print is larger than 180 mm.

Questions and answers

Can the Bambu Lab A1 print ABS?

Bambu Lab lists PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA as ideal for the A1. It is an open printer, so warp-prone materials such as ABS and ASA are hard to print well; an enclosed printer like the P1S or P2S suits them better.

What is the difference between the A1 and the A1 mini?

Mainly size. The A1 prints up to 256 mm on each side and its bed reaches 100 °C; the A1 mini prints up to 180 mm and its bed reaches 80 °C. Speed, acceleration and hot-end temperature are listed the same.

Does the A1 work without the cloud?

Bambu Lab offers a LAN-only mode in addition to its cloud service, so you can send prints over your local network.
