Creality Hi – Hi Combo specs, CFS multi-colour and setup

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Creality Hi is an open bed-slinger with a die-cast aluminium frame, a 260 × 260 × 300 mm build volume and 500 mm/s top speed.
  • The Hi Combo adds Creality's four-spool CFS, and up to four CFS units give 16 colours, with RFID filament mapping and automatic refill.
  • Creality announced the Hi at CES in January 2025 at US$299 (Hi) and US$469 (Hi Combo) and put it on sale worldwide in March 2025.

The Creality Hi is an open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a die-cast aluminium frame, a 260 × 260 × 300 mm build volume and support for up to 16 colours through Creality's CFS filament system. Creality announced it at CES on 8 January 2025 at US$299 for the Hi and US$469 for the Hi Combo (US), and started worldwide sales on 11 March 2025. Creality pitches it at families and beginners who want multi-colour printing.

Creality Hi vs Hi Combo

The Hi is the printer on its own and prints one filament at a time. The Hi Combo bundles one four-spool Creality CFS. The CFS holds 1 kg spools, keeps them dry with desiccant and reads RFID tags on Creality filament, so colours are mapped automatically. When a spool runs out, the printer can switch to a matching spool and keep going.

Tip: if you start with the plain Hi, you can add a CFS later – the multi-colour upgrade is the same unit.

Who the Creality Hi suits

The Hi suits beginners and families who want an easy first printer with a path to multi-colour prints. It is open, so it is best for PLA, PETG and PLA-CF; for regular ABS or ASA an enclosed printer such as the Creality K2 Plus is a better fit.

Setting up the Creality Hi

Creality says the Hi arrives about 95% pre-assembled and takes around 8 minutes to build:

  1. Fit the gantry to the base with the supplied screws.
  2. Mount the CFS hub on the gantry leg and connect the cables (Combo only); Creality's unboxing video shows this step.
  3. Power on; automatic levelling and calibration run on first start.
  4. Load filament into the printer or the CFS and print a test file.

Connecting the Creality Hi

The Hi joins Wi-Fi and works with Creality Cloud for remote monitoring with the built-in camera. Files can also go on a USB drive, and the printer has 8 GB of internal storage. Check sliced files with our G-code to STL tool.

Creality Hi manual and firmware

Creality publishes the Hi Combo manual, firmware and an unbox-and-setup video on the Hi Combo support page, with more articles on the Creality Wiki. The printer runs Creality OS.

Questions and answers

What is the Creality Hi?

The Creality Hi is a beginner-friendly multi-colour bed-slinger printer with a die-cast metal frame, a 260 × 260 × 300 mm build volume and support for Creality's CFS filament system.

Does the Creality Hi use the CFS or the CFS Lite?

The Hi Combo ships with the standard four-spool CFS. Up to four CFS units can be connected for 16 colours. The CFS Lite is a different unit that Creality lists for the SparkX i7.

How much did the Creality Hi cost?

Creality announced it at CES 2025 at US$299 for the Hi and US$469 for the Hi Combo.

What slicer does the Creality Hi use?

Creality Print 5.1 or newer, which handles the CFS colour mapping and Wi-Fi printing.
