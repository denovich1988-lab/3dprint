<h2>Setting up the K1 SE</h2>
<p>At a high level, setup follows Creality&#39;s quick start and unbox video:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unbox and remove the foam, cable ties and the transport screws under the bed.</li>
<li>Check that the power supply setting matches your region.</li>
<li>Fit the spool holder and connect power.</li>
<li>Power on and run the self-check, which includes auto levelling and input shaping.</li>
<li>Connect to Wi-Fi on the touchscreen and load filament.</li>
<li>Slice in Creality Print and start the first print.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the K1 SE</h2>
<p>The K1 SE joins <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> or <strong>LAN</strong> and works with <strong>Creality Cloud</strong> and Creality Print for remote printing and monitoring. You can also print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>. It accepts STL, OBJ and 3MF files and has 8 GB of storage. Check a sliced job with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Manual and firmware</h2>
<p>Creality publishes the K1 SE user manual (in several languages) on the Creality Wiki under K1 Flagship Series → K1 SE, and firmware on the K1 SE support page. Rerun the self-check after a firmware update.</p>
