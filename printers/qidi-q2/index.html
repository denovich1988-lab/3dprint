QIDI Q2 – specs, heated chamber, QIDI Box and setup

Updated November 24, 2026

Key points
  • The QIDI Q2 is a compact enclosed CoreXY printer with a 270 × 270 × 256 mm build volume and active chamber heating to 65 °C.
  • It has a 370 °C nozzle and a load-cell in the hot end for hands-free levelling.
  • QIDI launched it on 20 August 2025 at launch prices of US$499, or US$649 with the QIDI Box.

The QIDI Q2 is a compact enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 270 × 270 × 256 mm build volume, a 370 °C nozzle and active chamber heating up to 65 °C. QIDI launched it on 20 August 2025 at launch prices of US$499 for the printer and US$649 for the Q2 Combo with the QIDI Box. It brings engineering-filament features to a home-sized printer.

Who the QIDI Q2 suits

The Q2 suits users who want to print nylon, polycarbonate and fibre-filled filaments at home without buying a large industrial machine. The heated chamber and 370 °C nozzle cover those materials, while the QIDI Box adds multicolour PLA and PETG work. QIDI's FAQ notes that about 300 mm/s is typical for fast PLA and ABS printing; 600 mm/s is the toolhead maximum.

Heated chamber and filtration

A second-generation PTC heater warms the chamber to 65 °C on its own, independent of the bed. A three-stage filter (G3 pre-filter, H12 HEPA and coconut-shell activated carbon) cleans the chamber air.

Why it matters: warm, stable chamber air reduces warping and layer splitting in ABS, ASA, PA and PC.

QIDI Box multicolour

The optional QIDI Box holds four spools and feeds them to the single nozzle. QIDI says up to four boxes can be chained for 16 materials, and tangle detection needs the box. QIDI filament is recognised by RFID; third-party spools are entered on the screen.

Setting up the QIDI Q2

QIDI's quick start guide lists these steps:

  1. Check the accessories and remove the foam around the toolhead.
  2. Undo the four transport screws holding the bed, let it rise, take out the foam under it and unlock the platform and toolhead.
  3. Plug in the screen cable and slide the screen into place; fit the spool holder arm at the back.
  4. Check the voltage for your region before powering on.
  5. Feed filament through the PTFE tube to the nozzle.
  6. Print the built-in test model. If it does not stick, adjust the Z-offset or the bed temperature.

Levelling and input shaping run at the first calibration and before each print, using the load cell in the hot end.

Connecting the QIDI Q2

Send files over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet from QIDI Studio, print from a USB drive, or use QIDI Cloud for remote access. The printer runs Klipper-based firmware, and the camera and web interface can be opened in a browser at the printer's IP address.

QIDI Q2 manual

QIDI publishes the Q2 guides on the QIDI Wiki under Q2, including unboxing, the quick start guide, firmware updates and release history. A USB drive in the box also holds QIDI Studio and the manuals, though QIDI notes these may be older versions.

Firmware and software

Update the firmware with the online update on the printer's touchscreen. Slice in QIDI Studio; QIDI also lists OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer and accepts STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP files. To convert a 3MF project for another slicer, use our 3MF to STL converter.

Questions and answers

Does the QIDI Q2 have a heated chamber?

Yes. QIDI lists active, independent chamber heating up to 65 °C, which helps with ABS, ASA, PA and PC.

Can the QIDI Q2 print in multiple colours?

Yes, with the optional QIDI Box, which holds four spools. QIDI says up to four boxes can be chained for 16 materials. The Q2 Combo bundles the printer with one box.

What slicer works with the QIDI Q2?

QIDI recommends QIDI Studio. QIDI also lists OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer as compatible.

How do I update the QIDI Q2 firmware?

Use the online update from the printer's touchscreen while it is connected to the internet. QIDI keeps the update guide and release history on the QIDI Wiki under Q2.
