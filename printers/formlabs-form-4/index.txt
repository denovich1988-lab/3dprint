<h2>Who the Form 4 suits</h2>
<p>The Form 4 suits businesses, labs and schools that print prototypes, jigs, dental models and end-use resin parts and want a managed workflow with cartridges, cloud monitoring and a wide materials library. At launch, Formlabs offered 23 resins for the Form 4 and 37 for the Form 4B. Hobbyists who print miniatures will find consumer resin printers such as the <a href="/printers/elegoo-mars-5-ultra/">Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra</a> much cheaper.</p>
<h2>Low Force Display explained</h2>
<p>Formlabs&#39; <strong>Light Processing Unit 4</strong> uses high-power LEDs and masking lenses to cure each layer almost at once through an LCD. A dual-layer flexible-film tank reduces the peel forces on each layer. Formlabs says this lets the Form 4 print at a similar speed regardless of how many parts fill the platform, and claims it is 2–5 times faster than the Form 3+ depending on material.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Form 4</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer and place it on a stable, level surface in a room at 18–28 °C.</li>
<li>Remove the packing material and follow the on-screen setup on the 7-inch touchscreen.</li>
<li>Insert the resin tank, build platform and a 1-litre <strong>Resin Cartridge V2</strong>.</li>
<li>Connect the printer to Wi-Fi or Ethernet and register it to your Formlabs Dashboard account.</li>
<li>Prepare a file in <strong>PreForm</strong> and send it to the printer. The printer heats the resin automatically before printing.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> the resin tank is rated for an estimated 75,000+ layers, but scratches or debris shorten its life – inspect it after any failed print.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Form 4</h2>
<p>The Form 4 supports <strong>Wi-Fi (2.4 and 5 GHz)</strong>, <strong>Gigabit Ethernet</strong> and <strong>USB-C</strong>. For remote printing, turn it on in Settings → Connectivity and register the printer to <strong>Dashboard</strong>, Formlabs&#39; cloud service, which passes jobs from PreForm to the printer and tracks progress.</p>
<h2>Post-processing: Form Wash and Form Cure</h2>
<p>Resin parts need washing and curing. Formlabs updated <strong>Form Wash</strong> alongside the Form 4 so it fits several build platform sizes, and the second-generation <strong>Form Cure</strong>, announced in March 2025, fits all Form 4 parts and heats to 100 °C.</p>
<h2>Form 4 manual</h2>
<p>Formlabs publishes the Form 4 manual on Formlabs Support under Manuals and documentation → Form 4 generation. The same section has the Form 4B manual and the Form 4 quick start guide as an article and a PDF in several languages. The Form 4L and Form 4BL manuals are under the Form 4L generation.</p>
<h2>Software</h2>
<p><strong>PreForm</strong> is Formlabs&#39; free print-preparation software; it orients models, generates supports and sends jobs. Before importing a model, you can inspect it in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> or convert an OBJ with our <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a>.</p>
