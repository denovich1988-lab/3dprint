<h2>QIDI Max4</h2>
<p>The <strong>Max4</strong> is QIDI&#39;s large-format model, with a 390 × 390 × 340 mm build volume, a PTC chamber heater up to 65 °C, a 3-in-1 filter (G3, H12 HEPA and activated carbon), load-cell auto levelling, a 1080p AI camera and a 5-inch touchscreen. It connects over dual-band Wi-Fi, Ethernet and USB.</p>
<p><strong>Why it matters:</strong> a heated chamber of this size is what large ABS, ASA and nylon parts need to avoid warping and layer splits.</p>
<h2>QIDI Plus5</h2>
<p>The <strong>Plus5</strong> sits in the middle with 320 × 320 × 300 mm, a 65 °C heated chamber and up to 16 colours when four QIDI Box units are chained. The older Plus4 (305 × 305 × 280 mm) is now listed as discontinued.</p>
<h2>QIDI Q2 and Q2C</h2>
<p>The <strong>Q2</strong> is the compact model with a 270 × 270 × 256 mm build volume, a PTC chamber heater up to 65 °C and a 3-in-1 filter. The <strong>Q2C</strong> shares the size and speed but has no chamber heater, and the filter is optional – a cheaper choice if you print mainly PLA and PETG. Read our <a href="/printers/qidi-q2/">QIDI Q2 page</a> for the full specs.</p>

<h2>How to choose a QIDI printer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Mostly PLA and PETG, small parts:</strong> the Q2C.</li>
<li><strong>Engineering filaments in a compact size:</strong> the Q2, for its heated chamber and filter.</li>
<li><strong>Bigger parts and more colours:</strong> the Plus5.</li>
<li><strong>Largest parts:</strong> the Max4.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> if you want multicolour later, check that your model supports the QIDI Box – the Q1 Pro and X-Max 3 do not.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>QIDI printers run a customised version of <strong>Klipper</strong> with QIDI&#39;s Moonraker and Fluidd builds. QIDI advises against updating or modifying Klipper yourself, because changes can stop the printer working. Updates depend on the model:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Max4 and Q2:</strong> update online from the printer screen.</li>
<li><strong>Plus4:</strong> online from version 1.4.0; earlier versions need an offline update from a USB drive.</li>
<li><strong>X-Max 3, X-Plus 3, Q1 Pro and X-Smart 3:</strong> download releases from QIDI&#39;s GitHub.</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not downgrade firmware on any model. QIDI&#39;s own slicer is <strong>QIDI Studio</strong>; OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer are listed as alternatives. Multicolour projects usually come as 3MF files – preview them with our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
<h2>QIDI manuals and support</h2>
<p>QIDI publishes manuals, firmware release histories and QIDI Studio release notes on the official QIDI Wiki, linked from the Support menu of its website. Firmware downloads are on the Firmware Download page of the QIDI site.</p>
