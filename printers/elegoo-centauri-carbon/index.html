Elegoo Centauri Carbon – specs, setup and who it suits

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Elegoo Centauri Carbon is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume.
  • Elegoo opened pre-orders on 17 February 2025 at a launch price of US$299.99.
  • Elegoo lists a 320 °C nozzle, a 110 °C bed, 500 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration.

The Elegoo Centauri Carbon is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, a 320 °C hardened steel nozzle and a chamber camera. Elegoo announced it on 17 February 2025 as its first CoreXY printer, at a launch price of US$299.99. It is the first model of the line; the Centauri Carbon 2 followed later.

Who the Elegoo Centauri Carbon suits

The Centauri Carbon suits buyers on a budget who want an enclosed, fast printer. The tempered-glass door and top cover keep heat in for ABS and ASA, and the hardened steel nozzle handles carbon-fibre-filled filaments. Reviewers also pointed to classrooms and small print farms, since the printer runs well over the local network.

Features worth knowing

  • Auto levelling: four pressure sensors under the bed, plus automatic Z-offset.
  • Calibration: vibration compensation and pressure advance calibration.
  • Filament handling: a built-in filament cutter, automatic unloading and a run-out sensor.
  • Multi-colour: the CANVAS add-on gives four-colour printing on this printer only.

First setup

The setup follows Elegoo's included guide:

  1. Remove the bed lock screws and the transport packing.
  2. Connect the touchscreen ribbon cable and seat the screen.
  3. Rotate the spool holder into place and feed filament through the run-out detector.
  4. Switch on and pick your language.
  5. Check that the build plate's side A faces up.
  6. Let the self-check run; it takes about 30 minutes.

Tip: do not interrupt the first self-check – it measures the bed and tunes vibration compensation for later prints.

Connecting the Centauri Carbon

Join Wi-Fi under Settings → Network on the touchscreen. You can then send prints from ElegooSlicer over the local network, or open the printer's web interface by its IP address. The supplied USB drive holds the slicer installers and sample models, and you can print from it at the printer. Firmware updates arrive over the air.

Manual and software

Elegoo ships a printed user guide and a fuller user manual in the box. Online help is on the Elegoo Wiki, which has a Centauri Carbon section with tutorials such as multi-colour printing with CANVAS. The slicer is ElegooSlicer; OrcaSlicer profiles are available too. Check a model before slicing with our STL viewer.

Questions and answers

How much did the Elegoo Centauri Carbon cost at launch?

Elegoo opened pre-orders on 17 February 2025 at US$299.99, £299.99 or €329.99, excluding shipping.

Can the Elegoo Centauri Carbon print carbon fibre?

Yes. It has a hardened steel nozzle, and Elegoo lists carbon-fibre-filled filaments alongside PLA, PETG, TPU, ABS and ASA.

Does the Centauri Carbon print in multiple colours?

Not on its own. Elegoo sells the CANVAS add-on, made only for the Centauri Carbon, which adds four-colour printing.

Which slicer does the Elegoo Centauri Carbon use?

ElegooSlicer, Elegoo's open-source slicer based on OrcaSlicer. Elegoo also provides a profile for OrcaSlicer.
