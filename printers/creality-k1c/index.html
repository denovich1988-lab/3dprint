Creality K1C 3D printer – specs, setup and who it suits

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Creality K1C is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume and a built-in AI camera.
  • Creality opened pre-orders in January 2024 at US$559.
  • It adds a quick-swap hardened-steel-tip nozzle and a carbon air filter to the K1 design, for carbon-fibre filaments.

The Creality K1C 3D printer is an enclosed CoreXY machine with a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume, a quick-swap hardened-steel-tip nozzle and a built-in AI camera. Creality opened pre-orders in January 2024 at US$559. It builds on the K1 with changes aimed at carbon-fibre-filled filaments.

Who the Creality K1C suits

The K1C suits makers who want an enclosed, fast printer for PLA and PETG that can also handle ABS, ASA and carbon-fibre-filled PLA, PA and PET. Creality says the typical print speed is 300 mm/s and that silent mode runs at 45 dB or less at one metre. Buyers who need a larger bed or multi-colour printing should look at the CFS-equipped Creality K2 Combo.

Setting up the Creality K1C

Creality's user manual describes these steps:

  1. Remove the packaging, the grey foam and the foam holding the extruder.
  2. Fit the door handle and the air filter cartridge.
  3. Connect the touchscreen's flat cable and snap the screen into place.
  4. Fit the material rack, load filament into the PTFE tube and install the top cover.
  5. Set the voltage switch (115 V or 230 V) to match your mains before plugging in.
  6. Switch on, accept the privacy policy, join your network, choose a time zone and bind the printer in the Creality Cloud app.
  7. Let the self-check run for about 11 minutes without touching the printer.

Tip: for low-temperature or flexible filaments, Creality advises removing the top cover if the chamber goes above 35 °C.

Connecting the Creality K1C

Print from a USB drive, over LAN from Creality Print (add the printer by scanning or by IP address), or remotely through Creality Cloud. The AI camera sends failure alerts and records time-lapses; active alerts need the Creality Print or Creality Cloud app.

Creality K1C manual

Creality publishes the K1C user manual and maintenance tips on the Creality Wiki under K1 Flagship Series → K1C. The manual recommends recalibrating the platform after 300 hours of printing or after replacing the platform or nozzle, and lubricating the X, Y and Z axes as routine maintenance.

Firmware and software

The K1C runs Creality OS, based on Klipper. For the K1 series, Creality offers firmware updates over the air, through the Creality Cloud app, or from a USB drive; the printer reboots several times and you should run the self-tests before printing again. Slice in Creality Print, which accepts STL, OBJ and 3MF files. Check a 3MF project first in our 3MF viewer.

Questions and answers

What is the build volume of the Creality K1C 3D printer?

220 × 220 × 250 mm, the same as the K1.

What is the difference between the Creality K1 and K1C?

The K1C adds support for carbon-fibre filaments (PLA-CF, PA-CF, PET-CF), a quick-swap tri-metal nozzle with a hardened steel tip, an all-metal extruder, an AI camera as standard, an air filter and a PEI plate. Speed and build volume are the same.

Can the Creality K1C print carbon fibre?

Yes. Creality lists PLA-CF, PA-CF and PET-CF, and the nozzle has a hardened steel tip.

How do I update the Creality K1C firmware?

For the K1 series, Creality offers updates over the air when the printer is online, through the Creality Cloud app, or from a USB drive with firmware downloaded from Creality's website.
