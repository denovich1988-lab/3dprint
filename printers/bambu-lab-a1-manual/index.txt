This page summarises the Bambu Lab A1 manual in our own words.</p>
<h2>What the official A1 manual covers</h2>
<p>The A1 → Manual section has three parts:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Introduction</strong> – an overview of the A1, the quick start guide PDF, and how filament tangle monitoring, nozzle clumping detection, build plate detection and air-printing detection work.</li>
<li><strong>Setting up</strong> – unboxing the A1 Combo, the packing list notes, the AMS lite top mount guide, and the first print with an AMS lite or an external spool.</li>
<li><strong>Appendix</strong> – the A1 FAQ.</li>
</ol>
<p>The A1 has a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, a 300 °C hotend and a 100 °C bed. More on our <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-a1/">Bambu Lab A1</a> page.</p>
<h2>Bambu Lab A1 quick start</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Unpack carefully:</strong> lift by the Z-axis bracket and the base, never by the X axis or the heatbed.</li>
<li><strong>Assemble:</strong> fix the base, the AMS lite stand and the purge wiper with the supplied screws, and fit the spool holder if you use an external spool.</li>
<li><strong>Connect the AMS lite:</strong> short PTFE tubes to ports 1–2, long ones to 3–4, then plug in the 4-pin cable with room for the bed to move.</li>
<li><strong>Power on:</strong> fit a clean build plate, switch on and follow the on-screen initialisation and calibration.</li>
<li><strong>Bind:</strong> link the printer to Bambu Handy.</li>
<li><strong>First print:</strong> load filament, slice in Bambu Studio and print.</li>
</ol>
<p>The A1 needs about 695 mm of front-to-back space for the moving bed. Read more about the filament changer on our <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-ams-lite/">AMS lite</a> page.</p>

<h2>Common A1 problems and Bambu&#39;s fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Homing or levelling fails:</strong> clean the nozzle, make sure the hotend screws are tight and the plate is clear, then clean and grease the Z lead screws. The Wiki lists fixes by HMS error code.</li>
<li><strong>False touches in mid-air:</strong> update to firmware 1.03 or later and straighten bent PTFE tubes at the hub.</li>
<li><strong>Heatbed not heating or showing 0 °C:</strong> check the thermistor plug under the base, then follow the Wiki&#39;s measurement steps.</li>
<li><strong>Poor first layer:</strong> run auto bed levelling again with the plate you print on.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> ABS, ASA, PC and PA are only suited to small, low-infill parts on the open A1, with good ventilation.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>When the A1 is online, it shows an update pop-up; Bambu Handy can also update or roll back firmware. For offline updates, copy the package unchanged to the microSD card (firmware 01.04.00.00 or later) and do not switch off during the roughly 20-minute install. Slice in Bambu Studio, and check a project first with our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
