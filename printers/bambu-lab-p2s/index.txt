2b:T852,<p>The <strong>Bambu Lab P2S</strong> is the second generation of Bambu Lab’s enclosed P-series. Bambu Lab announced it on 14 October 2025 and describes it as a completely re-engineered P1 that borrows technology from the larger H2D and H2S.</p>
<h2>What is new compared with the P1S</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Screen and interface.</strong> A 5-inch touchscreen with Bambu Lab’s second-generation interface replaces the P1S’s small button-driven screen.</li>
<li><strong>Extruder.</strong> A PMSM servo extruder with up to 8.5 kg of force, which Bambu Lab says is 70% more than its predecessor. It checks resistance and position at 20 kHz to spot grinding and clogs.</li>
<li><strong>Calibration.</strong> Auto Flow Dynamics Calibration uses an eddy-current sensor.</li>
<li><strong>Cooling.</strong> Adaptive Airflow pulls cool outside air in so PLA and PETG can print with the door closed, and a heat-keeping mode for ABS and ASA.</li>
<li><strong>Camera and AI.</strong> A 1080p live-view camera with AI detection of spaghetti failures, nozzle blobs and air printing, plus a check that the plate and nozzle match the slicer settings.</li>
<li><strong>Maintenance.</strong> A quick-swap hot end that releases the nozzle and heatsink with one clip.</li>
<li><strong>Bed.</strong> Up to 110 °C, versus 100 °C on the P1S.</li>
</ul>
<p>The build volume stays at 256 × 256 × 256 mm. Bambu Lab says the P1S remains on sale and supported.</p>
<h2>The Combo and AMS 2 Pro</h2>
<p>The P2S Combo ships with the <strong>AMS 2 Pro</strong>, which feeds up to four spools and dries filament with active venting. Bambu Lab says this dries filament 30% faster than sealed heating.</p>
<h2>Setup and manual</h2>
<p>The P2S arrives mostly assembled. Follow Bambu Lab’s quick-start guide and the on-screen self-test, then bind the printer to the Bambu Handy app and send prints from Bambu Studio. The official manual, FAQ and maintenance pages are on the Bambu Lab Wiki.</p>
