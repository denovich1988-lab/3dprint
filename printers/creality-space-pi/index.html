Creality Space Pi filament dryer – SpacePi, Plus and X4

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • Creality Space Pi is Creality's line of filament dryers – the one-spool SpacePi, the two-spool SpacePi Plus and the four-spool SpacePi X4.
  • The SpacePi and SpacePi Plus heat from 45 to 70 °C; the X4 reaches 85 °C in two independent chambers and adds active dehumidification.
  • All three have a 1–48 hour timer, and you can print straight from the dryer by feeding filament through an outlet.

The Creality Space Pi is a family of filament dryers – the one-spool SpacePi, the two-spool SpacePi Plus and the four-spool SpacePi X4 – that heat filament to drive out moisture before and during printing. Damp filament causes stringing, bubbles and weak, rough parts, especially with PETG, TPU and nylon. This page compares the three models, explains setup and gives practical drying tips.

SpacePi and SpacePi Plus

The SpacePi and the SpacePi Plus share the same 45–70 °C range, 1–48 hour timer and basic material presets. The Plus simply takes two spools instead of one. Both take 1.75 mm and 2.85 mm filament. They cover the common drying temperatures for PLA, PETG and TPU.

Tip: if you mostly print one material at a time, the single SpacePi is enough; the Plus is useful for two-colour work or keeping a second spool ready.

SpacePi X4

The SpacePi X4 is the flagship. Each of its two chambers has its own 200 W PTC heater and reaches 85 °C, so it can dry higher-temperature materials such as nylon. Creality lists:

  • built-in desiccant that does not need replacing, a sealing ring and a one-way valve;
  • a 3.2-inch touchscreen with an auto drying mode and three custom "My Drying" slots;
  • silent mode, a beep when drying ends and automatic LED lights;
  • support for 1 kg and 2 kg spools up to 200 mm in diameter and 150 mm wide;
  • firmware updates by TF card.

Third-party coverage reported the X4 shipping in July 2025 at a US$199 launch price (US).

Setting up a Space Pi dryer

Creality's guide for the Space Pi Plus describes the same basic steps for all models:

  1. Place the dryer on a flat, stable surface with room for airflow and for the lid to open.
  2. Put the spool or spools on the rollers.
  3. Feed the filament out through an outlet, adding a PTFE tube if you print straight from the dryer.
  4. Close the lid and power on.
  5. Choose a material preset, or set the temperature and time manually, and start.

Drying tips

Start below the filament's glass-transition temperature – PLA softens at much lower temperatures than nylon, and an over-hot spool can fuse its windings. Dry for several hours rather than turning the heat up. For typical temperatures by material, see our filament dryer guide, and for material-specific advice read PETG vs PLA and our TPU filament guide.

Questions and answers

What temperature does the Creality Space Pi reach?

The SpacePi and SpacePi Plus heat from 45 to 70 °C. The SpacePi X4 goes up to 85 °C in each of its two chambers, according to Creality's comparison page.

How many spools does the Creality Space Pi hold?

The SpacePi holds one spool, the SpacePi Plus two and the SpacePi X4 four, in two chambers of two. Two X4 units can be stacked for eight spools.

Can I print directly from the Creality Space Pi?

Yes. Feed the filament out through the outlet, add a PTFE tube to the printer and keep the dryer running while you print.
