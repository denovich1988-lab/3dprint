Sovol SV08 – specs, setup, Klipper and the Voron-style design

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Sovol SV08 is an open-frame CoreXY printer based on the Voron 2.4 design, with a 350 × 350 × 345 mm build volume and quad gantry levelling.
  • Sovol lists 700 mm/s top speed, 40,000 mm/s² acceleration, up to 30 mm³/s flow and a 300 °C nozzle.
  • It runs Klipper, is fully open source, ships mostly assembled and was announced in April 2024 with a launch price from US$499.

The Sovol SV08 is an open-frame CoreXY 3D printer based on the Voron 2.4, with a 350 × 350 × 345 mm build volume, quad gantry levelling and Klipper firmware. Sovol announced it in April 2024 with a launch price from US$499 (US) and ships it mostly assembled, which makes it a lower-effort way into a Voron-style machine. This page covers its official specs, setup, connection, firmware and the larger SV08 Max.

Who the Sovol SV08 suits

The SV08 suits makers who want a large, fast CoreXY printer they can modify, without sourcing and building a full Voron kit. Hardware and software are open source, so mods and replacement parts from the Voron community often fit. It is less suited to someone who wants a closed, app-driven experience or to print ABS without buying the optional enclosure – compare it with the Voron 2.4 it is based on.

Quad gantry levelling explained

Instead of a bed that moves on one or two lead screws, the SV08's bed is fixed and the whole gantry rides on four independent Z motors. Before a print the printer probes the four corners and tilts the gantry until it is parallel with the bed, then builds a mesh of the plate surface.

Tip: run quad gantry levelling and the bed mesh with the bed at printing temperature – the plate shape changes as it heats.

Setting up the SV08

Sovol ships the SV08 as a partly assembled kit and quotes about an hour to finish it. The high-level steps are:

  1. Mount the gantry assembly on the base frame and connect the labelled cables.
  2. Fit the screen, spool holder and filament path.
  3. Set the power supply's voltage switch to match your local mains before switching on.
  4. Run the calibration routine: quad gantry levelling, bed mesh, Z offset and input shaping.
  5. Load filament and print a test file.

Connecting the Sovol SV08

The SV08 connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Once it is on your network you open its Klipper web interface in a browser to upload G-code, watch the built-in camera and change settings. USB drives also work for files. Check a sliced file before printing with our G-code to STL tool.

Sovol SV08 manual and firmware

Sovol publishes the SV08 user manual, firmware images and printable parts on the Sovol Wiki (SV08 section) and its download page. Because the machine runs Klipper, its configuration lives in plain text files you can edit from the web interface; follow the wiki for firmware updates, since the method depends on the image version.

Slicers

Sovol lists OrcaSlicer, Cura, PrusaSlicer and Simplify3D. OrcaSlicer includes an SV08 printer profile, which is the quickest start. You can open 3MF projects from other slicers with our 3MF to STL converter.

Sovol SV08 Max

The SV08 Max scales the design up to a 500 × 500 × 500 mm build volume, keeps CoreXY motion, 40,000 mm/s² acceleration and quad gantry levelling, and adds eddy-current bed levelling. Sovol launched it through Kickstarter.

Questions and answers

Is the Sovol SV08 a Voron?

It is based on the open-source Voron 2.4 design – CoreXY motion with quad gantry levelling and four Z motors – but it is Sovol's own product that ships mostly assembled rather than as a self-sourced kit.

What firmware does the Sovol SV08 use?

Klipper. The printer is controlled through a web interface on your network, and Sovol publishes its firmware and configuration on the Sovol wiki.

What is the Sovol SV08 build volume?

350 × 350 × 345 mm according to Sovol. The SV08 Max raises this to 500 × 500 × 500 mm.

Does the Sovol SV08 have an enclosure?

No. It is an open-frame printer; Sovol sells an enclosure as an optional add-on, which helps with ABS.
