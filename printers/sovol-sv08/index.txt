<h2>Who the Sovol SV08 suits</h2>
<p>The SV08 suits makers who want a large, fast CoreXY printer they can modify, without sourcing and building a full Voron kit. Hardware and software are open source, so mods and replacement parts from the Voron community often fit. It is less suited to someone who wants a closed, app-driven experience or to print ABS without buying the optional enclosure – compare it with the <a href="/printers/voron-2-4/">Voron 2.4</a> it is based on.</p>
<h2>Quad gantry levelling explained</h2>
<p>Instead of a bed that moves on one or two lead screws, the SV08&#39;s bed is fixed and the whole gantry rides on <strong>four independent Z motors</strong>. Before a print the printer probes the four corners and tilts the gantry until it is parallel with the bed, then builds a mesh of the plate surface.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> run quad gantry levelling and the bed mesh with the bed at printing temperature – the plate shape changes as it heats.</p>
<h2>Setting up the SV08</h2>
<p>Sovol ships the SV08 as a partly assembled kit and quotes about an hour to finish it. The high-level steps are:</p>
<ol>
<li>Mount the gantry assembly on the base frame and connect the labelled cables.</li>
<li>Fit the screen, spool holder and filament path.</li>
<li>Set the power supply&#39;s voltage switch to match your local mains before switching on.</li>
<li>Run the calibration routine: quad gantry levelling, bed mesh, Z offset and input shaping.</li>
<li>Load filament and print a test file.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Sovol SV08</h2>
<p>The SV08 connects over <strong>2.4 GHz Wi-Fi</strong> or <strong>Ethernet</strong>. Once it is on your network you open its Klipper web interface in a browser to upload G-code, watch the built-in camera and change settings. USB drives also work for files. Check a sliced file before printing with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Sovol SV08 manual and firmware</h2>
<p>Sovol publishes the SV08 user manual, firmware images and printable parts on the Sovol Wiki (SV08 section) and its download page. Because the machine runs Klipper, its configuration lives in plain text files you can edit from the web interface; follow the wiki for firmware updates, since the method depends on the image version.</p>
<h2>Slicers</h2>
<p>Sovol lists <strong>OrcaSlicer</strong>, Cura, PrusaSlicer and Simplify3D. OrcaSlicer includes an SV08 printer profile, which is the quickest start. You can open 3MF projects from other slicers with our <a href="/3mf-to-stl/">3MF to STL converter</a>.</p>
<h2>Sovol SV08 Max</h2>
<p>The SV08 Max scales the design up to a 500 × 500 × 500 mm build volume, keeps CoreXY motion, 40,000 mm/s² acceleration and quad gantry levelling, and adds eddy-current bed levelling. Sovol launched it through Kickstarter.</p>
