<h2>IDEX print modes</h2>
<p>Because each head moves on its own, the Creator Pro 2 can:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Duplicate</strong> – print two copies of one part at the same time.</li>
<li><strong>Mirror</strong> – print a part and its mirror image, handy for left and right pieces.</li>
<li><strong>Dual material</strong> – print a second colour, or soluble PVA supports, while the idle head parks out of the way.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> PVA supports dissolve in water, which makes complex parts with internal overhangs much easier. Keep PVA dry – see our <a href="/blog/filament-dryer/">filament dryer guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Who the Creator Pro 2 suits</h2>
<p>It suits schools, labs and makers who want a small, enclosed printer for ABS and two-material prints. The bed is small and there is no Wi-Fi or automatic levelling, so users who want big, fast, networked printing should look at newer models in our <a href="/printers/flashforge-3d-printers/">Flashforge 3D printers guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Creator Pro 2</h2>
<p>At a high level, following Flashforge&#39;s quick start guide:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer and remove the packing material from the chamber.</li>
<li>Fit the spool holders and load filament into each extruder.</li>
<li>Level the bed with the knobs underneath (reviewers describe a manual three-point system).</li>
<li>Run Flashforge&#39;s nozzle and X/Y calibration so both heads line up.</li>
<li>Print a test file from the SD card.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting and slicing</h2>
<p>The Creator Pro 2 prints from an <strong>SD card</strong> or over a <strong>USB cable</strong>; there is no Wi-Fi. <strong>FlashPrint</strong> is the official slicer and handles IDEX modes, and Flashforge publishes a Cura setup guide too. Check a model before slicing in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a>.</p>
<h2>The original Flashforge Creator Pro</h2>
<p>The first Creator Pro is an enclosed printer with two extruders on one carriage and a 227 × 148 × 150 mm build volume. It has a 240 °C nozzle limit, a heated bed, a button-controlled LCD and USB and SD card printing, and it weighs 14.8 kg. Flashforge says its Creator series began in 2013.</p>
<h2>Manuals and firmware</h2>
<p>Flashforge publishes the Creator Pro 2 user manual, quick start guide, spare parts list, Cura setup guide and firmware on its website, under Download Center → Creator Series. The original Creator Pro is not listed there.</p>
<h2>Compare the Creator Pro</h2>
<p>Flashforge also makes a larger IDEX model, the Creator 3 Pro (300 × 250 × 200 mm). See its other printers in our <a href="/printers/flashforge-3d-printers/">Flashforge 3D printers guide</a>, or use the <a href="/printers/compare/">printer comparison tool</a>.</p>
