Prusa MK4 – specs, MK4S changes, setup and manual

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Prusa MK4 is an open-frame printer with a 250 × 210 × 220 mm build volume, announced on 29 March 2023.
  • Its loadcell sensor in the Nextruder sets the first layer automatically, with no manual Live Z.
  • Prusa replaced it with the MK4S in August 2024 and sells an MK4 to MK4S upgrade kit.

Power240 W PSU; about 80 W printing PLA, 120 W printing ABS

The Prusa MK4 is an open-frame 3D printer with a 250 × 210 × 220 mm build volume and a loadcell in its Nextruder that sets the first layer automatically. Prusa Research announced the Original Prusa MK4 on 29 March 2023 and replaced it with the Prusa MK4S in August 2024.

Who the Prusa MK4 suits

The MK4 and MK4S suit beginners who want a reliable printer and experienced users who want an open, repairable machine. You can buy it as a kit to learn how it works, or assembled. As an open-frame printer it is best with PLA, PETG and flexible filaments; Prusa recommends its optional enclosure for ABS, ASA and nylon.

What makes the MK4 different

  • Loadcell sensor – the nozzle itself measures the bed, so there is no Live Z adjustment for a good first layer.
  • Nextruder – a direct-drive extruder with a 10:1 planetary gearbox and quick-swap nozzles.
  • 32-bit electronics with Input Shaper and Pressure Advance for faster printing.
  • Optional MMU3 for printing with up to five materials.

US launch prices were US$1,099 assembled and US$799 as a kit.

Prusa MK4 vs MK4S

Prusa announced the MK4S on 12 August 2024. It keeps the frame, build volume and loadcell, and adds:

  • 360° part cooling with a turbine fan;
  • a high-flow CHT 0.4 mm nozzle, which Prusa says gives 45–114% more flow depending on material;
  • tuned speed profiles;
  • Wi-Fi setup by NFC and support for the Prusa app.

MK4 owners can install the MK4 to MK4S upgrade kit themselves (US$99 at launch); Prusa's Knowledge Base has an eight-chapter guide.

First setup

  1. Unbox the assembled printer, or build the kit with the online assembly guide (nine chapters for the MK4S).
  2. Mount the spool and load filament.
  3. Run the Selftest wizard; on an assembled MK4S Prusa says this takes about 10 minutes.
  4. Connect to Wi-Fi from the touchscreen, by NFC or the app, or plug in Ethernet.
  5. In PrusaSlicer, run the Configuration Wizard, pick the printer and nozzle, slice and send your first print.

Connecting the MK4

PrusaLink runs on the printer and opens in a browser at its IP address, without internet. Prusa Connect is Prusa's cloud service for remote printing and monitoring. You can also print from a USB drive. Slice in PrusaSlicer, which reads STL, 3MF and STEP files. To convert a CAD file first, use our STEP to STL converter.

Manual and firmware

Prusa publishes the MK4 and MK4S handbooks as PDFs in the Prusa Knowledge Base under Firmware & Downloads, together with the assembly guides and the latest firmware.

Questions and answers

What is the Prusa MK4 build volume?

Prusa lists 250 × 210 × 220 mm for the MK4 and the MK4S.

Is the Prusa MK4 discontinued?

The MK4 was replaced by the MK4S in August 2024; unshipped MK4 orders were converted to MK4S. Prusa sells an MK4 to MK4S upgrade kit for existing owners.

What is the difference between the Prusa MK4 and MK4S?

The MK4S adds 360° cooling with a turbine fan, a high-flow CHT nozzle, tuned speed profiles, NFC Wi-Fi setup and app support. Build volume and the loadcell sensor stay the same.

Is the Prusa MK4 a kit?

Both. The MK4 and MK4S are sold as a self-assembly kit or fully assembled.
