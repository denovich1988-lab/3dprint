2b:Tc6c,<p><strong>The Prusa MK4 is an open-frame 3D printer with a 250 × 210 × 220 mm build volume and a loadcell in its Nextruder that sets the first layer automatically.</strong> Prusa Research announced the Original Prusa MK4 on 29 March 2023 and replaced it with the Prusa MK4S in August 2024.</p>
<h2>Who the Prusa MK4 suits</h2>
<p>The MK4 and MK4S suit beginners who want a reliable printer and experienced users who want an open, repairable machine. You can buy it as a kit to learn how it works, or assembled. As an open-frame printer it is best with PLA, PETG and flexible filaments; Prusa recommends its optional enclosure for ABS, ASA and nylon.</p>
<h2>What makes the MK4 different</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Loadcell sensor</strong> – the nozzle itself measures the bed, so there is no Live Z adjustment for a good first layer.</li>
<li><strong>Nextruder</strong> – a direct-drive extruder with a 10:1 planetary gearbox and quick-swap nozzles.</li>
<li><strong>32-bit electronics</strong> with Input Shaper and Pressure Advance for faster printing.</li>
<li><strong>Optional MMU3</strong> for printing with up to five materials.</li>
</ul>
<p>US launch prices were US$1,099 assembled and US$799 as a kit.</p>
<h2>Prusa MK4 vs MK4S</h2>
<p>Prusa announced the MK4S on 12 August 2024. It keeps the frame, build volume and loadcell, and adds:</p>
<ul>
<li>360° part cooling with a turbine fan;</li>
<li>a high-flow CHT 0.4 mm nozzle, which Prusa says gives 45–114% more flow depending on material;</li>
<li>tuned speed profiles;</li>
<li>Wi-Fi setup by NFC and support for the Prusa app.</li>
</ul>
<p>MK4 owners can install the MK4 to MK4S upgrade kit themselves (US$99 at launch); Prusa&#39;s Knowledge Base has an eight-chapter guide.</p>
<h2>First setup</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unbox the assembled printer, or build the kit with the online assembly guide (nine chapters for the MK4S).</li>
<li>Mount the spool and load filament.</li>
<li>Run the Selftest wizard; on an assembled MK4S Prusa says this takes about 10 minutes.</li>
<li>Connect to Wi-Fi from the touchscreen, by NFC or the app, or plug in Ethernet.</li>
<li>In PrusaSlicer, run the Configuration Wizard, pick the printer and nozzle, slice and send your first print.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the MK4</h2>
<p><strong>PrusaLink</strong> runs on the printer and opens in a browser at its IP address, without internet. <strong>Prusa Connect</strong> is Prusa&#39;s cloud service for remote printing and monitoring. You can also print from a USB drive. Slice in <strong>PrusaSlicer</strong>, which reads STL, 3MF and STEP files. To convert a CAD file first, use our <a href="/step-to-stl/">STEP to STL converter</a>.</p>
<h2>Manual and firmware</h2>
<p>Prusa publishes the MK4 and MK4S handbooks as PDFs in the Prusa Knowledge Base under Firmware &amp; Downloads, together with the assembly guides and the latest firmware.</p>
