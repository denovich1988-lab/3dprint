<h2>Who the Neptune 4 suits</h2>
<p>It suits beginners and hobbyists who want Klipper-level speed on a budget and print mostly PLA, PETG and TPU. It is an open-frame machine with a moving bed, so it needs some depth on the desk and an enclosure if you want to print ABS or ASA. If you need a bigger bed, the <a href="/printers/elegoo-neptune-4-max/">Elegoo Neptune 4 Max</a> offers 420 × 420 × 480 mm.</p>
<h2>Neptune 4 features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Klipper firmware</strong> with input shaping and pressure advance, running on a 1.5 GHz quad-core board.</li>
<li><strong>Dual-gear direct extruder</strong> with a 5.2:1 reduction and a copper-titanium heatbreak for nozzle temperatures up to 300 °C.</li>
<li><strong>Automatic bed levelling</strong> – 36 points by default, with a 121-point (11 × 11) option on the screen.</li>
<li><strong>Dual Z lead screws</strong> driven by two motors, a PEI spring steel plate and filament run-out detection.</li>
<li><strong>Removable touchscreen</strong> and a quiet mode that Elegoo rates under 50 dB.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Setting up the Neptune 4</h2>
<p>The printer arrives largely pre-assembled. The user guide&#39;s steps, in short:</p>
<ol>
<li>Fit the gantry to the base as shown in the assembly video on the USB drive, then mount the screen, filament sensor and cable clamp.</li>
<li>Set the voltage switch to match your mains supply.</li>
<li>Power on and do a quick paper check of the bed at the centre and corners.</li>
<li>Run <strong>auto levelling</strong> from the screen and set the Z-offset.</li>
<li>Load filament (<strong>Prepare → Extruder → Load</strong>) and print the test file from the USB drive.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> if your first layers look uneven at the edges, switch the levelling grid from 36 to 121 points and re-run it with the bed at printing temperature.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Neptune 4</h2>
<p>The base Neptune 4 has no Wi-Fi. Connect it to your router with an Ethernet cable and open its IP address in a browser to reach the Fluidd web interface, where you can upload files and watch prints. Otherwise copy G-code to the USB drive. To check a sliced file before printing, open it in our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Neptune 4 manual</h2>
<p>Elegoo publishes the Neptune 4 user guide in the ELEGOO Download Center under the Neptune series, and a copy comes on the USB drive with the assembly video. It covers the parts list, assembly, voltage setting, levelling, loading filament, the first print and troubleshooting.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Firmware for the mainboard and touchscreen is listed on the ELEGOO Wiki, with update guides for the Neptune 4 and 4 Pro; versions with a Type-C port also support over-the-air updates. Re-level after a firmware update. The printer ships with <strong>ELEGOO Cura</strong> profiles on the USB drive. Elegoo runs its own Klipper build, so updates come from Elegoo, not the main Klipper project.</p>
