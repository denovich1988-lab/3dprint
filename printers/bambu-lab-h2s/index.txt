2a:Td9e,<p><strong>The Bambu H2S (Bambu Lab H2S) is a large, enclosed single-nozzle 3D printer with a 340 × 320 × 340 mm build volume, a 350 °C hot end and a heated chamber.</strong> Bambu Lab announced it on 26 August 2025 as the single-nozzle model of its H2 series, next to the dual-nozzle H2D.</p>
<h2>Who the Bambu Lab H2S suits</h2>
<p>The H2S suits makers and small workshops that print big parts or engineering materials and do not need two nozzles. The heated chamber (up to 65 °C) helps with warp-prone filaments such as ABS, ASA, PC and nylon. Bambu Lab&#39;s product page names PLA, PETG, PC, PPA and TPU from its own range as supported materials.</p>
<p>It is also a multi-tool machine. Bambu Lab sells it in three versions (US launch prices from the announcement):</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>H2S</strong> – printer only, US$1,249.</li>
<li><strong>H2S AMS Combo</strong> – with an AMS 2 Pro for four-colour printing and filament drying, US$1,499.</li>
<li><strong>H2S Laser Full Combo</strong> – with a 10 W laser module, US$2,099. A standard H2S can be upgraded later with the Laser Upgrade Kit.</li>
</ul>
<p>The laser cuts up to 5 mm plywood, acrylic or PU, according to Bambu Lab. There are also cutting (paper, vinyl, PU leather) and pen-drawing modules.</p>
<h2>Bambu H2S vs H2D</h2>
<p>Both printers share the same frame size (492 × 514 × 626 mm), the 350 °C hot end, the 65 °C chamber, the servo extruder and the same AMS limits. The differences:</p>

<h2>First setup</h2>
<p>Bambu Lab&#39;s unboxing guide on the wiki covers the package contents and removing the transport restraints. Two points it stresses: remove the restraints without cutting the belt, and expect the heat bed to rise slowly during the first start. After that:</p>
<ol>
<li>Connect power and switch on.</li>
<li>Follow the on-screen calibration wizard.</li>
<li>Connect Wi-Fi and bind the printer to your account in the Bambu Handy app (or choose LAN mode).</li>
<li>Load filament into the AMS or the external spool holder and start a test print.</li>
</ol>
<p>The AMS Combo and Laser Full Combo have a separate unboxing guide.</p>
<h2>Connecting the H2S</h2>
<p>The H2S connects over Wi-Fi, including 5 GHz networks. You send prints from <strong>Bambu Studio</strong>, Bambu Lab&#39;s slicer, and watch them in the Bambu Handy app. It also works offline: insert a USB drive (one drive, no hubs) and print from it. Bambu Lab notes that LAN printing and sending files from Bambu Studio need a USB drive inserted.</p>
<h2>Manual and firmware</h2>
<p>Bambu Lab publishes the H2S manual pages, FAQ and maintenance guides on the Bambu Lab Wiki under the H2 series manual pages. Firmware updates install from the touchscreen, the Bambu Handy app or Bambu Studio.</p>
