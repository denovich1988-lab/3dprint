Bambu Lab H2S – specs, setup, laser option and who it suits

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Bambu H2S is an enclosed single-nozzle printer with a 340 × 320 × 340 mm build volume.
  • Bambu Lab lists a 350 °C hot end, a 120 °C bed and an actively heated chamber up to 65 °C.
  • It was announced on 26 August 2025 and can be bought with an AMS 2 Pro or a 10 W laser module.

The Bambu H2S (Bambu Lab H2S) is a large, enclosed single-nozzle 3D printer with a 340 × 320 × 340 mm build volume, a 350 °C hot end and a heated chamber. Bambu Lab announced it on 26 August 2025 as the single-nozzle model of its H2 series, next to the dual-nozzle H2D.

Who the Bambu Lab H2S suits

The H2S suits makers and small workshops that print big parts or engineering materials and do not need two nozzles. The heated chamber (up to 65 °C) helps with warp-prone filaments such as ABS, ASA, PC and nylon. Bambu Lab's product page names PLA, PETG, PC, PPA and TPU from its own range as supported materials.

It is also a multi-tool machine. Bambu Lab sells it in three versions (US launch prices from the announcement):

  • H2S – printer only, US$1,249.
  • H2S AMS Combo – with an AMS 2 Pro for four-colour printing and filament drying, US$1,499.
  • H2S Laser Full Combo – with a 10 W laser module, US$2,099. A standard H2S can be upgraded later with the Laser Upgrade Kit.

The laser cuts up to 5 mm plywood, acrylic or PU, according to Bambu Lab. There are also cutting (paper, vinyl, PU leather) and pen-drawing modules.

Bambu H2S vs H2D

Both printers share the same frame size (492 × 514 × 626 mm), the 350 °C hot end, the 65 °C chamber, the servo extruder and the same AMS limits. The differences:

H2S H2D
Nozzles 1 2
Build volume 340 × 320 × 340 mm 350 × 320 × 325 mm (total)
Laser options 10 W 10 W or 40 W

Choose the H2D if you want soluble supports or two materials in one print. See our Bambu Lab H2D page for details.

First setup

Bambu Lab's unboxing guide on the wiki covers the package contents and removing the transport restraints. Two points it stresses: remove the restraints without cutting the belt, and expect the heat bed to rise slowly during the first start. After that:

  1. Connect power and switch on.
  2. Follow the on-screen calibration wizard.
  3. Connect Wi-Fi and bind the printer to your account in the Bambu Handy app (or choose LAN mode).
  4. Load filament into the AMS or the external spool holder and start a test print.

The AMS Combo and Laser Full Combo have a separate unboxing guide.

Connecting the H2S

The H2S connects over Wi-Fi, including 5 GHz networks. You send prints from Bambu Studio, Bambu Lab's slicer, and watch them in the Bambu Handy app. It also works offline: insert a USB drive (one drive, no hubs) and print from it. Bambu Lab notes that LAN printing and sending files from Bambu Studio need a USB drive inserted.

Manual and firmware

Bambu Lab publishes the H2S manual pages, FAQ and maintenance guides on the Bambu Lab Wiki under the H2 series manual pages. Firmware updates install from the touchscreen, the Bambu Handy app or Bambu Studio.

Questions and answers

What is the build volume of the Bambu H2S?

Bambu Lab lists 340 × 320 × 340 mm for the H2S, slightly taller than the H2D's 325 mm.

Does the Bambu Lab H2S have two nozzles?

No. The H2S has a single nozzle. The H2D is the dual-nozzle model in the H2 series.

Can the H2S use a laser?

Yes, with the 10 W laser module, sold in the Laser Full Combo or as an upgrade kit. Bambu Lab says the 40 W laser is not supported on the H2S.

Which AMS works with the H2S?

The AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT. The first-generation AMS works without its drying function, and the AMS lite is not supported.
