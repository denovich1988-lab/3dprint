<h2>How to choose an Ender printer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Want the simplest start?</strong> Pick a model with auto levelling – the V2 Neo, S1 or any V3 – rather than the original Ender-3 or V2.</li>
<li><strong>Want Wi-Fi?</strong> The V3 KE has it; the V3 SE prints from an SD card only.</li>
<li><strong>Need a bigger bed?</strong> Look at the S1 Plus, V3 Plus, V3 Mega or Ender-5 Max.</li>
<li><strong>Buying used?</strong> Older models are cheap, but expect manual levelling and some tuning.</li>
</ol>
