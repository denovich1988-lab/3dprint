Bambu Lab A1 mini – specs, setup, manual and limits

Updated November 22, 2026

Key points
  • The A1 mini is a compact open printer with a 180 × 180 × 180 mm build volume.
  • Bambu Lab lists a 300 °C hot end, 80 °C bed, 500 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² acceleration.
  • Bambu Lab recommends PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA; ABS, ASA, PC and PA are not recommended.
Specifications (from the maker’s published spec sheet)
Build volume180 × 180 × 180 mm
ChassisSteel and extruded aluminium
Max hot end temperature300 °C
Nozzle0.4 mm stainless steel included; 0.2 / 0.6 / 0.8 mm optional
Max build plate temperature80 °C
Max toolhead speed500 mm/s
Max acceleration10,000 mm/s²
Max hot end flow28 mm³/s (Bambu ABS at 280 °C)
Size and weight347 × 315 × 365 mm; 5.5 kg
Recommended room10–30 °C, humidity under 85%
EnclosureOpen frame
Multi-colourUp to 4 colours with the optional AMS lite

The Bambu Lab A1 mini is a small, open printer that brings Bambu Lab’s automatic calibration to a desk-friendly size. Its build volume is 180 mm in each direction, so it is best for figures, toys, small household parts and keychains.

Who it suits

Beginners, children’s rooms (with supervision), classrooms and anyone short on space. With the optional AMS lite it prints in up to four colours. Bambu Lab says it prefers a room between 10 and 30 °C with humidity under 85%.

Materials

Bambu Lab lists PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA as ideal. Because the bed reaches 80 °C and the printer is open, it does not recommend ABS, ASA, PC or PA. If you need those, look at an enclosed printer such as the P1S or P2S.

Setup and manual

Assembly takes a few minutes using the official video. After the on-screen calibration, bind the printer in Bambu Handy and send prints from Bambu Studio. The manual, FAQ and maintenance guides are on the Bambu Lab Wiki.

Small printer, small files

Turning a logo or name into a keychain is a classic first print: try PNG to STL or SVG to STL, then open the STL in Bambu Studio.

Questions and answers

What can the A1 mini print?

Bambu Lab lists PLA, PETG, TPU and PVA as ideal. ABS, ASA, PC, PA, PET and carbon- or glass-filled filaments are listed as not recommended.

What is the A1 mini build volume?

180 × 180 × 180 mm.

Can I print from a micro SD card?

Yes. Bambu Lab’s wiki explains how to start a print from a micro SD card as well as over Wi-Fi.
