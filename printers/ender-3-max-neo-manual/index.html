Ender 3 Max Neo manual – where to find it and what it covers

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Ender 3 Max Neo user manual ships with the printer, and Creality points owners to its Download Center on creality.com for firmware and files.
  • The manual covers the parts, specifications, package contents, assembly, wiring, preheating, loading filament, auto levelling and the first print.
  • The Ender 3 Max Neo has a 300 × 300 × 320 mm build volume and levels with a CR Touch probe that measures 25 points.

The official Creality Ender 3 Max Neo manual comes with the printer, and the manual itself sends owners to the Download Center on creality.com (Support → Download) for firmware and files. The Ender-3 Max Neo download page lists the Creality Print slicer and the current firmware with release notes. This page summarises the Ender 3 Max Neo manual in our own words; it does not replace it.

What the official Ender 3 Max Neo manual covers

The Ender 3 Max Neo user manual is organised in these parts:

  1. About the printer – names of the main parts.
  2. Equipment parameters – the specifications.
  3. Package contents – everything in the box.
  4. Product installation – fitting the gantry, screen and spool holder.
  5. Equipment wiring – connecting the motors, sensors and power.
  6. Preheating, filling the filament and starting a print.
  7. Automatic levelling and initialisation settings.
  8. Safety notes and maintenance.

Ender 3 Max Neo key specs

Item Detail
Build volume 300 × 300 × 320 mm
Levelling CR Touch, 25 points
Extruder All-metal Bowden extruder
Max nozzle temperature 260 °C
Z axis Dual Z motors with a timing belt
Screen 4.3-inch colour screen with a knob
Power Built-in 350 W supply, 100–120 V / 200–240 V
Extras Filament sensor, power-loss resume

Ender 3 Max Neo quick start

A summary of the manual's setup steps:

  1. Assemble the frame. Set the gantry into the base and fix it with the four long screws.
  2. Fit the screen and spool holder, connect the screen cable, then wire the Z motor and the other labelled connectors.
  3. Set the voltage switch to 115 V or 230 V to match your mains before powering on.
  4. Initialise. Go to Control → Reset configuration, then Prepare → Auto Home, and clear the Z value.
  5. Level. Choose Leveling; the CR Touch probes 25 points. Use the knobs under the bed and a sheet of A4 paper to set a light drag at each corner, then adjust Prepare → Z-offset.
  6. Load filament. Preheat the nozzle, press the extruder lever and push the filament in until it flows from the nozzle.
  7. First print. Slice the model, save the G-code to the card, insert it and choose the file on the screen.

Tip: the manual asks for file names with Latin letters and numbers only, and warns not to remove the card while printing. To check a sliced file on your computer, use our G-code to STL tool.

Maintenance Creality recommends

  • Wipe the printer with a dry cloth, with the power off.
  • Clear dust and stuck filament from the guide rails.
  • Clean the nozzle while it is hot, using tools only.
  • Use the recommended filament, and keep the printer away from heat, vibration and flammable materials.

Common problems and fixes

  • First layer does not stick: the nozzle is too far from the bed. Re-level and lower the Z offset.
  • Under-extrusion or a scratched bed: the nozzle is too close. Raise the Z offset slightly.
  • Changing filament mid-print: the manual suggests dropping the print speed to 10% first. To pull filament out after cooling, heat the nozzle to at least 185 °C.

Firmware and software

Download firmware from the Ender-3 Max Neo page in the Creality Download Center and follow the release notes that come with it. Creality Print is the official slicer; the manual also mentions Creality Slicer. Before slicing a downloaded model, check it in our STL viewer.

Questions and answers

Where can I find the Creality Ender 3 Max Neo manual?

The user manual comes in the box. Creality's manual directs owners to creality.com (Support → Download) for firmware and files, where the Ender-3 Max Neo page lists Creality Print and firmware.

How do you level the Ender 3 Max Neo?

Reset the configuration, auto home, then choose Leveling so the CR Touch probes 25 points. Fine-tune the corners with the bed knobs and a sheet of A4 paper, then set the Z offset.

What voltage setting does the Ender 3 Max Neo use?

Set the power supply switch to 115 V for 100–120 V mains or 230 V for 200–240 V mains before switching on.
