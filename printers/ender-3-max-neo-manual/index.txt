<h2>Ender 3 Max Neo quick start</h2>
<p>A summary of the manual&#39;s setup steps:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Assemble the frame.</strong> Set the gantry into the base and fix it with the four long screws.</li>
<li><strong>Fit the screen and spool holder</strong>, connect the screen cable, then wire the Z motor and the other labelled connectors.</li>
<li><strong>Set the voltage switch</strong> to 115 V or 230 V to match your mains before powering on.</li>
<li><strong>Initialise.</strong> Go to Control → Reset configuration, then Prepare → Auto Home, and clear the Z value.</li>
<li><strong>Level.</strong> Choose Leveling; the CR Touch probes 25 points. Use the knobs under the bed and a sheet of A4 paper to set a light drag at each corner, then adjust Prepare → Z-offset.</li>
<li><strong>Load filament.</strong> Preheat the nozzle, press the extruder lever and push the filament in until it flows from the nozzle.</li>
<li><strong>First print.</strong> Slice the model, save the G-code to the card, insert it and choose the file on the screen.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> the manual asks for file names with Latin letters and numbers only, and warns not to remove the card while printing. To check a sliced file on your computer, use our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Maintenance Creality recommends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Wipe the printer with a dry cloth, with the power off.</li>
<li>Clear dust and stuck filament from the guide rails.</li>
<li>Clean the nozzle while it is hot, using tools only.</li>
<li>Use the recommended filament, and keep the printer away from heat, vibration and flammable materials.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Common problems and fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>First layer does not stick:</strong> the nozzle is too far from the bed. Re-level and lower the Z offset.</li>
<li><strong>Under-extrusion or a scratched bed:</strong> the nozzle is too close. Raise the Z offset slightly.</li>
<li><strong>Changing filament mid-print:</strong> the manual suggests dropping the print speed to 10% first. To pull filament out after cooling, heat the nozzle to at least 185 °C.</li>
</ul>
