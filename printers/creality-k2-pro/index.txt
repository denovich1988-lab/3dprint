<h2>Who the Creality K2 Pro suits</h2>
<p>The K2 Pro suits makers who want a large enclosed printer for engineering filaments such as ABS, ASA and carbon-fibre nylon, with the option of multi-colour printing. The heated chamber helps big ABS and ASA parts stay flat. If 260 mm is enough and you mostly print PLA, the K2 Combo costs less; if you need 350 mm or a 350 °C nozzle, look at the K2 Plus.</p>

<h2>CFS and multi-colour printing</h2>
<p>The K2 Pro Combo includes one <strong>CFS</strong> (Creality Filament System) with four slots, RFID spool recognition and desiccant storage. Up to four CFS units connect for 16 colours. The CFS takes PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, PET and PLA-CF; TPU does not go in it. When a spool runs out, auto filament relay can switch to a matching spool.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Creality K2 Pro</h2>
<p>Creality&#39;s unboxing and setup guide describes these steps:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unbox and remove the transport locks and foam, including the CFS screws, bed foam and Z-axis fasteners.</li>
<li>Connect and mount the screen, fit the glass lid, and install the CFS, buffer, PTFE tubes and 485 cables.</li>
<li>Power on and follow the on-screen guide.</li>
<li>Let the automatic self-check run, removing the foam under the bed when prompted.</li>
<li>Load filament – RFID spools sync their settings automatically.</li>
<li>Start a first print from a model stored on the printer.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the K2 Pro</h2>
<p>Send files over <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> (2.4 GHz) or <strong>Ethernet</strong> from Creality Print, print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>, or monitor remotely with <strong>Creality Cloud</strong>. The chamber camera watches for failures and records time-lapses; the nozzle camera runs flow calibration.</p>
<h2>Creality K2 Pro manual</h2>
<p>Creality publishes the &quot;K2 Pro Combo &amp; K2 Pro User Manual&quot; on the Creality Wiki under K2 Flagship Series → K2 Pro.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Update over the air from <strong>Settings → Version Checking</strong> while the printer is online and linked to Creality Cloud, or copy the firmware image from Creality&#39;s download centre to a USB drive. Connect only one CFS during the update; the CFS updates through the printer. Afterwards, rerun input shaping and auto levelling. Slice in <strong>Creality Print 6.0</strong> or newer; check 3MF projects first in our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
