<h2>Who the Toybox printer suits</h2>
<p>Toybox suits families who want children to print toys without learning slicers, bed levelling or 3D modelling first. Everything runs through the app, and the catalogue is moderated. The trade-off is size: Alpha-series prints are small, so larger objects need a Comet, a Luna or a regular desktop printer such as the <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-a1-mini/">Bambu Lab A1 mini</a>.</p>
<p><strong>Tip for parents:</strong> the nozzle gets hot during printing, so keep younger children supervised and let parts cool before removing them.</p>
<h2>How the app and toy catalogue work</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Creator Space:</strong> the free Toybox app (iOS, Android and web) is where kids browse toys and send prints. Toybox says the catalogue holds thousands of designs, with new ones added each week.</li>
<li><strong>Own designs:</strong> you can import STL, OBJ and gCode files, and 3MF on the Alpha Three. The slicer is built into the app.</li>
<li><strong>Starting a print:</strong> from the printer&#39;s screen, from the app, or from the Toybox website while logged into the same account as the printer.</li>
<li><strong>Subscriptions:</strong> Toybox Pro adds AI tools that turn a text prompt or image into a printable model; Toybox Plus adds exclusive toys and discounts.</li>
</ul>
<p>Our <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a> and <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> help prepare and check your own files before importing them.</p>
<h2>Filament</h2>
<p>Toybox printers use 1.75 mm PLA. Toybox sells its own plant-based PLA under the name &quot;Printer Food&quot;, and its current FAQ says other 1.75 mm PLA also works.</p>
<h2>Setting up a Toybox printer</h2>
<p>Setup is app-led. You plug the printer into mains power (100–240 V), connect it to a 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi network, and link it to your account in the Toybox app. After that, any toy you send from the app or the Toybox website while logged into the same account goes to that printer. Toybox says newer models such as the Alpha Four level the bed automatically and need no tools for calibration.</p>
<h2>Manual and support</h2>
<p>Toybox does not publish a classic printed manual online; instructions and troubleshooting articles are in the Toybox help centre, linked from the toybox.com contact page.</p>
