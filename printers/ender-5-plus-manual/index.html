Ender 5 Plus manual – where to find it and what it covers

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • Creality publishes the Ender 5 Plus user manual in the Ender-5 Plus section of its Download Center, next to the firmware and Creality Print.
  • The manual covers frame assembly, cable connection, the touchscreen, loading filament, manual (AUX) and BLTouch auto levelling, software and preparing to print.
  • The Ender 5 Plus has a 350 × 350 × 400 mm build volume, a pre-installed BLTouch probe, a 4.3-inch touchscreen and a filament runout sensor.

The official Creality Ender 5 Plus manual is in the Ender-5 Plus section of the Creality Download Center, under User Manual, next to the current firmware and the Creality Print slicer. A copy of the manual and the software also come on the storage card in the box. This page summarises the Ender 5 Plus manual in our own words; it does not replace it.

What the official Ender 5 Plus manual covers

The Ender 5 Plus user manual is organised in these parts:

  1. Preface and warnings – safety notes.
  2. Introduction – identifying the printer's components.
  3. Tool list – what is in the box.
  4. Frame assembly – four steps from base to top frame.
  5. Cable connection.
  6. Screen information – the touchscreen menus.
  7. Loading filament.
  8. Levelling – AUX (manual) levelling and auto levelling.
  9. Software installation.
  10. Preparing to print.
  11. Circuit wiring – the wiring diagram.

Ender 5 Plus quick start

In the box: wrench and screwdriver set, storage card and reader, spatula, pliers, nozzle cleaner, power cable, a small filament sample, spare parts and a USB drive.

Assembly (high level):

  1. Bolt the four aluminium uprights to the base, then fit the top frame and corner brackets.
  2. Mount the dual Z-axis frame and check that the axis moves smoothly; adjust the eccentric nuts if it rubs.
  3. Fix the bed to the Z support plate and fit the filament holder.
  4. Connect the cables as the manual shows.
  5. Set the power supply's voltage switch to 115 V or 230 V to match your mains.

First calibration: do the AUX levelling with paper at the four corner knobs, then set a gap of about 0.2 mm and run auto levelling – the BLTouch probes 16 points.

Loading filament: preheat with the PLA or ABS preset, press the extruder lever and feed the filament through the runout sensor and extruder until it comes out of the nozzle.

First print: install the slicer, choose the Ender 5 Plus, slice a model and save the G-code to the card. Clean the glass bed with glass cleaner or isopropyl alcohol, insert the card and choose Print. Check the sliced file first with our G-code to STL tool.

Maintenance the manual recommends

  • Switch off and unplug before cleaning.
  • Wipe the frame, guide rails and wheels with a dry cloth.
  • Keep the print surface clean.
  • Clear the hotend only with the supplied tools, and never touch a hot nozzle.

Common problems and fixes

  • First layer does not stick: the nozzle is too far from the bed – lower the Z offset and clean the glass.
  • Nozzle scrapes, little plastic comes out: the nozzle is too close – raise the Z offset slightly.
  • Uneven first layer across the bed: redo AUX levelling at the four knobs, then re-run auto levelling.
  • Axis grinding or wobbling: adjust the eccentric nuts on the wheels until they roll smoothly without play.

Firmware and software

The Download Center lists the Ender 5 Plus Marlin firmware with release notes and the current Creality Print slicer for Windows, macOS and Linux. The touchscreen has its own firmware that is updated separately from the mainboard. Read the release notes for your board version before flashing.

Questions and answers

Where can I find the Creality Ender 5 Plus manual?

In the Ender-5 Plus 3D Printer section of the Creality Download Center, under User Manual. The same page has the latest firmware and the Creality Print slicer.

How do you level the Ender 5 Plus?

The manual first has you do AUX levelling – adjust the four corner knobs with a sheet of A4 paper – then run auto levelling with the BLTouch after setting a gap of about 0.2 mm, and the printer probes 16 points.

Does the Ender 5 Plus manual have a troubleshooting section?

No dedicated one. It includes a picture guide to the first layer showing a nozzle that is too far, correct and too close, plus care and safety notes.
