<h2>Ender 5 Plus quick start</h2>
<p><strong>In the box:</strong> wrench and screwdriver set, storage card and reader, spatula, pliers, nozzle cleaner, power cable, a small filament sample, spare parts and a USB drive.</p>
<p><strong>Assembly (high level):</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Bolt the four aluminium uprights to the base, then fit the top frame and corner brackets.</li>
<li>Mount the dual Z-axis frame and check that the axis moves smoothly; adjust the eccentric nuts if it rubs.</li>
<li>Fix the bed to the Z support plate and fit the filament holder.</li>
<li>Connect the cables as the manual shows.</li>
<li>Set the power supply&#39;s voltage switch to 115 V or 230 V to match your mains.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>First calibration:</strong> do the AUX levelling with paper at the four corner knobs, then set a gap of about 0.2 mm and run auto levelling – the BLTouch probes 16 points.</p>
<p><strong>Loading filament:</strong> preheat with the PLA or ABS preset, press the extruder lever and feed the filament through the runout sensor and extruder until it comes out of the nozzle.</p>
<p><strong>First print:</strong> install the slicer, choose the Ender 5 Plus, slice a model and save the G-code to the card. Clean the glass bed with glass cleaner or isopropyl alcohol, insert the card and choose Print. Check the sliced file first with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Maintenance the manual recommends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Switch off and unplug before cleaning.</li>
<li>Wipe the frame, guide rails and wheels with a dry cloth.</li>
<li>Keep the print surface clean.</li>
<li>Clear the hotend only with the supplied tools, and never touch a hot nozzle.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Common problems and fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>First layer does not stick:</strong> the nozzle is too far from the bed – lower the Z offset and clean the glass.</li>
<li><strong>Nozzle scrapes, little plastic comes out:</strong> the nozzle is too close – raise the Z offset slightly.</li>
<li><strong>Uneven first layer across the bed:</strong> redo AUX levelling at the four knobs, then re-run auto levelling.</li>
<li><strong>Axis grinding or wobbling:</strong> adjust the eccentric nuts on the wheels until they roll smoothly without play.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>The Download Center lists the Ender 5 Plus Marlin firmware with release notes and the current <strong>Creality Print</strong> slicer for Windows, macOS and Linux. The touchscreen has its own firmware that is updated separately from the mainboard. Read the release notes for your board version before flashing.</p>
