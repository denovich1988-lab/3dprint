<h2>What the official Voron 2.4 manual covers</h2>
<p>The Voron 2.4 assembly manual follows the order of the build. In broad terms it covers:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Introduction</strong> – printing the parts, the hardware you need and general build notes.</li>
<li><strong>Frame assembly</strong> – squaring the aluminium extrusion frame.</li>
<li><strong>Z drive</strong> – the four corner Z motors that move the gantry.</li>
<li><strong>A/B drive modules</strong> – the CoreXY motor mounts.</li>

</ol>
<p>Section names have changed between manual revisions (the 2.4R2 revision came out in February 2022), so follow the version that matches your parts.</p>

<h2>Quick-start summary after assembly</h2>
<p>The Voron build guide continues after the mechanical build with electrical wiring, software installation (Mainsail, Fluidd or OctoPrint on a single-board computer) and Klipper configuration. In short:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Configure Klipper</strong> – download the Voron 2.4 printer.cfg template from the repository, set the serial IDs of your controller boards and use the <code>[quad_gantry_level]</code> section, then run <code>FIRMWARE_RESTART</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Check temperatures, heaters and fans</strong> before anything moves.</li>
<li><strong>Check stepper direction</strong> with <code>STEPPER_BUZZ</code>, then home X and Y.</li>
<li><strong>Set up the Z endstop</strong> and run <strong>Quad Gantry Level</strong> to level the gantry to the bed.</li>
<li><strong>Check endstops and the probe</strong> with <code>QUERY_ENDSTOPS</code> and <code>PROBE_ACCURACY</code>, then calibrate the probe.</li>
<li><strong>PID tune</strong> the hotend and bed, set the <strong>Z offset</strong> and calibrate the extruder&#39;s <code>rotation_distance</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Slicer setup and first print</strong>, then the secondary tuning guides (filament tuning, sensorless homing and more).</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> check every heater and thermistor reading before homing. A swapped thermistor or reversed motor is far cheaper to find at this stage than after a crash.</p>

<h2>Common Voron 2.4 build problems</h2>
<p>These come from the community section of the Voron documentation:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Warped bed</strong> – heating the bed too fast or tightening its bolts while cold can bend it; follow the bed mounting instructions.</li>
<li><strong>Mixed-up pulleys</strong> – the Z drive uses 16-tooth pulleys that look almost the same as the 20-tooth pulleys used elsewhere.</li>
<li><strong>Damaged drivers or board</strong> – never plug in motors while the electronics are powered, and avoid spinning steppers fast by hand.</li>
<li><strong>Nozzle crashes</strong> – a crash at speed can bend the frame or gantry, so confirm homing and probing before printing.</li>
</ul>
