<h2>A1 series: open printers for beginners</h2>
<p>The <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-a1-mini/">Bambu Lab A1 mini</a> and <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-a1/">A1</a> are open-frame bed-slingers. They suit PLA, PETG and TPU, and pair with the four-spool AMS lite. Bambu Lab does not recommend them for ABS, ASA, PC or nylon, which need an enclosure.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> choose the A1 over the A1 mini if you print helmets, storage boxes or other parts wider than 180 mm.</p>
<h2>P series: enclosed all-rounders</h2>
<p>The <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-p1s/">P1S</a> and its successor the <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-p2s/">P2S</a> are enclosed CoreXY printers with a 256 mm cube build volume. The enclosure lets them print ABS and ASA. The P2S, announced in October 2025, adds a 5-inch touchscreen, a 1080p camera and a servo extruder.</p>
<h2>X series: from X1 Carbon to X2D</h2>
<p>The <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-x1-carbon/">X1 Carbon</a> was Bambu Lab&#39;s first printer (2022), with lidar and a hardened steel nozzle. It went out of production on 31 March 2026. The <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-x2d/">X2D</a>, announced in April 2026, takes its place in the range with two switchable nozzles, a 65 °C heated chamber and HEPA plus carbon filtration.</p>
<h2>H2 series: large format, lasers and many materials</h2>
<p>The H2 printers share one large frame, a 350 °C hot end and a 65 °C heated chamber:</p>
<ul>
<li><a href="/printers/bambu-lab-h2s/">H2S</a> – one nozzle and the tallest build volume (340 mm).</li>
<li><a href="/printers/bambu-lab-h2d/">H2D</a> – two nozzles; optional 10 W or 40 W laser.</li>
<li><a href="/printers/bambu-lab-h2c/">H2C</a> – the Vortek system swaps between six hot ends for multi-material prints.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Multi-colour printing with AMS</h2>
<p>Bambu Lab&#39;s automatic material systems feed up to four spools each. The AMS lite fits the A1 series; the AMS and AMS 2 Pro fit the enclosed printers, and the AMS HT holds one spool for drying and high-temperature filaments. See our <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-ams/">Bambu Lab AMS</a> page for compatibility.</p>
<h2>Software for every Bambu printer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bambu Studio</strong> – Bambu Lab&#39;s free slicer for Windows, macOS and Linux. It saves projects as 3MF files; our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a> and <a href="/3mf-to-stl/">3MF to STL converter</a> open them in a browser.</li>
<li><strong>Bambu Handy</strong> – the phone app for binding, starting and watching prints.</li>
<li><strong>MakerWorld</strong> – Bambu Lab&#39;s model library, linked from Bambu Studio and Bambu Handy.</li>
<li><strong>LAN mode</strong> – lets every model print without the cloud.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How to choose a Bambu Lab printer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Materials:</strong> only PLA, PETG and TPU → A1 or A1 mini. ABS, ASA → P1S or P2S. Nylon, PC and fibre-filled at scale → X2D or the H2 series.</li>
<li><strong>Size:</strong> 180 mm (A1 mini), 256 mm (A1, P series, X2D) or about 330–350 mm (H2 series).</li>
<li><strong>Supports and colours:</strong> two nozzles (X2D, H2D) for soluble or break-away supports; the H2C for many materials with little waste.</li>
<li><strong>Extra tools:</strong> a laser module is only available on the H2 series.</li>
</ol>
