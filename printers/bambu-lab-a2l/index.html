Bambu A2L – specs, setup and who it suits

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Bambu Lab A2L is an open-frame bed-slinger with a 330 × 320 × 325 mm build volume.
  • Bambu Lab launched it on 1 June 2026 at US$469, or US$569 as a Combo with the AMS lite.
  • It takes up to four box-style AMS units plus one AMS lite, for up to 19 colours.

The Bambu A2L (Bambu Lab A2L) is an open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a 330 × 320 × 325 mm build volume, a 300 °C hotend and support for up to 19 colours with Bambu's AMS units. Bambu Lab launched it on 1 June 2026 at US$469, or US$569 for the A2L Combo with the AMS lite (US launch prices). It scales up the A1's design for larger prints.

Who the Bambu A2L suits

The A2L suits hobbyists and creators who print PLA and PETG and have outgrown a 256 mm bed and want to print larger parts in one piece. Bambu Lab rates it under 49 dB in silent mode. It is not built for engineering filaments: the bed tops out at 80 °C (the A1 reaches 100 °C) and there is no enclosure. For those materials, look at an enclosed model such as the Bambu Lab H2S.

Bambu A2L vs A1

The A2L keeps the A1's open bed-slinger layout and sensors – filament run-out, nozzle clog and tangle detection – and adds:

  • About twice the build space of the 256 mm-class Bambu Lab A1.
  • A servo extruder motor that monitors extrusion and a blob detector that protects the nozzle.
  • Adaptive vibration compensation and two granular dampers in the frame for the heavier moving bed.
  • Support for box-style AMS units, not just the AMS lite.

Multi-colour and add-on modules

The A2L Combo ships with the AMS lite. The printer also accepts the AMS, AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT – up to four box-style units plus one AMS lite, for 19 filament paths. Drying in the AMS 2 Pro or AMS HT needs a separate power cable.

A dedicated mounting point for expansion modules takes Bambu's Blade Cutting Upgrade Kit (US$69 at launch), which adds a cutting module and a pen module for stickers, leather, fabric and drawing. Bambu Lab says the laser module is not supported, for safety reasons on an open frame.

Setting up the Bambu A2L

Bambu Lab's quick start guide describes these steps:

  1. Lift out the base with both hands and fix the heatbed with four screws.
  2. Mount the base on the frame with ten screws, grease the Y-axis rail and refit its cover.
  3. Connect the Z-axis motor cable and the integrated cable, remove the two Z-axis limiters and all tapes.
  4. Fit the purge wiper, fold out the touchscreen and lay the build plate on the bed.
  5. Connect filament through the PTFE coupler (AMS) or the spool holder (external spool).
  6. Leave about 250 mm behind and 120 mm in front of the printer, then switch on.
  7. Join Wi-Fi, bind the printer in Bambu Handy or Bambu Studio and print a model from the microSD card.

Tip: do not use a guest network with device isolation – the printer and app must see each other.

Connecting the Bambu A2L

The A2L connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and reads files from a microSD card. Bind it with Bambu Handy by scanning the QR code on the screen, or add it from the device page in Bambu Studio on the same network.

Bambu A2L manual

A printed quick start guide comes in the box. It points to Bambu Lab's online video quick start, a separate A2L AMS connection guide for multiple or mixed AMS units, and the Bambu Lab Wiki for tutorials and maintenance.

Firmware and software

Slice in Bambu Studio; third-party slicers that export standard G-code also work, though some features may be unavailable. Check multi-colour projects first in our 3MF viewer, and compare the A2L with other printers in our printer comparison tool.

Questions and answers

What is the build volume of the Bambu A2L?

330 × 320 × 325 mm. Bambu Lab says that is about 105% more space than its 256 mm-class printers.

How much did the Bambu Lab A2L cost at launch?

Bambu Lab launched it on 1 June 2026 at US$469 for the printer and US$569 for the A2L Combo with the AMS lite, in the US.

Can the Bambu A2L print ABS or ASA?

Bambu Lab positions it for PLA, PETG and other non-engineering filaments. The bed reaches 80 °C and the printer is open, so ABS and ASA are not what it is made for.

Does the Bambu A2L work with the AMS 2 Pro?

Yes. Besides the AMS lite it accepts the AMS, AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT. Drying in the AMS 2 Pro or AMS HT needs a separate power cable, because the printer's bus does not supply enough power.
