<h2>Who the Mars 5 Ultra suits</h2>
<p>It suits hobbyists who print tabletop miniatures and small detailed models and want automatic levelling and remote monitoring without moving up to a larger, pricier resin printer. The build volume is small, so for bigger models look at the <a href="/printers/elegoo-saturn-4-ultra/">Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra</a>.</p>
<h2>Mars 5 Ultra features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tilt release:</strong> the build plate tilts to peel each layer from the PFA film, with switchable fast and slow modes. In the Mars 5 line this is only on the Ultra.</li>
<li><strong>AI camera:</strong> detects an empty build plate and warping, alerts you to errors and records time-lapses.</li>
<li><strong>Auto levelling:</strong> one-click self-check and levelling with a mechanical sensor.</li>
<li><strong>Smart sensors:</strong> resin shortage, residue in the vat, levelling failure, power-loss resume, and a pause if the printer reaches 80 °C.</li>
<li><strong>Release film reminder</strong> after about 60,000 uses.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Setting up the Mars 5 Ultra</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer and remove all protective film and packing pieces.</li>
<li>Run the <strong>device self-check</strong> from the touchscreen.</li>
<li>Fit the build plate and vat and run <strong>one-click auto levelling</strong>; Elegoo says no manual levelling is needed.</li>
<li>Pour resin into the vat up to the marked line and print the test file from the USB drive.</li>
<li>Wash and cure the print, wearing gloves when handling uncured resin.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> run a resin calibration print (the manual has a section on it) whenever you switch resin brands, so the exposure time matches the new resin.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Mars 5 Ultra</h2>
<p>Print from the <strong>USB drive</strong> or send files over <strong>Wi-Fi</strong>. On a network, the printer works with CHITUBOX&#39;s network management tools, and you can manage several printers at once (cluster printing). No internet connection is needed for local Wi-Fi printing.</p>
<h2>Mars 5 Ultra manual</h2>
<p>The user manual ships printed with the printer and as a file on the included USB drive. It covers the packing list, specifications, self-check, test printing, software setup, network management, the AI camera, resin calibration, firmware upgrades, FAQ and maintenance. Firmware and other downloads are on Elegoo&#39;s website.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>When the printer is online it shows a pop-up when printer or camera firmware updates are available. To update offline, copy the firmware package from Elegoo to a USB drive and choose <strong>Settings → Version Upgrade → Local Upgrade</strong>. Slice in <strong>CHITUBOX</strong> with the ELEGOO Mars 5 Ultra profile. Before slicing, you can inspect a model in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> or convert an OBJ with our <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a>.</p>
