Questions and answers

How do I compare 3D printers?

Pick two or three printers above. The 3D printer comparison table lines up the manufacturers’ published specs – build volume, speed, temperatures, enclosure, multi-colour support – and marks the highest number in each measurable row.

Where do the specs come from?

Every value comes from the manufacturer’s official spec page, wiki or manual, listed on each printer’s guide. We do not add test scores or user ratings.

Is a higher max speed always better?

Not by itself. Max speed is a rated limit; real print speed also depends on acceleration, hot end flow, the material and the model. Compare acceleration and flow too, and read the printer guide for who it suits.

Why are prices not compared?

Prices change often and differ by country and bundle, so we leave them out. Where a guide mentions a launch price it is labelled with its region.
