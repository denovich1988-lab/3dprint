29:Tebf,<p><strong>The Bambu Lab AMS lite is a four-slot automatic material system that adds multi-colour and multi-material printing to the Bambu Lab A1 and A1 mini.</strong> It has an open design with rotating spool holders and no cover. When a spool runs out, it can switch to another spool with the same filament. Bambu Lab announced it with the A1 mini on 20 September 2023.</p>

<h2>Which printers work with the AMS lite</h2>
<p>The AMS lite connects only to the A1 and A1 mini, with a 4-pin cable. It does not work with the X1 or P1 series. Bambu Lab&#39;s Wiki also describes an AMS Hub for the A1 series that lets the A1 use the AMS, AMS 2 Pro and AMS HT instead; the AMS lite cannot be mixed with those units. See our <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-ams/">Bambu AMS guide</a> for the other units.</p>
<h2>Supported filaments</h2>
<p>Bambu Lab lists PLA, PETG, ASA, PET, PA, PC, PP, POM, HIPS, Bambu PLA-CF, Bambu PAHT-CF, and dry PVA and BVOH as supported.</p>
<p><strong>Do not load</strong> TPU 95A or softer, TPE, wet PVA or BVOH, Bambu PET-CF, PA6-CF or other carbon- or glass-filled filaments. Bambu Lab also advises against glow-in-the-dark PLA, which wears parts faster. Feed these from an external spool instead.</p>
<h2>RFID and filament settings</h2>
<p>Each slot has an RFID coil that reads Bambu Lab filament tags and sends the type and colour to Bambu Studio. Third-party filament has no tag, so you set its type and colour by hand for that slot.</p>
<h2>Setting up the AMS lite</h2>
<ol>
<li>Fix the AMS lite stand with the supplied screws and place the unit about 50 mm from the printer.</li>
<li>Connect the PTFE tubes: the short ones to ports 1–2, the long ones to ports 3–4.</li>
<li>Plug the 4-pin cable into the printer, leaving room for the bed to move.</li>
<li>Mount a spool so the filament leaves from below the feeder and push it into the inlet – it feeds about 40 cm automatically.</li>
<li>Slice in Bambu Studio, enable &quot;Use AMS&quot; and map each colour to a slot.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> put heavier spools in slots 2 and 3 and lighter ones in slots 1 and 4. Do not mount the AMS lite on a wall.</p>
<h2>Mounting the AMS lite on top of the A1</h2>
<p>On the A1 (not the A1 mini), the AMS lite can sit on the frame beam with a printed bracket set and screws, described in Bambu Lab&#39;s top mount tutorial. The total height is then about 80 cm.</p>
<h2>Common AMS lite problems</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>False tangle alarms:</strong> usually PTFE tubes that are too long or bent sharply – use the supplied lengths.</li>
<li><strong>Jams:</strong> brittle or fibre-filled filament; dry it or feed it from an external spool.</li>
<li><strong>Feeding failures:</strong> check the PTFE tubes for wear during routine maintenance and replace worn ones.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Launch price</h2>
<p>The A1 mini Combo with AMS lite launched at US$459 in the US. All3DP reported the AMS lite on its own at US$249.</p>
