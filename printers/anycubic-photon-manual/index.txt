<h2>Quick start: levelling and the LCD test</h2>
<ol>
<li>Go to Tools → Move Z → Home.</li>
<li>Loosen the platform screw and lay a sheet of A4 paper on the LCD.</li>
<li>Lower Z in small steps until the paper drags slightly when pulled.</li>
<li>Press the platform flat, tighten the screw and confirm Z = 0 in the Tools menu.</li>
<li>Raise the platform and run the detection test: a full rectangle should light up on the LCD.</li>
<li>Fit the vat against its stops and tighten the nuts on both sides.</li>
</ol>
<h2>The first print</h2>
<p>Wear gloves and a mask. Fill the vat to about a third and start the test file from the USB drive. If you need more resin during a print, pause, add it and resume. After printing, rinse the part in alcohol and post-cure it under UV light. Filter leftover resin back into the bottle and make sure no cured bits stay in the vat.</p>

<h2>Maintenance</h2>
<ul>
<li>Lubricate the Z lead screw if it gets noisy.</li>
<li>Never use sharp tools on the FEP film at the bottom of the vat.</li>
<li>Do not leave resin in the vat for more than two days.</li>
<li>Clean the vat fully when you change resin colour.</li>
</ul>
