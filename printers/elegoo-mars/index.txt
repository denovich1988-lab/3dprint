<h2>Who the Elegoo Mars suits</h2>
<p>The Mars suits hobbyists who print miniatures, jewellery models or small detailed parts and have a ventilated space for resin. Its 2K colour LCD exposes layers more slowly than modern mono screens, so a newer Mars model is a better buy if you are starting today.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Elegoo Mars</h2>
<p>Based on Elegoo&#39;s product information and published reviews, setup is short:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpack, fit the build plate and connect the power adapter.</li>
<li>Insert the USB drive that ships with the printer.</li>
<li>Level the platform with a sheet of paper (see below).</li>
<li>Wearing gloves and a mask, pour resin into the vat – about a third full is enough for a first print.</li>
<li>Print the test file from the USB drive.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> resin is sensitive to temperature. Print in a room that is not cold, and wash and cure parts after printing.</p>
<h2>Levelling the build plate</h2>
<p>Loosen the screw on the build plate, lay a sheet of paper on the LCD, then home the plate from the touchscreen. Press the plate flat onto the paper, tighten the screw and save the zero position. Elegoo describes a steel-ball balancing joint in the plate that helps it settle flat.</p>
<h2>Slicing for the Elegoo Mars</h2>
<p>Elegoo pairs the Mars with <strong>CHITUBOX</strong>. Hollow and support your model, slice, save the file to the USB drive and start the print on the touchscreen. Check that your model is a clean mesh before slicing – our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> shows it in the browser, and the <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a> handles other formats.</p>
