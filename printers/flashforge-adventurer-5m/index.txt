2b:Tc4c,<p><strong>The Flashforge Adventurer 5M 3D printer is an open-frame CoreXY machine with a 220 × 220 × 220 mm build volume, one-click auto levelling and a quick-release nozzle.</strong> Flashforge introduced it in September 2023 at US$399 as the open &quot;sister version&quot; of the enclosed Adventurer 5M Pro (US$599). It is aimed at beginners who want a fast printer for PLA and PETG.</p>
<h2>Who the Flashforge Adventurer 5M suits</h2>
<p>The 5M suits first-time buyers and hobbyists who print PLA and PETG and want CoreXY speed without tuning. Flashforge says it arrives 95% pre-assembled. With a 0.6 or 0.8 mm nozzle it also prints TPU and carbon-fibre-filled PLA and PETG. It is not meant for ABS – that needs the enclosed 5M Pro.</p>
<p>The nozzle unlocks with a lever, so changing nozzle size or clearing a clog takes seconds rather than a heated disassembly.</p>

<h2>First setup</h2>
<p>Flashforge&#39;s Quick Start Guide lists these steps:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unbox and remove the foam, bags and tape.</li>
<li>Remove the transport screws and sheet-metal brackets, then flip up and lock the screen.</li>
<li>Fit the spool holder and the guide tube between the run-out sensor and the extruder.</li>
<li>Remove the four build-plate lock screws.</li>
<li>Switch on, choose a language and join Wi-Fi.</li>
<li>Optionally bind the printer in the Flash Maker app by scanning the QR code on the screen.</li>
<li>Run the first auto-calibration – noise during it is normal.</li>
<li>Load PLA through the sensor, clean the plate, apply glue and print the built-in test file.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Adventurer 5M</h2>
<p>The 5M connects over Wi-Fi or Ethernet. Send prints from <strong>Orca-Flashforge</strong> (from the Flashforge Download Center), OrcaSlicer 1.8.0 or later, or FlashPrint 5. The <strong>Flash Maker</strong> app handles binding and remote control, and a USB drive works for offline printing.</p>
<h2>Flashforge Adventurer 5M manual</h2>
<p>Flashforge publishes the quick-start guide on the Flashforge Wiki under Adventurer Series → Manual → &quot;Quick-start Guide for Your Adventurer 5M&quot;, with a separate guide for the 5M Pro in the same place. Firmware updates install from the touchscreen.</p>
