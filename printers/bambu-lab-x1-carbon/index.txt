2b:Td4e,<p><strong>The Bambu Lab X1 Carbon (X1C) is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, a hardened steel nozzle, a lidar sensor and optional four-spool AMS multi-colour printing.</strong> It was Bambu Lab&#39;s first printer, launched on Kickstarter in May 2022. Bambu Lab ended X1-series production on 31 March 2026, so this page also covers how long the X1C stays supported.</p>
<h2>Who the Bambu X1 Carbon suits</h2>
<p>The X1C was built for engineering materials. The enclosure, the 300 °C all-metal hot end, the hardened steel nozzle and the activated carbon filter let it print PA (nylon), PC and fibre-reinforced filaments, as well as everyday PLA and PETG. The lidar scans the first layer and helps calibrate flow.</p>
<p>It was sold in two versions: the <strong>X1-Carbon</strong> on its own and the <strong>X1-Carbon Combo</strong> with an AMS for four-colour printing. The plain X1 (without the &quot;Carbon&quot; parts) had the chamber camera only as an option.</p>
<p>If you are buying new today, look at its successors in our <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-3d-printers/">Bambu Lab 3D printers overview</a> – the <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-p2s/">Bambu Lab P2S</a> has the same 256 mm build volume.</p>
