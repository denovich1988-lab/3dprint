<h2>Ender 3 V2 Neo quick start</h2>
<p><strong>Assembly.</strong> The printer arrives in three main parts: the base, the gantry frame, and the screen with the spool holder. Fix the gantry to the base with M5×45 screws, then mount the screen and spool holder. Plug in the X, Y/E and Z motors and the X limit switch using the yellow labels.</p>
<p><strong>Power.</strong> Set the voltage switch before you plug in: 115 V for 100–120 V mains, 230 V (the default) for 200–240 V. The manual warns that a wrong setting can damage the power supply.</p>
<p><strong>Check the wheels.</strong> If a pulley slips or binds, turn its eccentric nut until it rolls smoothly without play.</p>
<p><strong>Level.</strong> Choose Leveling on the screen to probe 16 points, then set the Z offset under Prepare → Z-offset so the nozzle just grips a sheet of A4 paper.</p>
<p><strong>Load filament.</strong> Heat the nozzle, cut the filament end at an angle, press the extruder lever and push until plastic flows from the nozzle.</p>
<p><strong>First print.</strong> Install the slicer, choose the Ender-3 V2 Neo profile, slice and save the G-code to the card. Use only Latin letters and digits in file names.</p>
<h2>Maintenance the manual recommends</h2>
<p>The manual keeps maintenance basic: with the power off, wipe the printer with a dry cloth, clear dust and debris from the guide rails, and clean the hot nozzle with a suitable tool. It does not give a lubrication or belt schedule. For more upkeep tips that apply to the same design family, see our <a href="/printers/ender-3-v2-manual/">Ender 3 V2 manual guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Common problems and fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>First layer does not stick or the nozzle scrapes the bed:</strong> redo the auto levelling and adjust the Z offset.</li>
<li><strong>Printer shuts off or loses position:</strong> do not move the nozzle or bed by hand while the printer is on.</li>
<li><strong>Power supply damage:</strong> the manual warns that a wrong voltage setting can burn out the power supply, so check the switch before the first switch-on.</li>
<li><strong>File does not show on the screen:</strong> rename it using only Latin letters and digits.</li>
</ul>
