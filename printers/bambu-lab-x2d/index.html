Bambu Lab X2D – dual-nozzle specs, launch price and who it suits

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • Bambu Lab announced the X2D on 14 April 2026 as an enclosed printer with two switchable nozzles.
  • Its main nozzle prints 256 × 256 × 260 mm; dual-nozzle prints use 235.5 × 256 × 256 mm.
  • It launched at US$649, or US$899 as the X2D Combo with an AMS 2 Pro.

The Bambu X2D (Bambu Lab X2D) is an enclosed dual-nozzle 3D printer with a 256 × 256 × 260 mm main build volume, a chamber that heats up to 65 °C and three-stage air filtration. Bambu Lab announced it on 14 April 2026, bringing H2-series ideas such as the servo extruder and two nozzles to a desktop-size printer.

Who the Bambu Lab X2D suits

The X2D is for makers who want an enclosed printer for everyday and engineering filaments, and who print parts that need supports. With two nozzles, one can print the model and the other a support material that breaks away cleanly. It also helps PLA and PETG multi-colour prints by cutting the amount of purged filament. Bambu Lab rates the chamber noise below 50 dB.

Its main extruder is a direct-drive PMSM servo; the second nozzle is an auxiliary Bowden extruder, which is slower (TechRadar measured it at 200 mm/s). Dynamic flow calibration works on the main extruder only. Bambu Lab sells an optional Vision Encoder for motion accuracy.

The Bambu Lab H2D is larger and hotter; the Bambu Lab P2S is the single-nozzle option with the same 256 mm footprint.

First setup and connection

Setup follows Bambu Lab's usual order: remove the transport screws and packing, connect the AMS 2 Pro if you bought the Combo, power on and let the calibration wizard run. You slice in Bambu Studio, send prints over Wi-Fi and watch them in the Bambu Handy app. LAN mode and a USB drive work for offline printing.

Manual and firmware

Bambu Lab publishes its printer manuals, maintenance and troubleshooting guides on the Bambu Lab Wiki, organised by printer series. Firmware updates install from the touchscreen, Bambu Handy or Bambu Studio.

Questions and answers

When was the Bambu Lab X2D released?

Bambu Lab announced the X2D on 14 April 2026, and it went on sale at launch.

How much did the Bambu X2D cost at launch?

US$649 for the X2D and US$899 for the X2D Combo with an AMS 2 Pro (US prices before tax). In the EU the launch prices were €629 and €849 including tax.

How do the two nozzles on the X2D work?

The left nozzle is a direct-drive main extruder; the right is an auxiliary Bowden nozzle. The printer switches between them mechanically, which suits break-away support material and two-colour prints with less purging.

Does the X2D have a heated chamber?

Yes. Bambu Lab lists a Heat Mode that warms the chamber up to 65 °C and a Cool Mode for PLA.
