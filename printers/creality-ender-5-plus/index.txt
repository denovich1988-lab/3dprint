<h2>Who the Ender 5 Plus suits</h2>
<p>It suits makers who need a big build volume for cosplay helmets, props or long parts and are happy to tune an open printer. It has no Wi-Fi, camera or enclosure. Reviewers noted the BL Touch sits about 7 cm left of the nozzle, so it cannot probe the full bed.</p>

<h2>Assembling the Ender 5 Plus</h2>
<p>The user manual describes a semi-assembled kit:</p>
<ol>
<li>Fix three aluminium profiles to the base with M5×25 screws.</li>
<li>Mount the top frame; tighten the top screws before the side screws.</li>
<li>Fit the dual Z-axis frame and check that the Y axis moves smoothly.</li>
<li>Fix the hot bed to the Z support plate with eight M4×10 screws.</li>
<li>Fit the filament holder, connect all cables and set the voltage switch for your mains supply.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> check every connector against the wiring diagram in the manual before the first power-on.</p>
<h2>Levelling the Ender 5 Plus</h2>
<p>Level the four corners by hand first, using the knobs and a sheet of A4 paper, with a nozzle gap of about 0.2 mm. Then home the printer and run the auto level, which probes 16 points with the BL Touch.</p>
<h2>Loading filament and printing</h2>
<p>Preheat with the PLA or ABS preset, hold the extruder lever and push 1.75 mm filament through the run-out sensor into the extruder until it flows from the nozzle. Copy sliced G-code to the SD card and print from the touchscreen. To check a sliced file first, use our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Ender 5 Plus manual</h2>
<p>Creality lists the Ender-5 Plus user manual in the Download Center on its website under &quot;Ender-5 Plus 3D Printer&quot;. It covers component identification, frame assembly, cable connection, screen menus, loading filament, levelling, software installation and circuit wiring.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>The download page offers <strong>Marlin 2.0</strong> firmware for the <strong>V4.2.2</strong> mainboard; early printers shipped with an older board, so match the file to your board. The current slicer is <strong>Creality Print</strong>; older units came with Cura or Creality Slicer.</p>
