<h2>Who the Ender 3 Max suits</h2>
<p>It suits hobbyists who need a big bed for helmets, cosplay parts or long brackets on a budget and are happy to tune a classic Cartesian printer. It is less suited to beginners who want automatic levelling or fast printing – it has neither, and a large bed-slinger moves a heavy bed back and forth.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Ender 3 Max</h2>
<p>Creality&#39;s manual describes a semi-assembled kit. At a high level:</p>
<ol>
<li>Place the gantry frame on the base and fix it with the four long screws.</li>
<li>Mount the power supply and the screen, then fit the spool holder.</li>
<li>Connect the X, E and Z motors and the limit switches using the labelled cables.</li>
<li>Set the power supply switch to your mains voltage (115 or 230 V), then power on.</li>
<li>Level the bed with paper at the four corners and the centre.</li>
<li>Heat the nozzle and load 1.75 mm filament through the extruder lever.</li>
<li>Slice a model and print from the SD card.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Bed levelling on the Ender 3 Max</h2>
<p>Home the axes, disable the steppers and move the nozzle over each corner. Turn the knob under that corner until a sheet of A4 paper drags slightly between nozzle and glass. Go round twice, then check the centre. The large 300 mm bed needs this more often than a small one.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> if you add a BL Touch probe, you also need firmware built for it.</p>

<h2>Connecting the Ender 3 Max</h2>
<p>There is no Wi-Fi. You print from an <strong>SD card</strong>, or over a <strong>USB cable</strong> from a computer running a host program. Check a sliced file before a long print with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Manual, firmware and slicer</h2>
<p>Creality publishes the Ender-3 Max user manual and firmware in the Creality download centre on its website. The printer uses Creality firmware for the 4.2.2 board; there are separate files for the STM and GD chip versions, so check which chip your board has before flashing.</p>
<p>The manual lists Creality Slicer, Cura, Repetier-Host and Simplify3D as slicers. It reads STL, OBJ and AMF models – convert other files with our <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a>.</p>
