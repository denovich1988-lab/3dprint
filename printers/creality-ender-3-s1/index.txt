<h2>Who the Ender 3 S1 suits</h2>
<p>The S1 suits beginners and tinkerers who want an affordable, upgradable bed-slinger that handles flexible TPU well thanks to its direct-drive extruder. It is slower than modern CoreXY printers and has no Wi-Fi or camera; if speed matters, look at the <a href="/printers/creality-ender-3-v3-se/">Ender-3 V3 SE</a> or <a href="/printers/creality-ender-3-v3/">Ender-3 V3</a>.</p>
<h2>Key features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Sprite extruder:</strong> dual-gear direct drive with a 1:3.5 gear ratio and up to 80 N of push force, according to Creality.</li>
<li><strong>Dual Z axis:</strong> two lead screws and two motors keep the gantry level.</li>
<li><strong>Build plate:</strong> removable PC-coated spring steel sheet on a magnetic base.</li>
<li><strong>Mainboard:</strong> 32-bit silent board.</li>
<li><strong>Extras:</strong> ports for an LED light and a laser engraving module.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Setting up the Ender 3 S1</h2>
<p>Creality says the S1 comes 96% pre-assembled. The quick start guide covers:</p>
<ol>
<li>Mounting the Sprite extruder assembly on the X carriage with four screws.</li>
<li>Fitting the gantry into the base with M5×45 screws.</li>
<li>Attaching the display bracket and screen, then the spool holder.</li>
<li>Connecting the cables and setting the voltage switch (115 V or 230 V) to match your mains.</li>
<li>Powering on, running auto levelling and setting the Z offset.</li>
<li>Printing a test G-code file from the SD card.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Ender 3 S1</h2>
<p>Copy sliced G-code to the <strong>SD card</strong>, or print over a <strong>USB-C</strong> cable from a computer. There is no built-in network connection.</p>
<h2>Ender 3 S1 manual</h2>
<p>Creality publishes the Ender-3 S1 user manual on the Ender-3 S1 download page in the Download Center on its website, alongside firmware and Creality Print. A printed quick start guide comes in the box.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Creality&#39;s S1 firmware is built for STM32F401 mainboards, so check the chip first. Format an SD card with a 4096 allocation unit size, copy the update folder to it, insert it with the printer off, then switch on and wait for the update to finish. The screen firmware is updated separately. Slice in <strong>Creality Print</strong>; check a sliced file in our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a> before printing.</p>
