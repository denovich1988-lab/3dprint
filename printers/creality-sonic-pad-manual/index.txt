<h2>Quick start: the setup wizard</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Connect:</strong> power on the pad and connect it to the printer with the USB cable.</li>
<li><strong>Basics:</strong> choose language and region, join Wi-Fi if you want it, accept the privacy policy and name the device.</li>
<li><strong>Pick your printer</strong> from the list of preconfigured Creality models.</li>
<li><strong>Flash the printer:</strong> the wizard installs firmware on the printer&#39;s mainboard, from an SD/TF card or over USB. Boards with an ATmega2560 chip cannot use the card method, and some ATmega1284P boards need a bootloader update first.</li>
<li><strong>Self-test:</strong> let the wizard check the printer, then tap to finish.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Input shaping with the G-sensor</h2>
<p>Klipper reduces ringing by measuring resonance. Mount the G-sensor on the hot end (Creality supplies a bracket model; orientation does not matter) and run the resonance measurement. The manual has separate steps for bed-slinger printers like the Ender-3 S1 and printers where the bed does not move on Y, like the Ender-5 Plus. Hot end and bed PID calibration results save automatically.</p>
<h2>Web control and thumbnails</h2>
<p>Open Fluidd in a browser at the pad&#39;s IP address, or Mainsail on port 8819. The manual explains how to add preview thumbnails in Cura, PrusaSlicer, SuperSlicer and Creality Print, so the pad shows a picture of each file.</p>
<h2>Firmware updates</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online:</strong> Configure → Other Settings, with at least 500 MB of free space.</li>
<li><strong>Offline:</strong> download the package from the Download Center, unzip it to the root of a USB drive and plug it into the pad.</li>
</ul>
<p>Creality&#39;s updates have added more printers, SSH root access, editable bed-mesh points and USB mouse and keyboard support.</p>
