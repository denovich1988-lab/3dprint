Flashforge Adventurer 5M Pro – specs, setup and 5M vs 5M Pro

Updated November 24, 2026

Key points
  • The Flashforge Adventurer 5M Pro is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 220 × 220 × 220 mm build volume.
  • Flashforge announced it on 1 September 2023 at an introductory price of US$599.
  • It adds a full enclosure, HEPA13 and activated-carbon filtration and a built-in camera to the open Adventurer 5M.

The Flashforge Adventurer 5M Pro is a fully enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 220 × 220 × 220 mm build volume, HEPA13 and activated-carbon filtration and a built-in camera. Flashforge announced it on 1 September 2023 at an introductory price of US$599 and opened orders on 5 September. It is the enclosed version of the open-frame Flashforge Adventurer 5M.

Who the Adventurer 5M Pro suits

The 5M Pro suits home users and classrooms that want a fast, compact printer they can use indoors with ABS and ASA as well as PLA and PETG. The enclosure keeps heat in for warp-prone materials, and the filters treat the air inside. Flashforge lists noise of about 50 dB in low-noise mode and 55 dB in standard mode.

Quick-release nozzle and filaments

The nozzle swaps without tools. Flashforge offers 0.25, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm sizes; 0.4 mm is fitted and a 0.6 mm nozzle comes in the box. For carbon-fibre filaments (PLA-CF, PETG-CF) and TPU, Flashforge recommends the 0.6 or 0.8 mm nozzle.

Setting up the Adventurer 5M Pro

Flashforge's quick start guide describes these steps:

  1. Unpack the printer, remove the foam and take out the four build-plate locking screws with a 2.5 mm Allen key.
  2. Fit the spool holder to the rear panel with two screws.
  3. Power on, choose a language and join your Wi-Fi network.
  4. Bind the printer to your Flashforge account by scanning the on-screen QR code with the Flash Maker app, or skip this step.
  5. Let the printer run its first automatic calibration.
  6. Load PLA through the run-out sensor, apply glue to the plate and print the built-in test file.

Connecting the Adventurer 5M Pro

Send files over Wi-Fi or Ethernet from Orca-Flashforge or FlashPrint, or print from a USB drive. The Flash Maker app shares the same account as Orca-Flashforge and shows the camera feed for remote monitoring and time-lapses.

Adventurer 5M Pro manual

Flashforge publishes the Adventurer 5M Pro quick start guide on the Flashforge Wiki under Adventurer Series → User Manual. Firmware and help articles are in the Flashforge Help Center under Adventurer 5M Series. Our Adventurer 5M manual guide covers the shared parts of the series.

Firmware and software

When connected to the internet, the printer prompts you when new firmware is available; accept and restart. You can also check manually from the printer's info screen. Slice in Orca-Flashforge, FlashPrint 5 or Orca Slicer. To look at a sliced file before printing, open it in our G-code to STL tool.

Questions and answers

Is the Flashforge Adventurer 5M Pro 3D printer enclosed?

Yes. It has a fully enclosed chamber with HEPA13 and activated-carbon filtration, which is why Flashforge lists ABS and ASA for it.

What is the difference between the Adventurer 5M and the 5M Pro?

Both have a 220 mm cube build volume, 600 mm/s travel speed and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration. The 5M Pro adds the enclosure, the HEPA and carbon filters and a built-in camera. Launch prices were US$399 for the 5M and US$599 for the 5M Pro.

Which slicer works with the Adventurer 5M Pro?

Flashforge lists FlashPrint 5, Orca-Flashforge and Orca Slicer. The Orca-Flashforge installer is on the USB drive in the box.

How do I update the Adventurer 5M Pro firmware?

When the printer is on Wi-Fi or Ethernet it shows a prompt when an update is available; select Yes and restart. You can also check from the printer's info screen under Firmware update.
