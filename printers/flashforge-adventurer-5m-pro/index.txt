<h2>Quick-release nozzle and filaments</h2>
<p>The nozzle swaps without tools. Flashforge offers 0.25, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm sizes; 0.4 mm is fitted and a 0.6 mm nozzle comes in the box. For carbon-fibre filaments (PLA-CF, PETG-CF) and TPU, Flashforge recommends the 0.6 or 0.8 mm nozzle.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Adventurer 5M Pro</h2>
<p>Flashforge&#39;s quick start guide describes these steps:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer, remove the foam and take out the four build-plate locking screws with a 2.5 mm Allen key.</li>
<li>Fit the spool holder to the rear panel with two screws.</li>
<li>Power on, choose a language and join your Wi-Fi network.</li>
<li>Bind the printer to your Flashforge account by scanning the on-screen QR code with the Flash Maker app, or skip this step.</li>
<li>Let the printer run its first automatic calibration.</li>
<li>Load PLA through the run-out sensor, apply glue to the plate and print the built-in test file.</li>
</ol>

<p>Send files over <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> or <strong>Ethernet</strong> from Orca-Flashforge or FlashPrint, or print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>. The Flash Maker app shares the same account as Orca-Flashforge and shows the camera feed for remote monitoring and time-lapses.</p>
