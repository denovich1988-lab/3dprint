Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 – specs, CANVAS and setup

Updated November 23, 2026

Key points
  • The Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume and a 350 °C hardened steel nozzle.
  • The Centauri Carbon 2 Combo, with the CANVAS four-colour system, launched on 26 January 2026 at US$449.
  • It adds HEPA and activated carbon filtration and a 5-inch touchscreen.

The Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume, a 350 °C hardened steel nozzle and support for Elegoo's CANVAS four-colour system. Elegoo launched the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo, which includes CANVAS, on 26 January 2026 at a launch price of US$449 in the US. It follows the original Elegoo Centauri Carbon.

Who the Centauri Carbon 2 suits

The Centauri Carbon 2 suits budget buyers who want an enclosed printer for PLA and PETG that can also print ABS, PC and fibre-filled filaments, with the option of four-colour printing. The closed chamber and the HEPA and carbon filter make it a better fit for indoor rooms than an open printer.

The CANVAS multi-colour system

CANVAS mounts four spools on the side of the printer and feeds them through PTFE tubes to a four-colour hub on the print head. Elegoo lists automatic loading, filament detection, auto refill and tangle detection, and a spool holder that rewinds unused filament. Elegoo's RFID-tagged spools are recognised automatically and get recommended settings; other spools are set on the touchscreen. The spool holder fits spools with a 53–58 mm centre hole.

Setting up the Centauri Carbon 2

Elegoo ships the printer with the touchscreen, spool holder, tools, a nozzle wiper, a filament sample and a USB flash drive. At a high level:

  1. Unpack the printer and fit the touchscreen and the spool holder.
  2. Connect the filament run-out sensor cable and, on the Combo, mount CANVAS and its tubes.
  3. Switch on and run the full auto calibration – auto Z offset, pressure advance and vibration compensation.
  4. Load filament and print a test file from the USB drive.

Tip: the partial levelling option probes only the area under the print, which Elegoo says cuts levelling time by up to 75%.

Connecting the Centauri Carbon 2

Elegoo's ELEGOO Matrix app starts and monitors prints remotely, and the built-in camera gives a live view and time-lapses. You can also print offline from the USB flash drive.

Questions and answers

When was the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 released?

Elegoo launched the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo on 26 January 2026 and opened orders on its website the same day.

How much did the Elegoo Centauri Carbon 2 cost at launch?

The Centauri Carbon 2 Combo launched at US$449, €439 or £339.

What is the difference between the Centauri Carbon and Centauri Carbon 2?

Both have a 256 mm cube build volume and 500 mm/s. The Carbon 2 raises the nozzle limit from 320 °C to 350 °C, has a 5-inch screen instead of 4.3-inch and adds HEPA plus activated carbon filtration.

Does the CANVAS system work with any filament?

CANVAS feeds four spools, but automatic colour and material recognition only works with Elegoo's RFID-tagged spools. Other spools are assigned on the touchscreen.
