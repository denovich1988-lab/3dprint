<h2>Elegoo printer series in the Download Center</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Mars</strong> (resin): from the original Mars to the Mars 5 Ultra. See our <a href="/printers/elegoo-mars/">Elegoo Mars</a> and <a href="/printers/elegoo-mars-3/">Elegoo Mars 3</a> pages.</li>
<li><strong>Saturn</strong> (larger resin): Saturn to Saturn 4 Ultra 16K. See the <a href="/printers/elegoo-saturn-4-ultra/">Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Neptune</strong> (open FDM): Neptune 2 to Neptune 4 Max. See the <a href="/printers/elegoo-neptune-4/">Elegoo Neptune 4</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Centauri</strong> (enclosed CoreXY FDM): Centauri Carbon and Centauri Carbon 2 Combo. See the <a href="/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/">Elegoo Centauri Carbon</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> manuals change between hardware revisions. Some Neptune models have separate firmware for ribbon-cable and Type-C mainboards, so check the label on your printer before you download.</p>
<h2>What a typical Elegoo FDM manual covers</h2>
<p>Taking the Neptune 4 Pro manual as an example, the sections are:</p>
<ol>
<li>Machine parameters and components.</li>
<li>Packing list.</li>
<li>Setup and installation.</li>
<li>Screen operation.</li>
<li>Levelling (manual corners, then automatic).</li>
<li>Model test.</li>
<li>Resume printing after an interruption.</li>
<li>Software installation and LAN printing.</li>
<li>Mainboard wiring diagram and a troubleshooting guide.</li>
</ol>
<p>The Centauri Carbon 2 Combo manual follows a similar order for an enclosed printer: specs, components, unboxing, installing the printer and its multi-colour unit, loading filament, the first print, ElegooSlicer and LAN printing.</p>
<h2>Quick start on an Elegoo FDM printer</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Unbox:</strong> remove the transport screws or locks the manual marks. On the Centauri Carbon 2 Combo, three screws lock the bed.</li>
<li><strong>Assemble:</strong> Neptune printers need the gantry fitted to the base; Centauri printers ship assembled.</li>
<li><strong>Level:</strong> on the Neptune 4 Pro, set the centre height with paper, adjust the corners, run the automatic mesh and set the Z offset.</li>
<li><strong>Load filament</strong> from the screen menu and print the test model from the USB drive.</li>
</ul>
<h2>What a typical Elegoo resin manual covers</h2>
<p>Resin manuals such as the Mars 3 manual cover safety with resin, levelling the build plate with a sheet of paper, filling the vat, the first print from the USB drive, slicing in CHITUBOX and washing and curing the parts.</p>
<h2>Maintenance Elegoo recommends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Wipe the printer with a dry cloth while it is switched off.</li>
<li>Lubricate the X/Y rods and the Z lead screws as the manual says.</li>
<li>Clean the nozzle while it is warm.</li>
<li>On resin printers, keep sharp tools away from the release film and clean the vat when you change resin.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Firmware for each model is in its Download Center entry with update notes. For the Saturn, Elegoo&#39;s guide says to copy the files to the root of a USB drive and install the .LCD file before the .CBD file. Elegoo lists ElegooSlicer, OrcaSlicer, ELEGOO Cura, CHITUBOX and ELEGOO SatelLite as slicers.
