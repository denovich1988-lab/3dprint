28:T1301,<p><strong>The Creality CFS (Creality Filament System) is a sealed four-spool filament box that lets Creality K2-series printers and the Creality Hi print in up to 16 colours when four units are chained.</strong> Creality announced it alongside the K2 Plus in April 2024, and the K2 Plus + CFS combo went on pre-sale in June 2024. Each unit stores, feeds and switches filament automatically, including refilling from a matching spool when one runs out.</p>

<h2>Which printers work with the Creality CFS</h2>
<p>Creality lists the <strong>K2 series</strong> – <a href="/printers/creality-k2-plus/">K2 Plus</a>, <a href="/printers/creality-k2-pro/">K2 Pro</a> and K2 – and the <strong>Creality Hi</strong> as compatible. The K2 combos and the Hi Combo ship with one CFS in the box. See our <a href="/printers/creality-k2-combo/">Creality K2 combo guide</a> for how the combos differ.</p>
<p>K1-series printers, including the <a href="/printers/creality-k1-max/">Creality K1 Max</a> and <a href="/printers/creality-k1c/">K1C</a>, do not use the CFS. Creality sells the <strong>CFS-C</strong> for them instead (see below).</p>
<h2>Supported filaments and spools</h2>
<p>Creality lists PLA, PETG, ABS, ASA, PET and PLA-CF for the CFS. It does <strong>not</strong> support flexible filament such as TPU 95A, damp BVOH or PVA, or warped cardboard spools. Spools must be 1 kg size, 197–202 mm across and 42–68 mm wide.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> cardboard spools can deform and jam the feeder. Respool the filament onto a plastic spool or print an adapter ring before loading it.</p>
<h2>RFID and non-Creality filament</h2>
<p>When you load a Creality spool with an RFID tag, the CFS reads its material and colour and sends them to the printer, so the slicer shows the right filament. RFID is optional: for other brands you choose the brand, type and colour on the printer screen yourself.</p>
<h2>Humidity and drying</h2>
<p>The CFS is a sealed box with desiccant and a small display that shows the temperature and humidity inside. It keeps dry filament dry but does <strong>not</strong> heat or actively dry spools. If you print moisture-sensitive materials, dry them first, or look at the CFS 2, which dries at up to 85 °C.</p>
<h2>How to load filament into the CFS</h2>
<ol>
<li>Open the lid and place a spool in a free slot.</li>
<li>Push the filament end into the slot&#39;s PTFE tube until you feel resistance, then let go – the slot light turns on and the CFS pulls it in.</li>
<li>On the printer, select the slot and set the material if it has no RFID tag.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Extrude</strong> to feed it to the nozzle; <strong>Retract</strong> cuts the filament and pulls it back.</li>
</ol>
<p>For connecting a second or third unit, follow the installation steps on the Creality Wiki for your printer.</p>

<h2>CFS compared with other multi-colour systems</h2>
<p>The CFS works much like the <a href="/printers/bambu-lab-ams/">Bambu Lab AMS</a>: four spools per unit, chained units and one shared nozzle, so multi-colour prints still create purge waste. To check a multi-colour project before printing, open it in our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>, or compare compatible printers in the <a href="/printers/compare/">printer comparison tool</a>.</p>
