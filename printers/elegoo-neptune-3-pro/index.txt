<h2>Who the Neptune 3 Pro suits</h2>
<p>It suits beginners and hobbyists who want a low-cost printer with a direct-drive extruder (good for flexible TPU) and automatic levelling. It has no Wi-Fi, camera or enclosure, so files go in by microSD card. Elegoo lists PLA, TPU, PETG, ABS and ASA, but ABS and ASA are harder to print without an enclosure.</p>
<h2>Neptune 3 Pro features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Dual Z axis:</strong> two lead screws and two motors keep the gantry level.</li>
<li><strong>Direct-drive extruder:</strong> stainless steel dual gears with a 3:1 reduction.</li>
<li><strong>PEI spring-steel plate:</strong> magnetic and flexible, so prints pop off.</li>
<li><strong>Silent drivers and smart fans:</strong> Elegoo says the fans stop when the hot end cools below 50 °C.</li>
<li><strong>LED light bar</strong> on the gantry and a filament run-out sensor.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Setting up the Neptune 3 Pro</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer; the main parts come pre-assembled and Elegoo includes the tools.</li>
<li>Mount the touchscreen on its bracket and connect its cable.</li>
<li>Check the voltage setting for your region, then power on.</li>
<li>Run <strong>auto levelling</strong> from the screen with the steel plate in place – the inductive sensor only detects the metal plate.</li>
<li>Set the <strong>Z-offset</strong> and save it. Elegoo suggests re-levelling about once a week, or after changing bed temperature or filament.</li>
<li>Load filament through the run-out sensor and print a test file from the microSD card.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> if your first layer is too squashed or too thin, adjust the Z-offset in small steps rather than re-tightening anything.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Neptune 3 Pro</h2>
<p>There is no Wi-Fi. Slice on your computer, copy the G-code to the <strong>microSD card</strong> and print from the screen, or connect a computer by <strong>USB cable</strong>. To check a sliced file before printing, open it in our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Neptune 3 Pro manual</h2>
<p>Elegoo publishes the Neptune 3 Pro user guide on its website, on the Neptune 3 Pro/Plus/Max support files page and in the ELEGOO Download Center. The same page links the firmware and ELEGOO Cura.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>To update, format a microSD card of 32 GB or less as FAT32. Copy the mainboard firmware to the root, insert it and restart; the printer loads it in about 15 seconds. The screen firmware goes on a card inserted behind the screen&#39;s back cover. Keep mainboard and screen versions matched, and level again afterwards because flashing resets levelling. Slice in <strong>ELEGOO Cura</strong>, which includes Neptune 3 Pro profiles and screen thumbnails.</p>
