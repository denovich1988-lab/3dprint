Flashforge Adventurer 3 – specs, setup, camera and FlashPrint

Updated November 24, 2026

Key points
  • The Flashforge Adventurer 3 is a compact, fully enclosed FDM printer with a 150 × 150 × 150 mm build volume.
  • It has a quick-swap 0.4 mm nozzle up to 240 °C, a removable flexible heated bed, a built-in camera and Wi-Fi, Ethernet and USB.
  • Flashforge unveiled it at CES 2018; it prints PLA and ABS and is sliced in FlashPrint.

The Flashforge Adventurer 3 is a compact, fully enclosed FDM 3D printer with a 150 × 150 × 150 mm build volume, a quick-swap nozzle and a built-in camera. Flashforge unveiled it at CES 2018 as a plug-and-play printer for homes, offices and classrooms. It prints PLA and ABS and connects over Wi-Fi, Ethernet or a USB stick.

Who the Adventurer 3 suits

It suits beginners, families and classrooms that want a quiet, closed printer for small PLA parts with little setup. The enclosure keeps fingers away from hot parts and helps with ABS. The build volume is small and the spool holder only fits smaller reels, so it is less suited to large prints. Flashforge's newer Adventurer 5M is faster and larger.

Adventurer 3 features

  • Quick-swap nozzle: a buckle release lets you change the 0.4 mm nozzle without tools.
  • Flexible heated plate: removable and bendable, so prints pop off.
  • No levelling out of the box, according to Flashforge.
  • Filament run-out detection and automatic filament loading.
  • Built-in camera for remote monitoring.
  • Flashforge quotes a noise level of about 45 dB.

Setting up the Adventurer 3

  1. Unpack the printer and remove the packing pieces inside the chamber.
  2. Power on and follow the touchscreen setup.
  3. Fit a small 1.75 mm PLA spool on the internal holder and use the screen's load function to feed it.
  4. Connect to Wi-Fi or Ethernet, or use a USB stick.
  5. Print the sample file to check the first layer.

Tip: if prints do not stick, clean the flexible plate and make sure it is seated flat on the bed before starting.

Connecting the Adventurer 3

Send files from FlashPrint over Wi-Fi or Ethernet, print from a USB stick, or use cloud printing through FlashCloud or Polar Cloud. The camera feed lets you watch prints from your computer. To check a sliced file, open it in our G-code to STL tool.

Adventurer 3 manual

The quick start guide that ships with the printer points users to Flashforge's official website for the latest user guide and quick start guide. Downloads for the Adventurer family are in the Download Center on Flashforge's website.

Firmware and software

Slice with FlashPrint, Flashforge's free slicer with standard and expert modes. Get firmware only from Flashforge's website and follow the instructions supplied with it.

Questions and answers

What is the Flashforge Adventurer 3 build volume?

150 × 150 × 150 mm inside a fully enclosed chamber.

Does the Flashforge Adventurer 3 need bed levelling?

Flashforge says it needs no levelling out of the box. The heated build plate is flexible and removable, so you bend it to release prints.

What filament does the Adventurer 3 use?

1.75 mm PLA and ABS. The built-in holder only fits smaller spools, and Flashforge warns that print quality is not guaranteed with third-party filament.

What is the difference between the Adventurer 3 and Adventurer 3 Lite?

Both have the same 150 mm cube volume, screen and connections. The Lite drops the built-in camera and the filament run-out sensor.
