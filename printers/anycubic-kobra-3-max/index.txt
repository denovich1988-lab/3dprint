<h2>Who the Kobra 3 Max suits</h2>
<p>The Kobra 3 Max suits makers who print large single parts – helmets, costume armour, furniture parts, big planters – or several smaller parts in one go, and want multi-colour without a second printer. As an open bed-slinger with a 90 °C bed, it is best with PLA, PETG and TPU. Anycubic lists ABS and ASA as workable but PA and PC as not recommended.</p>
<h2>The ACE Pro on the Kobra 3 Max Combo</h2>
<p>The ACE Pro holds four spools, feeds them to the print head and dries filament – also while printing. Two ACE Pro units with an eight-colour hub give eight colours. Expect purge waste on colour changes; Anycubic&#39;s slicer lets you adjust purge volumes.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> dry PETG and TPU before long prints – see our <a href="/blog/filament-dryer/">filament dryer guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Kobra 3 Max</h2>
<p>Based on Anycubic&#39;s quick start guide, at a high level:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unbox and remove the transport screws from the gantry.</li>
<li>Set the gantry on the base, fix it with three screws per side and add the left and right support rods.</li>
<li>Plug in the labelled cables, mount the print head and fit the touchscreen.</li>
<li>For the Combo: fit the filament cutter and purge wiper, then connect the ACE Pro and its four PTFE tubes.</li>
<li>Power on, run the initial calibration with auto levelling and update the firmware.</li>
<li>Load filament into the ACE Pro, set type and colour on the screen, then slice and print.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Kobra 3 Max</h2>
<p>The printer joins <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> and works with the <strong>Anycubic app</strong> and Anycubic Cloud, with a <strong>LAN mode</strong> for local printing. You can also print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>. A camera for AI detection and time-lapse is optional.</p>
<h2>Manual, firmware and slicer</h2>
<p>Anycubic publishes the Kobra 3 Max quick start guide and the multi-language Kobra 3 Max Combo user manual on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D printers. A paper quick start guide and a USB stick with the manual come in the box. The printer runs Kobra OS; check the wiki for the current firmware.</p>
<p>Anycubic recommends <strong>Anycubic Slicer Next</strong>, based on OrcaSlicer; other slicers also work. Open projects from other slicers with our <a href="/3mf-to-stl/">3MF to STL converter</a>, or preview a sliced job with the <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Kobra 3 Max V2: what changed</h2>
<p>The Kobra 3 Max V2 keeps the 420 × 420 × 500 mm volume, 600 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s², and adds an all-metal hotend, a dual-gear extruder, a hardened steel nozzle and the ACE 2 Pro. Anycubic notes that the V2 hotend and print head do not fit the original Kobra 3 Max.</p>
