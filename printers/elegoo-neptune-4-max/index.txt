<h2>Who the Neptune 4 Max suits</h2>
<p>It suits makers who want the largest possible build area for the money and are comfortable with an open-frame machine. The bed moves front to back, so you need about 658 × 950 × 960 mm of space including the filament rack. Elegoo lists ABS, ASA and nylon, but without an enclosure those are harder to print than PLA, PETG and TPU.</p>
<h2>Neptune 4 Max features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Klipper firmware</strong> with input shaping and pressure advance for faster printing.</li>
<li><strong>Dual-gear direct extruder</strong> with a 5.2:1 reduction and a 300 °C high-temperature nozzle kit.</li>
<li><strong>121-point (11 × 11) automatic levelling</strong> with an inductive probe.</li>
<li><strong>Filament run-out detection and resume printing.</strong></li>
<li><strong>CNC-machined aluminium extrusion frame</strong> and a removable touchscreen.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Setting up the Neptune 4 Max</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the parts and assemble the gantry, screen and filament rack as shown in the user guide.</li>
<li>Check the voltage setting for your region, connect the cables and power on.</li>
<li>Run <strong>auto levelling</strong> from the screen; the probe maps the bed at 121 points.</li>
<li>Set the <strong>Z-offset</strong> while watching a test line.</li>
<li>Load filament through the run-out sensor and print the test file from the USB drive.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> on a bed this large, let it heat for a few minutes before levelling – the mesh is more accurate when the plate has reached its print temperature.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Neptune 4 Max</h2>
<p>Print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>, or connect the printer to your network over <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> or <strong>LAN</strong> and send jobs from your computer through the Klipper web interface. To preview a sliced file before you send it, open it in our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Neptune 4 Max manual</h2>
<p>Elegoo publishes the Neptune 4 Max user guide in the ELEGOO Download Center under the Neptune series. It covers machine parameters, component diagrams, the packing list, setup and installation, screen operation, auto levelling, the test print, resume printing, software installation, LAN printing, the mainboard wiring diagram and troubleshooting.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Firmware packages are in the ELEGOO Download Center, with step-by-step guides on the ELEGOO Wiki. Mainboard firmware is installed from a USB drive and the screen has its own firmware; update the mainboard first and re-level afterwards, because flashing clears the levelling data. Type-C versions also support over-the-air updates. The printer shipped with <strong>ELEGOO Cura</strong> on the USB drive; Elegoo&#39;s current FDM slicer is ELEGOO Slicer, based on OrcaSlicer.</p>
