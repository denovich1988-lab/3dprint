<h2>Centauri series: enclosed CoreXY printers</h2>
<p>The <a href="/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/">Elegoo Centauri Carbon</a> is a fully enclosed CoreXY printer with a die-cast aluminium frame, 500 mm/s top speed, 20,000 mm/s² acceleration and a 320 °C hardened steel nozzle for carbon-fibre filaments. The <a href="/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon-2/">Centauri Carbon 2</a> keeps the 256 mm cube volume, raises the nozzle to 350 °C and is sold as a combo with <strong>CANVAS</strong>, Elegoo&#39;s four-colour filament system. The <strong>Centauri 2</strong> is an open-frame version without the enclosure, camera or filter.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> choose an enclosed Centauri if you plan to print ABS, ASA or nylon blends; open-frame printers are best kept to PLA, PETG and TPU.</p>
<h2>Neptune 4 series: Klipper bed-slingers</h2>
<p>The Neptune 4 printers are open-frame bed-slingers with Klipper firmware pre-installed, dual-gear direct extruders and 121-point automatic levelling. The <strong>Neptune 4 Pro</strong> is the compact model, the <strong>Neptune 4 Plus</strong> steps up to 320 × 320 × 385 mm, and the <a href="/printers/elegoo-neptune-4-max/">Elegoo Neptune 4 Max</a> is the largest at 420 × 420 × 480 mm. The older <a href="/printers/elegoo-neptune-3-pro/">Neptune 3 Pro</a> is a simpler microSD-card printer without Klipper.</p>
<h2>OrangeStorm Giga: large-format FDM</h2>
<p>The OrangeStorm Giga has an 800 × 800 × 1000 mm build volume, four separately heated 410 × 410 mm PEI bed sections and room for up to four printheads on the X axis. Elegoo rates it at 300 mm/s maximum and recommends 150 mm/s. It suits props, furniture parts and cosplay pieces that would otherwise be printed in sections.</p>
<h2>Mars and Saturn: resin printers</h2>
<ul>
<li>The <a href="/printers/elegoo-mars-5-ultra/">Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra</a> is a small 7-inch 9K resin printer with tilt release, an AI camera and Wi-Fi.</li>
<li>The <a href="/printers/elegoo-saturn-4-ultra/">Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra</a> uses a 10-inch 12K LCD with a larger 218.88 × 122.88 × 220 mm volume.</li>
<li>The <strong>Saturn 4 Ultra 16K</strong> keeps a 10-inch screen at 16K resolution (14 × 19 µm pixels) and heats the resin tank to 30 °C.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Jupiter 2: large resin printer</h2>
<p>The Jupiter 2, launched at RAPID + TCT 2026, is Elegoo&#39;s large-format resin printer with a 302 × 162 × 300 mm build volume, a 16K LCD, force-sensor auto levelling, a heated vat and a two-way resin pump fed from a 2 kg bottle.</p>
<h2>Elegoo software</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>ELEGOO Slicer (ElegooSlicer)</strong> – the FDM slicer for current printers, based on OrcaSlicer.</li>
<li><strong>ELEGOO Cura</strong> – supplied with older Neptune printers.</li>
<li><strong>SatelLite</strong> – Elegoo&#39;s free resin slicer for Windows and macOS; resin printers also work with CHITUBOX.</li>
<li><strong>Elegoo Matrix app</strong> – remote control of supported printers.</li>
</ul>
<p>Firmware, user manuals and slicers are in the <strong>ELEGOO Download Center</strong>, and how-to guides are on the <strong>ELEGOO Wiki</strong>. Before slicing, you can check a model in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> or a multi-colour project in the <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
<h2>How to choose an Elegoo printer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Filament or resin?</strong> Filament for functional parts and large objects; resin for miniatures and fine detail.</li>
<li><strong>Materials:</strong> for ABS, ASA or carbon-fibre filaments, choose an enclosed Centauri Carbon model.</li>
<li><strong>Size:</strong> Neptune 4 Plus or Max for big prints on a budget; OrangeStorm Giga for very large parts.</li>
<li><strong>Colours:</strong> Centauri Carbon 2 with CANVAS for four-colour printing.</li>
</ol>
