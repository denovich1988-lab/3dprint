<h2>Ender 3 S1 Pro specs explained</h2>
<p>The headline upgrade over the regular S1 is the hotend: the S1 Pro reaches <strong>300 °C</strong>, which opens up nylon (PA) and makes ABS and PETG easier. The <strong>Sprite extruder</strong> is an all-metal dual-gear direct drive weighing 334 g, so flexible TPU feeds reliably. Dual Z motors keep the gantry level.</p>
<p>Creality&#39;s store page lists a 110 °C heated bed, while the spec table in the user manual says 100 °C. Plan on about 100 °C to stay safe.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> without an enclosure, ABS and nylon warp easily. Print them in a draught-free room or build a simple cover.</p>
<h2>Who the Ender 3 S1 Pro suits</h2>
<p>It suits hobbyists who want a classic, open and repairable Ender with a better hotend and a touchscreen. It is slower than newer CoreXY printers and has no Wi-Fi or camera, so buyers who want app control and high speed should look at newer models in our <a href="/printers/creality-ender-3d-printers/">Creality Ender 3D printers guide</a>.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Ender 3 S1 Pro</h2>
<p>Creality says the printer arrives about 96% pre-assembled. Following the official manual, at a high level:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the parts and fit the gantry to the base.</li>
<li>Mount the touchscreen and the spool holder, then connect the cables.</li>
<li>Check the voltage setting matches your mains supply.</li>
<li>Run auto levelling with the CR Touch and set the Z offset.</li>
<li>Load filament and print the test model from the SD card.</li>
</ol>
<p>Our <a href="/printers/ender-3-s1-pro-manual/">Ender 3 S1 Pro manual guide</a> goes through each step in more detail.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Ender 3 S1 Pro</h2>
<p>The printer has <strong>no built-in Wi-Fi</strong>. Print from the SD card, or connect a computer over USB Type-C. For remote printing, Creality sells a separate Wi-Fi Box that works with the Creality Cloud app. Before printing a downloaded file, check it in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a>.</p>
<h2>Manual, firmware and slicer</h2>
<p>Creality publishes the Ender-3 S1 Pro user manual, the firmware with release notes and the Creality Print slicer in the Creality Download Center on creality.com (Support → Download → Ender-3 S1 Pro). The manual is also on the Creality Wiki under Ender Series. Read the release notes for the update steps before flashing firmware.</p>
<p><strong>Creality Print</strong> is the official slicer. The manual also lists Cura and other common slicers. To recover a model from a sliced file, use our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Ender 3 S1 Pro vs Ender 3 S1</h2>
<p>Both share the frame, the Sprite extruder and the 220 × 220 × 270 mm build volume. The S1 Pro adds the 300 °C hotend, the touchscreen, the PEI plate and the LED light bar. See the full comparison on our <a href="/printers/creality-ender-3-s1/">Ender 3 S1 page</a>.</p>
