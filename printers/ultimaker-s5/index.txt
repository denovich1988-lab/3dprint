<h2>Who the UltiMaker S5 suits</h2>
<p>The S5 suits offices, labs and classrooms that print functional parts in engineering materials and want dual extrusion with dissolvable or breakaway supports. It is a large, heavy machine with a closed front and an open top. For filtration and a closed top, UltiMaker sells the optional <strong>Air Manager</strong>; the <strong>S5 Pro Bundle</strong> combines the printer with the Air Manager and the <strong>Material Station</strong>, which stores up to six spools and keeps them dry with self-regenerating silica gel.</p>
<h2>Print cores explained</h2>
<p>The S5 has two print-core slots, and the idle nozzle lifts automatically so it does not drag over the print.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>AA</strong> – build materials and Breakaway support.</li>
<li><strong>BB</strong> – water-soluble support such as PVA.</li>
<li><strong>CC</strong> – composite and abrasive materials.</li>
</ul>
<p>Nozzle sizes are 0.25, 0.4, 0.6 and 0.8 mm. Swapping a core takes seconds and does not need tools.</p>
<h2>Setting up the UltiMaker S5</h2>
<ol>
<li>Unpack the printer, remove the packing materials and fit the spool holder, Bowden tubes and glass plate as described in the quick start.</li>
<li>Connect power (100–240 V) and follow the welcome setup on the touchscreen.</li>
<li>Insert the print cores and let the printer run <strong>active levelling</strong> and XY offset calibration.</li>
<li>Load material: the NFC spool holder recognises UltiMaker spools automatically.</li>
<li>Connect the printer to your network and add it in UltiMaker Cura.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> run the XY offset calibration again whenever you swap a print core, so both nozzles line up on dual-material prints.</p>
<h2>Connecting the S5</h2>
<p>The S5 connects over <strong>Wi-Fi (2.4 GHz)</strong>, <strong>Ethernet</strong> or a <strong>USB stick</strong>. On a network, you can send jobs from UltiMaker Cura or manage a print queue in <strong>UltiMaker Digital Factory</strong>, locally or through the cloud. The built-in camera lets you watch prints remotely. Before printing, you can check models in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> or <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
<h2>UltiMaker S5 manual</h2>
<p>UltiMaker publishes the combined UltiMaker S3 and S5 user manual on the UltiMaker support site. It covers safety, installation, operation, maintenance, troubleshooting and warranty. The maintenance schedule is based on about 1,500 print hours a year: monthly cleaning of nozzles and the build plate, quarterly checks of belt tension and axle play, and a yearly replacement of the Bowden tubes.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Online update:</strong> connect the printer by Wi-Fi or Ethernet, then go to Maintenance → Update firmware and choose a stable or latest release.</li>
<li><strong>Offline update:</strong> download the firmware file for your model revision (R1 or R2) from UltiMaker, copy it to the root of a USB stick and choose Maintenance → Update firmware.</li>
</ul>
<p>Do not switch off the printer or remove the stick during an update. UltiMaker says the original S5 R1 (built from 2018 to early 2020) has reached end of life and no longer gets firmware updates. Slice with <strong>UltiMaker Cura</strong>, which is free for Windows, macOS and Linux.</p>
<h2>UltiMaker S5 vs S7</h2>
<p>The S7 keeps the same 330 × 240 × 300 mm volume but adds an inductive levelling sensor, a flexible PEI-coated magnetic build plate instead of glass, a built-in air filter and a single glass door. If you already use an S5, both printers use print cores and Cura.</p>
