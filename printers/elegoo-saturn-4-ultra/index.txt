<h2>Who the Saturn 4 Ultra suits</h2>
<p>The Saturn 4 Ultra suits makers who print miniatures, tabletop terrain, dental-style models or small detailed parts. Resin printing gives much finer surface detail than filament printers but needs gloves, ventilation and post-processing (washing and curing). If you want large functional parts in PLA or PETG, an FDM printer such as the <a href="/printers/elegoo-centauri-carbon/">Elegoo Centauri Carbon</a> is the better fit.</p>
<h2>Key features</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Tilt release:</strong> the vat tilts to separate the cured layer from the release film, which Elegoo uses to reduce peel forces and speed up printing. Elegoo quotes up to 150 mm/h, with fast and slow modes.</li>
<li><strong>COB light source:</strong> 144 LEDs at 405 nm through a Fresnel collimating lens.</li>
<li><strong>AI camera:</strong> detects an empty build plate and warping, records time-lapses and lets you watch the print.</li>
<li><strong>Self-check and safety:</strong> one-click self-check, power-loss resume and an LED over-temperature alarm.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Setting up the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra</h2>
<p>The printer ships assembled. At a high level:</p>
<ol>
<li>Remove the packing material and check that the vat and build plate are seated.</li>
<li>Power on and run the self-check.</li>
<li>Let the printer level itself – there is no paper levelling.</li>
<li>Install CHITUBOX from the included USB drive, choose &quot;ELEGOO Saturn 4 Ultra&quot; as the printer and slice a test model.</li>
<li>Send the file over Wi-Fi or copy it to a USB drive and start the print.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> check STL models for holes and thin walls before slicing. Our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> opens them in your browser.</p>
<h2>Connecting the Saturn 4 Ultra</h2>
<p>Files go to the printer by USB drive or over Wi-Fi. Elegoo also supports Wi-Fi cluster printing, so you can control several printers from one computer.</p>
<h2>Saturn 4 Ultra manual</h2>
<p>Elegoo publishes the user manual on the ELEGOO Wiki under Saturn → Saturn 4 Ultra, and a printed copy comes in the box.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Firmware updates arrive over Wi-Fi (OTA). Slice in CHITUBOX; the 16K model also supports Elegoo SatelLite. If your model is an OBJ or 3MF file, convert it with our <a href="/obj-to-stl/">OBJ to STL converter</a> first if your slicer needs STL.</p>
