Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra – resin printer specs, setup and 16K version

Updated November 24, 2026

Key points
  • The Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra is a 10-inch 12K mono LCD resin printer with a 218.88 × 122.88 × 220 mm build volume.
  • Elegoo announced it on 15 April 2024; the US launch price was US$399.
  • It tilts the vat to release each layer, levels itself automatically and has a built-in AI camera.

The Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra is a resin (MSLA) 3D printer with a 10-inch 12K mono LCD, a 218.88 × 122.88 × 220 mm build volume and a tilting vat that peels each layer off the film. Elegoo announced it on 15 April 2024 at a US launch price of US$399. It suits miniature, jewellery and figure makers who want fine detail with little manual setup.

Who the Saturn 4 Ultra suits

The Saturn 4 Ultra suits makers who print miniatures, tabletop terrain, dental-style models or small detailed parts. Resin printing gives much finer surface detail than filament printers but needs gloves, ventilation and post-processing (washing and curing). If you want large functional parts in PLA or PETG, an FDM printer such as the Elegoo Centauri Carbon is the better fit.

Key features

  • Tilt release: the vat tilts to separate the cured layer from the release film, which Elegoo uses to reduce peel forces and speed up printing. Elegoo quotes up to 150 mm/h, with fast and slow modes.
  • COB light source: 144 LEDs at 405 nm through a Fresnel collimating lens.
  • AI camera: detects an empty build plate and warping, records time-lapses and lets you watch the print.
  • Self-check and safety: one-click self-check, power-loss resume and an LED over-temperature alarm.

The 16K also adds a chamber light for the camera, dual-band Wi-Fi and a sensor that warns about residue, low resin and levelling problems.

Setting up the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra

The printer ships assembled. At a high level:

  1. Remove the packing material and check that the vat and build plate are seated.
  2. Power on and run the self-check.
  3. Let the printer level itself – there is no paper levelling.
  4. Install CHITUBOX from the included USB drive, choose "ELEGOO Saturn 4 Ultra" as the printer and slice a test model.
  5. Send the file over Wi-Fi or copy it to a USB drive and start the print.

Tip: check STL models for holes and thin walls before slicing. Our STL viewer opens them in your browser.

Connecting the Saturn 4 Ultra

Files go to the printer by USB drive or over Wi-Fi. Elegoo also supports Wi-Fi cluster printing, so you can control several printers from one computer.

Saturn 4 Ultra manual

Elegoo publishes the user manual on the ELEGOO Wiki under Saturn → Saturn 4 Ultra, and a printed copy comes in the box.

Firmware and software

Firmware updates arrive over Wi-Fi (OTA). Slice in CHITUBOX; the 16K model also supports Elegoo SatelLite. If your model is an OBJ or 3MF file, convert it with our OBJ to STL converter first if your slicer needs STL.

Questions and answers

What is the build volume of the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra?

218.88 × 122.88 × 220 mm for the 12K model. The Saturn 4 Ultra 16K is slightly smaller at 211.68 × 118.37 × 220 mm.

Does the Elegoo Saturn 4 Ultra need levelling?

No manual levelling. It uses a mechanical sensor for one-click automatic levelling and runs a self-check.

Does the Saturn 4 Ultra have a heated vat?

Elegoo lists no vat heater for the 12K model, only a cooling system. The Saturn 4 Ultra 16K adds smart vat heating that keeps resin at 30 °C.

What slicer does the Saturn 4 Ultra use?

CHITUBOX, which Elegoo lists in the spec table and includes on the USB drive. The 16K version also works with Elegoo's own SatelLite slicer.
