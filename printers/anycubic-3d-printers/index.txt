<h2>Kobra 4 and Kobra X: entry multi-colour</h2>
<p>The <strong>Kobra 4</strong> has a 260 mm cube build volume, a 300 °C nozzle and up to 600 mm/s, with an ACE 2 Pro feeder for four colours. The <strong>Kobra X</strong>, first shown at Formnext 2025, keeps the same build volume but moves the filament changer into the print head (Anycubic calls it ACE Gen 2), which Anycubic designed to cut waste. See our <a href="/printers/anycubic-kobra-x/">Anycubic Kobra X</a> page for full specs.</p>
<h2>Kobra S1 and S1 Max: enclosed CoreXY</h2>
<p>The <strong>Kobra S1 ACE 2 Pro Combo</strong> is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 250 mm cube build volume, a 320 °C nozzle, a 120 °C bed, 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s². Its ACE 2 Pro dries filament at up to 65 °C. The <strong>Kobra S1 Max</strong> scales up to 350 × 350 × 350 mm, with a 350 °C hot end and active chamber heating to 65 °C for engineering filaments. Our <a href="/printers/anycubic-kobra-s1-combo/">Kobra S1 Combo</a> page covers the original S1.</p>
<h2>Kobra 3 Max V2: large format</h2>
<p>The <strong>Kobra 3 Max V2</strong> has the biggest FDM build volume in the range at 420 × 420 × 500 mm, with up to 600 mm/s and 10,000 mm/s² and an ACE 2 Pro for four to eight colours. It suits cosplay helmets, large props and long functional parts.</p>
