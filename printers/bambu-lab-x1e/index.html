Bambu Lab X1E – specs, security features and how it differs from the X1C

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Bambu Lab X1E is an enterprise version of the X1 Carbon with active chamber heating up to 60 °C, a 320 °C hotend and a 256 × 256 × 256 mm build volume.
  • It adds wired Ethernet, WPA2-Enterprise and 802.1X network security, physical kill switches for Wi-Fi and Ethernet, and a LAN-only mode.
  • Bambu Lab announced it in October 2023 with a launch price of US$2,499, sold through resellers.

The Bambu Lab X1E is an enterprise version of the X1 Carbon with a heated chamber up to 60 °C, a 320 °C hotend, wired Ethernet and network security features for offices and engineering teams. It keeps the X1 Carbon's 256 × 256 × 256 mm CoreXY platform and AMS support. Bambu Lab announced it in October 2023 at a launch price of US$2,499, sold through resellers.

Why it matters: a heated chamber reduces warping and layer splitting on ABS, ASA, PA and PPS parts, and the network features let IT teams put the printer on a managed corporate network. Bambu Lab's wiki says an X1 Carbon cannot be converted into an X1E.

Who the X1E suits

The X1E is aimed at professional and engineering users who print functional parts in high-temperature materials and must meet company network rules. For home use, the X1 Carbon or the newer H2D usually make more sense.

Setting up the X1E

The X1E follows the X1 unboxing: remove the packaging and transport fixings, fit the AMS if you have one, power on and follow the on-screen calibration. During this first setup you connect either by Ethernet or by direct Wi-Fi with your Bambu account. Enterprise Wi-Fi is added afterwards by loading certificate files from the setup guide.

Connecting and network security

  • Ethernet takes priority when both wired and wireless are connected; custom IP and DNS are supported.
  • WPA2-Enterprise supports EAP-PEAP, EAP-TLS and EAP-TTLS, with CA and client certificates.
  • Kill switches physically turn off Wi-Fi and Ethernet, and the network module can be removed.
  • LAN-only mode keeps print jobs off Bambu Cloud; firmware updates still need internet access.

Bambu Lab X1E manual and firmware

Bambu Lab publishes the X1E documentation on the Bambu Lab Wiki under the X1 series: an X1E FAQ, the X1E network connection guide, unboxing steps and the firmware release history. Firmware updates are installed from the touchscreen, Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy when the printer is online.

Software

Slice in Bambu Studio on Windows or macOS; chamber temperature can be set from the touchscreen, Bambu Studio or Bambu Handy. Other G-code slicers work, but some features may not. Check exported files with our 3MF to STL converter or G-code to STL tool.

Questions and answers

What is the difference between the Bambu Lab X1E and X1 Carbon?

The X1E adds an active chamber heater (up to 60 °C), a 320 °C hotend, a wired network module with Ethernet, enterprise Wi-Fi security, physical network kill switches and a HEPA plus carbon filter. Bambu Lab says an X1 Carbon cannot be upgraded to an X1E.

Does the Bambu Lab X1E work without the cloud?

Yes. It has a LAN-only mode that works without Bambu Cloud, although firmware updates still need an internet connection.

How much did the Bambu Lab X1E cost at launch?

US$2,499 at its October 2023 launch, sold through Bambu Lab's resellers.

What filaments can the X1E print?

Everything the X1 Carbon prints plus high-temperature engineering grades such as PPA-CF, PPS and PPS-CF, helped by the 320 °C hotend and the heated chamber.
