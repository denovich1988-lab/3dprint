<h2>The 12.8-inch 6K screen</h2>
<p>The mono LCD measures 12.8 inches and has 5448 × 3064 pixels, so each pixel is 51 µm. That is coarser than small 8K and 12K printers, but the large area lets you print a whole set of miniatures, a cosplay helmet or a tall bust in one job. Layer heights run from 0.01 to 0.2 mm.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> large resin parts need hollowing and drain holes. Check wall thickness in our <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> before slicing.</p>
<h2>Build and features</h2>
<p>The Jupiter has an all-metal body, a ball-screw Z axis with four sliders, a 5-inch capacitive touchscreen and an LED light strip. Elegoo also built in an automatic resin-feeding inlet and a small activated-carbon air purifier. The build plate measures 286 × 166 mm.</p>
<h2>Who the Elegoo Jupiter suits</h2>
<p>It suits resin users who print large parts or big batches, such as tabletop terrain, dental models or props. At about 40 kg it needs a sturdy bench and good ventilation. Users who print small miniatures get finer detail from smaller high-resolution printers such as the <a href="/printers/elegoo-mars-5-ultra/">Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra</a>.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Jupiter</h2>
<p>At a high level, following Elegoo&#39;s user manual:</p>
<ol>
<li>Place the printer on a level, stable bench away from sunlight.</li>
<li>Fit the build plate and level it against the screen.</li>
<li>Install the resin vat and fill it (or connect the resin feeder).</li>
<li>Slice a test file, copy it to a USB drive and start the print.</li>
<li>Wash and cure the finished parts.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Slicer, firmware and manual</h2>
<p>Elegoo bundled <strong>Chitubox</strong> with a one-year Chitubox Pro licence. Elegoo&#39;s Jupiter support-files page lists the firmware and the English user manual, and the Download Center on elegoo.com lists the Jupiter, Jupiter SE and Jupiter 2. Files are copied to the printer on a USB drive.</p>
<h2>Jupiter SE: what changed</h2>
<p>The Jupiter SE keeps the 12.8-inch 6K LCD and almost the same build volume (277.848 × 156.264 × 300 mm). It weighs 19.5 kg instead of about 40 kg, uses a 3.5-inch resistive touchscreen and four-point levelling, and turns the resin feeder and air purifier into USB add-ons. Elegoo lists Chitubox and Voxeldance as slicers. Elegoo&#39;s store now shows the SE as sold out too, and the newer 16K Jupiter 2 has taken over the large-format slot.</p>
