Elegoo Jupiter – 12.8-inch 6K resin printer specs and Jupiter SE

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Elegoo Jupiter is a large MSLA resin printer with a 12.8-inch 6K mono LCD and a 277.848 × 156.06 × 300 mm build volume.
  • It launched on Kickstarter in September 2021 with a US$1,300 MSRP; Elegoo's store now shows it as sold out.
  • The later Jupiter SE keeps the same screen and build volume in a lighter body with a smaller touchscreen.

The Elegoo Jupiter is a large MSLA resin 3D printer with a 12.8-inch 6K mono LCD and a 277.848 × 156.06 × 300 mm build volume. Elegoo launched it on Kickstarter on 11 September 2021 with a US$1,300 MSRP (US), and backers' units started shipping in April 2022. The original model is now sold out on Elegoo's store, so this page also covers the Jupiter SE.

The 12.8-inch 6K screen

The mono LCD measures 12.8 inches and has 5448 × 3064 pixels, so each pixel is 51 µm. That is coarser than small 8K and 12K printers, but the large area lets you print a whole set of miniatures, a cosplay helmet or a tall bust in one job. Layer heights run from 0.01 to 0.2 mm.

Tip: large resin parts need hollowing and drain holes. Check wall thickness in our STL viewer before slicing.

Build and features

The Jupiter has an all-metal body, a ball-screw Z axis with four sliders, a 5-inch capacitive touchscreen and an LED light strip. Elegoo also built in an automatic resin-feeding inlet and a small activated-carbon air purifier. The build plate measures 286 × 166 mm.

Who the Elegoo Jupiter suits

It suits resin users who print large parts or big batches, such as tabletop terrain, dental models or props. At about 40 kg it needs a sturdy bench and good ventilation. Users who print small miniatures get finer detail from smaller high-resolution printers such as the Elegoo Mars 5 Ultra.

Setting up the Jupiter

At a high level, following Elegoo's user manual:

  1. Place the printer on a level, stable bench away from sunlight.
  2. Fit the build plate and level it against the screen.
  3. Install the resin vat and fill it (or connect the resin feeder).
  4. Slice a test file, copy it to a USB drive and start the print.
  5. Wash and cure the finished parts.

Slicer, firmware and manual

Elegoo bundled Chitubox with a one-year Chitubox Pro licence. Elegoo's Jupiter support-files page lists the firmware and the English user manual, and the Download Center on elegoo.com lists the Jupiter, Jupiter SE and Jupiter 2. Files are copied to the printer on a USB drive.

Jupiter SE: what changed

The Jupiter SE keeps the 12.8-inch 6K LCD and almost the same build volume (277.848 × 156.264 × 300 mm). It weighs 19.5 kg instead of about 40 kg, uses a 3.5-inch resistive touchscreen and four-point levelling, and turns the resin feeder and air purifier into USB add-ons. Elegoo lists Chitubox and Voxeldance as slicers. Elegoo's store now shows the SE as sold out too, and the newer 16K Jupiter 2 has taken over the large-format slot.

Questions and answers

What is the Elegoo Jupiter build volume?

277.848 × 156.06 × 300 mm, according to Elegoo's release announcement. That is one of the largest volumes among consumer MSLA printers of its time.

What resolution is the Elegoo Jupiter screen?

The 12.8-inch mono LCD is 5448 × 3064 pixels (6K), which gives a 51 µm XY pixel size.

Is the Elegoo Jupiter discontinued?

Elegoo's US and UK store pages show the original Jupiter as sold out. Elegoo has since released the Jupiter SE and the 16K Jupiter 2.

What slicer does the Elegoo Jupiter use?

Chitubox. Elegoo included a one-year Chitubox Pro licence with the printer.
