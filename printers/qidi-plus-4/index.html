QIDI Plus 4 – specs, 65 °C heated chamber and setup

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The QIDI Plus 4 is an enclosed CoreXY printer with a 305 × 305 × 280 mm build volume and independent chamber heating up to 65 °C.
  • Its 370 °C bimetal nozzle and 120 °C bed are made for engineering filaments such as ABS, ASA, PC, nylon and fibre-filled grades.
  • QIDI now lists the Plus 4 as discontinued, with the QIDI Plus5 as its successor.

The QIDI Plus 4 is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 305 × 305 × 280 mm build volume, a 370 °C nozzle and independent chamber heating up to 65 °C, built for engineering filaments. QIDI launched it in September 2024 at US$799 (launch price, US, as reported by All3DP and Gizmochina). QIDI now marks the Plus 4 as discontinued, with the Plus5 as its successor, so this page is most useful if you own one or are buying second-hand.

Who the QIDI Plus 4 suits

The Plus 4 suits makers who print functional parts in ABS, ASA, PC, nylon or carbon-fibre filament and want a large enclosed volume. For PLA-only printing a smaller open printer is cheaper and simpler. If you want the same idea in a smaller box, see the QIDI Q2.

Heated chamber and filtration

The chamber heater is separate from the bed: a 400 W PTC heater with a circulation fan holds the chamber at up to 65 °C. A warm chamber reduces warping and layer splitting in ABS, ASA and nylon. Air circulates through an activated carbon filter.

Tip: keep the door closed and let the chamber warm up before printing ABS or PC, and open it for PLA so the filament does not soften in the hot end.

QIDI Box multicolour

QIDI lists the Plus 4 as compatible with the QIDI Box, an automatic filament changer. Each box holds four spools, and QIDI says up to four boxes can be chained for 16 materials. Without a box, the Plus 4 prints one filament at a time.

Setting up the QIDI Plus 4

QIDI ships the printer mostly assembled and publishes an unboxing guide on the QIDI Wiki. In outline:

  1. Remove the packing foam and the shipping screws that lock the motion system, as the unboxing guide shows.
  2. Fit the filament holder, the door handle and the screen if they are packed separately.
  3. Power on and follow the on-screen setup. Levelling is automatic.
  4. Load filament and print a test model from the internal storage or a USB drive.

Connecting the QIDI Plus 4

The Plus 4 connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet, and also prints from a USB drive. Send jobs from QIDI Studio on your network. QIDI's app offers remote monitoring, with cloud or LAN-only access. A camera of up to 1080p records time-lapses.

QIDI Plus 4 manual

QIDI publishes the Plus 4 manual on the QIDI Wiki under X-Plus4 → Manual, including the unboxing steps and maintenance pages. There is no printed handbook to rely on; check the wiki for the current version.

Firmware and software

The Plus 4 runs Klipper-based firmware. QIDI Studio is the official slicer and comes with Plus 4 profiles; it opens STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP files. OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer also work. Questions and answers

What is the QIDI Plus 4 build volume?

QIDI lists 305 × 305 × 280 mm. That is larger than the QIDI Q2 (270 × 270 × 256 mm) and suits large enclosed prints in ABS, ASA or nylon.

How hot does the QIDI Plus 4 chamber get?

QIDI specifies independent chamber heating up to 65 °C, from a 400 W PTC heater. The nozzle reaches 370 °C and the bed 120 °C.

Does the QIDI Plus 4 support multicolour printing?

Yes, with the QIDI Box. QIDI says up to four boxes can be chained for 16 materials.

What firmware and slicer does the QIDI Plus 4 use?

It runs Klipper-based firmware and slices in QIDI Studio. QIDI also names third-party slicers such as OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer.
