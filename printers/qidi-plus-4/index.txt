<h2>Who the QIDI Plus 4 suits</h2>
<p>The Plus 4 suits makers who print functional parts in ABS, ASA, PC, nylon or carbon-fibre filament and want a large enclosed volume. For PLA-only printing a smaller open printer is cheaper and simpler. If you want the same idea in a smaller box, see the <a href="/printers/qidi-q2/">QIDI Q2</a>.</p>
<h2>Heated chamber and filtration</h2>
<p>The chamber heater is separate from the bed: a 400 W PTC heater with a circulation fan holds the chamber at up to 65 °C. A warm chamber reduces warping and layer splitting in ABS, ASA and nylon. Air circulates through an activated carbon filter.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> keep the door closed and let the chamber warm up before printing ABS or PC, and open it for PLA so the filament does not soften in the hot end.</p>
<h2>QIDI Box multicolour</h2>
<p>QIDI lists the Plus 4 as compatible with the QIDI Box, an automatic filament changer. Each box holds four spools, and QIDI says up to four boxes can be chained for 16 materials. Without a box, the Plus 4 prints one filament at a time.</p>
<h2>Setting up the QIDI Plus 4</h2>
<p>QIDI ships the printer mostly assembled and publishes an unboxing guide on the QIDI Wiki. In outline:</p>
<ol>
<li>Remove the packing foam and the shipping screws that lock the motion system, as the unboxing guide shows.</li>
<li>Fit the filament holder, the door handle and the screen if they are packed separately.</li>
<li>Power on and follow the on-screen setup. Levelling is automatic.</li>
<li>Load filament and print a test model from the internal storage or a USB drive.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the QIDI Plus 4</h2>
<p>The Plus 4 connects over 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi or Ethernet, and also prints from a USB drive. Send jobs from QIDI Studio on your network. QIDI&#39;s app offers remote monitoring, with cloud or LAN-only access. A camera of up to 1080p records time-lapses.</p>
<h2>QIDI Plus 4 manual</h2>
<p>QIDI publishes the Plus 4 manual on the QIDI Wiki under X-Plus4 → Manual, including the unboxing steps and maintenance pages. There is no printed handbook to rely on; check the wiki for the current version.</p>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>The Plus 4 runs Klipper-based firmware. QIDI Studio is the official slicer and comes with Plus 4 profiles; it opens STL, OBJ, 3MF and STEP files. OrcaSlicer and PrusaSlicer also work. If a model arrives as a 3MF project from another slicer, our <a href="/3mf-to-stl/">3MF to STL converter</a> extracts the mesh, and the <a href="/stl-viewer/">STL viewer</a> checks it before slicing.</p>
