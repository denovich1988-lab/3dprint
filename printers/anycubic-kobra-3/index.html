Anycubic Kobra 3 – Combo specs, ACE Pro multi-colour and setup

Updated November 25, 2026

Key points
  • The Anycubic Kobra 3 is a bed-slinger with a 250 × 250 × 260 mm build volume, 600 mm/s top speed and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration.
  • The Kobra 3 Combo adds the ACE Pro, a four-spool feeder and dryer for multi-colour printing, expandable to eight colours with a second unit.
  • Anycubic opened orders in May 2024 with a US$549 RRP for the Combo; the store now lists the original Kobra 3 as end-of-life and sells the Kobra 3 V2.

The Anycubic Kobra 3 is an open-frame bed-slinger 3D printer with a 250 × 250 × 260 mm build volume and 600 mm/s top speed, best known as the Kobra 3 Combo with Anycubic's ACE Pro four-colour filament unit. Anycubic opened orders in May 2024 with a launch RRP of US$549 for the Combo (US). The store now lists the original Kobra 3 as end-of-life and sells the updated Kobra 3 V2, so this page also covers what changed.

The ACE Pro filament unit

The ACE Pro holds four spools and feeds them to the print head for multi-colour or multi-material printing. It also dries filament: two PTC heaters raise the box to up to 55 °C and Anycubic says it keeps filament dry for up to 24 hours while printing. RFID-tagged Anycubic spools are recognised automatically.

Tip: drying matters most for PETG and TPU – see our filament dryer guide for typical temperatures.

Who the Kobra 3 suits

The Kobra 3 Combo suits hobbyists who want affordable four-colour printing on an open bed-slinger. It is less suited to ABS or ASA – Anycubic's manual lists them as possible but not recommended – because there is no enclosure.

Setting up the Kobra 3

Following Anycubic's manual, at a high level:

  1. Unpack and assemble the gantry onto the base.
  2. Connect the cables and place the ACE Pro, linking it with the feed tubes.
  3. Power on and let the printer run its self-check.
  4. Run LeviQ 3.0 automatic levelling.
  5. Load filament into the ACE Pro and start the first test print.

Connecting the Kobra 3

The Kobra 3 joins Wi-Fi and works with the Anycubic App and Anycubic Cloud for remote control, monitoring and time-lapse (AI monitoring needs the optional camera). You can also print from a USB drive. Check a sliced file with our G-code to STL tool.

Kobra 3 manual and firmware

Anycubic publishes the Kobra 3 Combo quick start guide, user manual and firmware notes on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D printers. The printer runs Kobra OS; check the wiki for the current firmware version and the update steps before installing.

Slicer

Anycubic recommends Anycubic Slicer Next, based on OrcaSlicer, with LAN mode. Cura and OrcaSlicer also work. Open other slicers' projects with our 3MF to STL converter.

Kobra 3 V2: what changed

The Kobra 3 V2 grows the build volume to 255 × 255 × 260 mm, keeps the 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² figures, adds a 720p camera as standard, zoned levelling and better nozzle sealing.

Questions and answers

What is the Anycubic Kobra 3 build volume?

250 × 250 × 260 mm according to Anycubic. The Kobra 3 V2 is slightly larger at 255 × 255 × 260 mm.

What is the difference between the Kobra 3 and the Kobra 3 Combo?

The Combo bundles the printer with the ACE Pro, a four-spool filament feeder that also dries filament up to 55 °C, so it can print up to four colours or materials.

Is the Anycubic Kobra 3 discontinued?

Anycubic's store files the original Kobra 3 under end-of-life products. The Kobra 3 Combo and the updated Kobra 3 V2 are still listed.

What slicer does the Kobra 3 use?

Anycubic Slicer Next, which is based on OrcaSlicer and supports LAN printing. Cura and OrcaSlicer also work.
