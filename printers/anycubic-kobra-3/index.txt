<h2>The ACE Pro filament unit</h2>
<p>The ACE Pro holds four spools and feeds them to the print head for multi-colour or multi-material printing. It also dries filament: two PTC heaters raise the box to up to 55 °C and Anycubic says it keeps filament dry for up to 24 hours while printing. RFID-tagged Anycubic spools are recognised automatically.</p>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> drying matters most for PETG and TPU – see our <a href="/blog/filament-dryer/">filament dryer guide</a> for typical temperatures.</p>
<h2>Who the Kobra 3 suits</h2>
<p>The Kobra 3 Combo suits hobbyists who want affordable four-colour printing on an open bed-slinger. It is less suited to ABS or ASA – Anycubic&#39;s manual lists them as possible but not recommended – because there is no enclosure.</p>
<h2>Setting up the Kobra 3</h2>
<p>Following Anycubic&#39;s manual, at a high level:</p>
<ol>
<li>Unpack and assemble the gantry onto the base.</li>
<li>Connect the cables and place the ACE Pro, linking it with the feed tubes.</li>
<li>Power on and let the printer run its self-check.</li>
<li>Run LeviQ 3.0 automatic levelling.</li>
<li>Load filament into the ACE Pro and start the first test print.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Connecting the Kobra 3</h2>
<p>The Kobra 3 joins <strong>Wi-Fi</strong> and works with the <strong>Anycubic App</strong> and Anycubic Cloud for remote control, monitoring and time-lapse (AI monitoring needs the optional camera). You can also print from a <strong>USB drive</strong>. Check a sliced file with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
<h2>Kobra 3 manual and firmware</h2>
<p>Anycubic publishes the Kobra 3 Combo quick start guide, user manual and firmware notes on the Anycubic Wiki under FDM 3D printers. The printer runs Kobra OS; check the wiki for the current firmware version and the update steps before installing.</p>
<h2>Slicer</h2>
<p>Anycubic recommends <strong>Anycubic Slicer Next</strong>, based on OrcaSlicer, with LAN mode. Cura and OrcaSlicer also work. Open other slicers&#39; projects with our <a href="/3mf-to-stl/">3MF to STL converter</a>.</p>
<h2>Kobra 3 V2: what changed</h2>
<p>The Kobra 3 V2 grows the build volume to 255 × 255 × 260 mm, keeps the 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² figures, adds a 720p camera as standard, zoned levelling and better nozzle sealing.</p>
