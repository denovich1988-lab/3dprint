<h2>Quick start: from the box to the first print</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Unbox:</strong> take out the accessory box and lift the AMS out through the top.</li>
<li><strong>Remove transport locks</strong> with the supplied Allen key – the AMS and toolhead locks, packing foam and cardboard, the screen screw and the heatbed screws. Leave the foam under the bed until calibration is done, as the guide says.</li>
<li><strong>Install</strong> the screen, the AMS (with its Bambu Bus cables and PTFE tubes) and, if you need it, the external spool holder.</li>
<li><strong>Power on</strong>, load at least one spool into the AMS and follow the on-screen initial calibration.</li>
<li><strong>First print:</strong> apply a thin layer of glue to the plate and print one of the pre-loaded models from internal storage. Bambu Lab suggests Bambu PLA Basic.</li>
</ul>

<p>When printing ABS or ASA, Bambu Lab also asks for extra cleaning of the rods, the camera lens and the Micro Lidar window.</p>
<h2>Common problems and fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>PLA lifting from the plate</strong> – apply a thin coat of glue and turn off cooling for the first three layers.</li>
<li><strong>PETG lifting</strong> – use glue, turn off cooling for more of the first layers and keep part cooling at 70% or less.</li>
<li><strong>Purge waste jams behind the printer</strong> – clear the space at the back and lower the purge speed.</li>
<li><strong>Ringing on outer walls</strong> – re-tension the belts, rerun vibration compensation and lower acceleration.</li>
<li><strong>Clogged nozzle</strong> – heat and push filament through, then use the unclogging pin, then a hot hex-key pull; replace the nozzle as a last step.</li>
<li><strong>HMS error codes</strong> – look up the code on the wiki; each has its own fix page.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Online, the X1C checks for updates and shows a pop-up; follow the on-screen steps. Offline, install from a FAT32 microSD card – but only if the printer already runs firmware 01.08.02.00 or later. Do not unzip or rename the package, and do not switch off during the update.</p>
