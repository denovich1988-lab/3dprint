<p>The K1 user manual is organised in these parts:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>About the device and specifications</strong> – parts of the printer and the spec table.</li>
<li><strong>Packing list and unboxing</strong> – what is in the box and the transport screws to remove.</li>
<li><strong>Installing the product</strong> – screen, top cover, material barrel and filament.</li>
<li><strong>Power-on guide</strong> – network, time zone and Creality Cloud binding.</li>
<li><strong>User interface</strong> – files, tune and support menus.</li>
<li><strong>First printing and LAN printing</strong> – printing from a USB drive or from Creality&#39;s slicer over the network.</li>
<li><strong>Functional specification</strong> – what the menu functions do.</li>
<li><strong>Tips and routine maintenance</strong> – lubrication, cleaning and build plate care.</li>
<li><strong>Precautions for printing.</strong></li>
</ol>
<p>The specification table lists a 220 × 220 × 250 mm build volume, a 300 °C nozzle, a 100 °C bed and 350 W rated power. Creality&#39;s product page adds up to 600 mm/s and 20,000 mm/s² acceleration.</p>
<h2>Creality K1 quick start</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Unbox:</strong> lift the printer out and remove the three transport screws at the positions shown in the manual. The touchscreen is packed in the toolbox.</li>
<li><strong>Fit the screen:</strong> connect the flat cable from the base and snap the screen into its slot the right way round. Do not power on while connecting it.</li>
<li><strong>Install the top cover and the material barrel</strong> (spool holder).</li>
<li><strong>Load filament:</strong> push it into the deepest part of the PTFE tube.</li>
<li><strong>Set the voltage:</strong> 115 V for 100–120 V mains or 230 V (the default) for 200–240 V.</li>
<li><strong>Power on:</strong> follow the guide to join your network, set the time zone and bind the printer in Creality Cloud.</li>
<li><strong>First print:</strong> insert the USB drive and choose a model, or install Creality Print, add the printer by scanning or IP address and send the file over LAN.</li>
</ol>

<h2>Common K1 problems and Creality&#39;s fixes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Poor first-layer adhesion:</strong> recalibrate the platform, clean or replace the plate and use glue stick.</li>
<li><strong>Filament stuck on the nozzle:</strong> remove it with the supplied tools while the hotend is still warm – never touch the hotend.</li>
<li><strong>Error codes on screen:</strong> look the code up in the manual&#39;s error table, then use Creality&#39;s after-sales tutorials for your model.</li>
<li><strong>Online (OTA) update not offered:</strong> the Creality Wiki has a troubleshooting page for this; update from a USB drive instead.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Creality offers three update routes for the K1: over the air when the printer is online, through the Creality Cloud app, or from a USB drive with firmware downloaded from Creality&#39;s K1 download page. Printers on old firmware must step through intermediate versions by USB first. The printer restarts several times during an update; run the self-tests before printing again. Newer firmware asks you to accept a privacy agreement before network features work. Slice in <strong>Creality Print</strong> and preview the G-code with our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL</a> tool.</p>
