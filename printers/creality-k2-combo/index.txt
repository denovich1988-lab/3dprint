2b:Td3e,<p><strong>The Creality K2 Combo is an enclosed CoreXY 3D printer with a 260 × 260 × 260 mm build volume, bundled with one CFS (Creality Filament System) for four-colour printing.</strong> Creality launched the K2 and K2 Combo on 25 August 2025, next to the K2 Pro. The Combo cost US$699 at launch and the printer alone US$549.</p>
<h2>Who the Creality K2 Combo suits</h2>
<p>The K2 Combo suits makers who want an enclosed, fast multi-colour printer with a bed a little larger than the common 256 mm. It handles PLA, PETG, PET, ABS and PLA-CF. Buyers who need a heated chamber or nylon-based engineering filaments should look at the K2 Pro or K2 Plus.</p>

<h2>The CFS multi-colour system</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Four spools</strong> per CFS in a sealed box, read by RFID.</li>
<li><strong>Auto refill</strong> and tangle detection.</li>
<li><strong>Up to four units</strong> for 16 colours.</li>
<li><strong>Not a dryer:</strong> the original CFS stores filament; the CFS 2 adds drying. A review by Notebookcheck noted that the CFS does not feed TPU.</li>
</ul>
<h2>First setup</h2>
<p>From Creality&#39;s K2 user manual:</p>
<ol>
<li>Remove the four bed locking screws with yellow labels.</li>
<li>Route the screen&#39;s ribbon cable through the top slot, connect it and slide the screen into place.</li>
<li>Connect the CFS.</li>
<li>Switch on and follow the start-up guide: language, network and time zone.</li>
<li>Wait for the automatic self-check, about 13 minutes, without touching the printer.</li>
<li>Load filament, set its details and start a print.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> after any firmware update, run input shaping and auto levelling again, as Creality advises.</p>
<h2>Connecting the K2</h2>
<p>The K2 joins 2.4 GHz Wi-Fi and links to the Creality Cloud app for remote printing and monitoring. Creality Print can send jobs over the local network; the printer&#39;s IP address is under Settings → Network. You can also print from a USB drive. The base K2 has no Ethernet port.</p>
<h2>Manual, firmware and slicer</h2>
<p>The user manual is on the Creality Wiki under K2 Flagship Series → K2, and firmware is also in Creality&#39;s Download Center. Update from the screen under Settings → Version Checking, or from a USB drive. The CFS updates through the printer; Creality advises connecting only one CFS during an online update. Slice in Creality Print, and preview projects in our <a href="/3mf-viewer/">3MF viewer</a>.</p>
