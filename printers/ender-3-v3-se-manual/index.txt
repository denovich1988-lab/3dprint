<h2>Quick-start summary</h2>
<p><strong>Unboxing.</strong> The box holds the base, the gantry frame, the display, the material rack and a kit with screws, tools, a ribbon-cable clip, a filament sample, a spare nozzle and a storage card.</p>
<p><strong>Assembly.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Seat the gantry in the base slot and fix it from below with the long screws, then the short ones at the back.</li>
<li>Mount the display on the right of the base and connect it. The card slot on the screen is only for screen firmware.</li>
<li>Fix the material rack to the top of the gantry.</li>
<li>Set the voltage switch (115 V or 230 V) for your mains, connect the extruder ribbon cable with its clip and plug in the X and Z motors.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>First calibration.</strong> Clean the bed and choose <strong>Leveling</strong>. The CR Touch probes the bed, a strain sensor sets the Z offset, and the result is shown in colours from green (level) to red (strongly tilted). If the first layer is too loose or too tight, adjust Z compensation with the knob.</p>
<p><strong>First print.</strong> Use auto-feed on the screen to load filament (or cut the end at 45° and push it in by hand). Install Creality Print from the card, add the printer, slice, export to the card, then choose <strong>Print</strong> on the screen. The manual asks for short file names in Latin letters and digits in the card&#39;s root folder.</p>
<h2>Maintenance Creality recommends</h2>
<ul>
<li>Let the build plate cool, then flex it to release prints; scrape off residue and use glue stick if adhesion drops. The plate is a consumable.</li>
<li>Lubricate the lead screws and smooth rods regularly.</li>
<li>To change the nozzle, preheat, hold the heater block, remove the silicone sock and swap the nozzle.</li>
<li>Clean the printer and rails with a dry cloth with the power off.</li>
</ul>

<h2>Firmware and software</h2>
<p>Download the firmware for the Ender-3 V3 SE from Creality&#39;s website and install it from the storage card. The screen has its own firmware, loaded through its own card slot. There is no Wi-Fi, so there are no over-the-air updates. Slice in <strong>Creality Print</strong>; Cura and PrusaSlicer work with a custom profile. To check a sliced file before printing, use our <a href="/gcode-to-stl/">G-code to STL tool</a>.</p>
